# Premise

## Narrative

D dies in the modern world and wakes as a child in Terra, inside the body of Roland Arden. Curious by nature, he builds a life of adventure and creation through runecraft, craft, and trade. Helci is the center of his romantic and emotional life. When pure close combat proves beyond him, he leans into his specialization instead of trying to do everything.

## Detail

**Name:** D

**Faith:** Christian

**Background:** Medical appliance craftsman in the modern world

**Age at death:** Late twenties

**Death:** Killed by a truck

**Transmigration:** D alone. For everyone else, death is death.

**Wake:** Child body of Roland Arden, noble son, Terra

**World:** Magic and a status system

**Hobbies:** Gaming, cooking, shooting at the range, tinkering

**Nature:** Curious

**Life goal:** Adventure and creation

**Means:** Runecraft, workshop skill, trade

**Conflict:** Politics and identity, not endless poverty

**Romance:** Helci is the main focus of his romantic and emotional life

**Fighting:** Fights however he likes at first. When close-combat specialists outpace him physically, he leans into ranged kit and fundamental magic control. Golems and other workshop toys stay side projects next to that personal power.
