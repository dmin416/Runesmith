- Ch 1: D dies in a storm on Earth and wakes as Roland Arden, age 5, bastard fourth son of Baron Wentworth.
- Ch 2–3: Maps status and Caldris. Reveals Mana Sense at dinner. Standing rises; books only, no tutor.

- Ch 5: Takes Mage. Affinities all **0%**. Easy elemental path closed.
- Ch 6: Aims **Runesmith** via Mana Scribe + Blacksmith. Lustile denied. Must adventure as a mage; hide Arden smith link.
- Ch 7: Leaves estate for Carwen cabin. Secret goodbye to Lucienne. Buries stolen class crystal. Almost no coin.
- Ch 8: Registers Bronze as **Roland**. Guild card, first-name only.
- Ch 9–9.5: Forest goblin grind. Goblin Hunter title. Hilde’s inn. Coin and stone ledger toward Mage L20.
- Ch 10: Settles Carwen loop. Aims Edelgard craft city; Scribe before Blacksmith.
- Ch 11–13: Rebecca recruits him with Sahildr and Reyna. Town dungeon floors. Sees Sahildr’s **Impact** rune. Iron Flagon binge. Tames Needle Worm (**Ned**).
- Ch 14: Arden watcher ambush in the west woods. Party kills him. Detonation rapier loot.
- Ch 15: Cremates body. Diagnosis → **Lesser Detonation** schematic. Title **Runic Scholar**.
- Ch 16: Class change to **Runic Mana Scribe**. Trial: **Fire Orb** scroll.
- Ch 17: Quits party for craft path. Confesses part of bastard story. Splits loot. Promises future gear.
- Ch 18: Copies Sahildr’s **Impact** schematic. Drawing breakthrough. Train to Edelgard. Trio goodbye.
