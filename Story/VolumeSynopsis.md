# Volume Synopsis

## Volume 1 (Chapters 1–18)

D dies in a storm on Earth and wakes as Roland Arden at 5, the bastard fourth son of Baron Wentworth in Caldris. He keeps an adult mind and a status system. At the Arden estate he recovers, maps the world, reveals Mana Sense at dinner and earns better standing without a tutor. He trains body and magic for ascension, kills his first dungeon goblin at nine and takes Mage at ten. Zero elemental affinities close the easy mage path so he aims at Runesmith craft instead. Wentworth denies Lustile Academy, orders him to earn his keep as an adventurer mage and forbids tying the Arden knight name to smith work in public. Roland packs calligraphy books, says a secret goodbye to Lucienne and leaves for Carwen with a buried class crystal and almost no coin.

In Carwen he registers bronze under his first name only, grinds forest goblins into the Goblin Hunter title and rents at Hilde’s inn. Rebecca recruits him into her party with Sahildr and Reyna for the town dungeon. They clear floors together, binge at the Iron Flagon and Roland tames a Needle Worm he later names Ned. After about half a year he digs up the class crystal. An Arden watcher ambushes him in the west woods. The party kills the man. Roland confesses part of his bastard story, cremates the body, keeps the rapier and takes Runic Mana Scribe. He splits loot, quits the party for a craft path toward Edelgard, copies Sahildr’s Impact rune and leaves Carwen by train while the three girls say goodbye.
