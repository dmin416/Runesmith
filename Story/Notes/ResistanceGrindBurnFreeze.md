## Without Healing Potions

### Burn Spot

- Skin reddens, blisters, then forms a dry crust.
- The crust and blister fluid insulate the spot so further damage slows.
- Blood flow in the thigh carries heat away constantly.
- End state: a deep, crusted, coin-sized wound that hurts badly at first then goes partly numb as surface nerves die.

### Freeze Spot

- Skin goes white and numb as the top layers die.
- Becomes a small patch of frostbitten tissue that blackens and sloughs off.
- Blood flow keeps the damage shallow.

### Risks

- Death risk is very low at this size and intensity.
- Main threat is infection in the open wounds. With four men's robustness and basic cleanliness it stays local.
- Long term: numb scar tissue. Chronic burn scars rewounded for years can develop Marjolin's ulcer, a rare aggressive skin cancer, typically decades later.

### Grind Problem

Numbness kills the stimulus. Once surface nerves die the skill stalls. He must rotate to fresh skin, leaving a constellation of coin-sized scars across his body.

## With Healing Potions

He splashes a healing potion whenever the damage gets too deep.

### Why It Works

- Tissue is restored before damage reaches anything important.
- Fresh nerve endings return each cycle so sensitivity and skill gain stay high.
- Wounds close before infection can set in.
- Death risk effectively disappears.

### Additional Opportunity to Level Skills

- **Pain:** Fresh nerves mean full-intensity pain every cycle with no numb plateau. Desk starve / SP nutrient burn also credits Pain (hollow gut, headache, body ache; `Chapter21_5.md`). **Ned leg jabs** credit Pain too.
- **Ned leg jabs:** Blunt (bash) + Sharp pierce + Poison toxin + Pain per jab (`Chapter21_5.md`).
- **Sleep:** Constant exposure makes sleep nearly impossible. Sleep deprivation becomes the biggest physical threat. (Ch 21.5 already locks Sleep Resistance **+1** over the half year.)
- **Hunger:** High burn with thin or no meals is starvation → Hunger Resistance (`Chapter21_5.md` / `MultiMechanismResistance.md`).
- **Metabolism:** If potions accelerate natural healing, each splash spends protein, fat and water. He must eat heavily to avoid wasting. If potions build tissue from mana alone this cost disappears.
- **Supply:** Dozens of splashes a day may be needed. Sustainability depends on potion cost or his ability to make them.

## Story Hooks

- The burn-heal cycle mirrors real progressive overload so a system rewarding it fits naturally.
- Repeated regeneration could gradually toughen the skin at those spots as a physical counterpart to the skill level.
- Healing skill or potion tolerance could level alongside the resistances.
- Constant pain and sleep loss feed the grind as both coping mechanism and the thing wearing him down. Keep Roland rewrite voice in prose (curious, logical, capable) even when the desk habit is extreme.
