# Kitchen Craft Story Hooks

> Invent / pacing color from KitchenCraft. Not law.

## Hooks

- The first lathe is rough. Every precision tool made on it inherits the flaw until a better one is built.
- A hydraulic press fails if the seals leak. A leather supplier or a tanner becomes a plot-relevant ally.
- Steel quality varies by region. Crucible steel might be a trade secret.
- Enchantments on cookware (heat, non-stick, self-clean) are luxury goods. A plain iron skillet is a commoner item.
- Standard measures (cup, pound) may be tied to a guild or a ruling house. Calibrating a scale could be a political act.
- A mana-powered motor drains reserves. A water wheel does not. The character chooses between convenience and independence.
- Stainless / chrome plate is **not baseline** (`Materials.md` SPECIALTY). Plain steel or mythril face for rust resistance (Part 9). Do not write stainless as street cookware.
- Glass is a different trade. A thermometer or glass bowl may require a partnership with a glassblower.
