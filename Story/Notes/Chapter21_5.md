## Process / temperament

### Hunger, mana, and stamina pours

- He **cannot will himself to feel hungry**. Appetite is not a switch he can flip with Willpower.
- He **can still make himself hungry**: burn body fuel until the need is real (desk hours, burn/freeze patch, blood for ink, **stamina pours** into scrolls).
- **Mana** for desk work is not the same kind of bill. Ambient mana fills him from around him. Draining mana stones to crumbly powder for homemade magic ink is also outside his gut; the stone's stored mana is feedstock, not his dinner.
- **Runic scrolls can be filled with stamina** as well as mana (`MagicScrollMaking.md`: absorbing runes; non-mages already pay SP). He can preload / pour with SP when he wants.
- SP **refills quickly**, but each stamina pour **uses up nutrients**. Fast SP regen does not erase the food debt. One-meal days plus stamina-heavy pour stretches force heavier evening eats or he goes lightheaded.
- **Burning thousands of kcal with little or no food is starvation.** That unlocks and levels **Hunger Resistance** (`MultiMechanismResistance.md`: **1%** of that day's energy out unmet by food = **1** use; full fast day = **100**). The SP loop is a starve engine, not a dead grind.
- **Starve also feeds Pain Resistance.** Hollow gut, headache, and body ache from unmet calories are real pain doses. Meter: **15 min** of felt starve-ache under deficit = **1** Pain use. Soft-repeat after the first ~**2 h** of a continuous stretch the same day (same ache family). Burn/freeze patch stays the sharp Pain climb; hunger is the background grind.

#### SP→starve ceiling (physiology max)

Law: `References/World/Science/Body/Stamina.md`. Rune pour peg **1 SP = 10 J**. Sheet into this skip (Ch 19 exit): Str **49** / Agi **64** / End **81** → SP **1149**.

| Layer | Size | Resting half-time | Max continuous refill if kept near empty |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Burst (PCr) | **(Str+Agi)×3 = 339** SP | **15 s × (Str+Agi)/End ≈ 21 s** | **339 × ln2 / 21 ≈ 11.2 SP/s** |
| Reserve (glycolysis) | **End×10 = 810** SP | **7 min** | **810 × ln2 / 420 ≈ 1.3 SP/s** |
| **Both** | **1149** | — | **≈ 12.6 SP/s** |

That is the rate if he **always** dumps SP back into scrolls (or any SP sink) as fast as the bar refills, so stores never catch up.

| | Continuous max | Per day if 24 h | Per desk day if **14 h** awake loop |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SP through the body | **~12.6 /s** | **~1.09M SP** | **~635k SP** |
| Energy into scrolls (×10 J) | **~126 W** | **~10.9 MJ ≈ 2,600 kcal** | **~6.3 MJ ≈ 1,500 kcal** |
| Plus resting metabolism (~**100 W**) | — | **~2,100 kcal** | **~1,200 kcal** (14 h) |
| **Store pull floor (scroll loop + rest)** | — | **~4,700 kcal/day** | **~2,700 kcal** over the desk day |

Floor assumes store pull ≈ rune joules restored (energy left the body into the scroll). Aerobic overhead on PCr rebuild can push higher; gut long-term ceiling is still ~**2.5× BMR** (~**4–5k kcal/day**) from `Stamina.md`.

**What that does to hunger / mass**

| Fuel | Size (Earth basline in `Stamina.md`) | Time to empty at +**2,600 kcal/day** SP loop alone |
|---|---|---|
| Glycogen (muscle+liver) | ~**2,000 kcal** | **under a day** of hard continuous loop (hours if he also burns the reserve layer hard) |
| Body fat **10 kg** | ~**90,000 kcal** | ~**35 days** SP-loop-only; ~**19 days** if rest+loop (~**4,700 kcal/day**) both come from fat |
| Fat mass loss rate | — | ~**0.34 kg/day** at 2,600 kcal/day (÷ ~7,700 kcal/kg); ~**0.6 kg/day** at 4,700 |

He still cannot flip appetite on. Running near this ceiling **makes** hunger: glycogen empties, then fat pull + lightheaded / one-meal catch-up.

**Desk craft vs physiology max.** Ordinary FA pours (~**10–20**/day at list **50**) are only hundreds of SP/day if paid in stamina. That is **≪** the **12.6 SP/s** refill cap. To starve on purpose he must **keep** spending SP (practice pours, preloads, failed seats, deliberate SP dumps into hide patches / scrap scrolls) so the bar stays in refill, not only the few auction keepers.

**Hunger Resistance (skip lock):** Unlocks on the first real starve stretch (SP burn / one-meal / skip lunch with stores pulling). Meter: unmet % of that day's burn. Worked band for this desk: one meal (~**800 kcal**) against ~**2,700–4,700 kcal** out → ~**70–85** uses/day. Over ~**180** days ≈ **~13k–15k** uses → exit **L6** (clears **5,000**; short of **L7** at **20,000**). Zero-food hard-loop days pay **100**/day and accelerate. Vit/End **+1** per level.

**Pain from starve (skip lock):** Enter Pain **L5** (train). Starve-ache ~**8–20** clean Pain uses/day after soft-repeat (first ~**2 h** dense, then thin). Half year ≈ **~1.5k–3.6k** Hunger-side Pain uses, plus burn/freeze patch cycles. Patch + hunger together clear **L6** (**5,000** cum); **L7** (**20,000**) only if patch grind is hard every day. ## Experimentation locks

### Ratios / scale

- Component **size ratios** are extremely important for how mana is processed.
- Forcing ratio changes to make parts bigger/smaller or nudge effects works **only barely** — slight altered results, heavy inefficiency.
- Scaling the **whole** diagram smaller lowers mana **input** and power **output** together (clean enough path for quiet/cheap variants).
- Enlarging the **simple compression** segment alone to make Bolt more explosive is more likely to **implode** than to give a fiercer explosion.

### Component swaps (much more successful)

- Swapping converters / fills onto known shapes beats ratio surgery by a wide margin.
- Once he can make **Aqua Ball**, **Fireball** is almost **laughably easy**.
- Same path for **water arrow** and **light ball**.
- Lazy grafts with **unmodified ratios** still let **Diagnosis** mark what is wrong, as long as the paper does not **spontaneously burn up** (occasional).

### Freeze-flip (fails)

- Flipping the fire runic sequence upside down to freeze water does **not** work. Already in chapter prose.

### Skill feel (not a brain dump)

- As Rune Scribing / Mastery / Diagnosis rise he gets a **vague sense** of how other runes might need to change — the same feeling from messing with a **clock mechanism**.
- He does **not** get schematics dumped straight into his brain.
- Looking at runes gives **familiarity**: things will make sense the moment he sees them.

### Mana refill: empty-slow vs near-top fast

Chapter lock (Ch 23): empty→full in a **couple of hours**; **still / rest** faster; **more bare skin** faster. Curve from `Energy.md`: rate = `a × (M + b × P)`, speeds as the tank fills; `b` sets empty→full time `T`.

| | |
|---|---|
| Pool (formula) | Int **156** / Will **147** → **MP = 2148** (printed **2578** soft; spend math uses **2148**) |
| Desk clothed empty→full | **T ≈ 2.0 h** (couple of hours) → **average 17.9 MP/min** (`P / T`) |
| Near **~99%** (same body) | Peak band ≈ **21.1 MP/min** (`Energy.md` at `M ≈ 0.99 P`, `S ≈ (145/175)²`, `Rob = 1`) |
| Still + more skin | **T ≈ 1.5 h** → avg **~23.9 MP/min**; tip **~28.5 MP/min** |
| Mage / Blessed by Mana | Recovery narrative; **no** numeric mult locked |

**Why near-top wins the desk:** dumping to empty forces the **slow bottom** of the refill curve. Sipping at ~99% keeps every minute at peak ambient. Dump-cycle at clothed `T = 2 h` averages **2148 / 2 ≈ 1074 MP/h** (**~17.9 MP/min**); tip-sip beats that.

**Tip-sip spendable / 10 h** (desk clothed, continuous) ≈ **21.1 × 600 ≈ 12,650 MP**. Still + skin tip ≈ **28.5 × 600 ≈ 17,100 MP**.

### What gets cheaper (compound)

```
paid = (cast / η_cond) × (1 − 0.1 × Rune_Scribing_L)
```

Two separate levers:

1. **Rune Scribing L** → **S = 0.9, 0.8, … 0.5** at L1–L5 (−**10%** pay per rank on the same seating).
2. **η quality** → Lowest **0.20** (list **5×** cast) → Highest **1.00** (list **1×**). Learning to seat cleanly is often a **bigger** cut than one RS rank.

Also (not in the formula, but real):

- **Botch / cascade** → unbounded burn; fall off as method + class **learning-ease** land.
- **Hands pen/press** → faster seating, fewer wasted mats (skill uses, not MP formula).
- **Class learning-ease** → first-correct diagrams sooner; lost if he ever drops Runic Mana Scribe.
- **Known schematic** → redraw is seconds–minutes; first assembly was the slow part.
