## Spare mana (with Mana Hands)

### Friction (Heat family)

Heat / pressure-friction (stove kindling / train thermal family). Multitasking can overlap Hands / clicks.

- **Burn-train himself** — friction heat on skin / small patch → **Heat** (spell) + **Heat Resistance** (+ Pain as it bites).
- **Hit / scrape himself** — friction slap, drag, pinch-strike → **Blunt** / **Sharp** (abrasion) / **Pain**.
- Utility — warm tools, light a corner; tiny Fire Orb lighter scraps still count when fired.

Do not invent a named **Friction** skill unless a chapter unlocks one. Credit Heat / Heat Res / Pain / Blunt / Sharp as the dose fits.

### Suffocation

Deliberate **holds** and light **blood-volume** work (Hands drip / Ned nick already in the loop) so **Suffocation Resistance** keeps climbing past train **L6**. Same meters (`15 s` hold ≈ 1 use; ~`50 mL` ≈ 1 volume use). **No Breath Control** unlock required. Do not pass out at the desk on purpose as a grind (zero-MP style dead ends stay banned).

### Recovery

Let friction burns, scrapes, and controlled nicks **clot and close** on purpose (potion only when he must keep scribing). Each real knit cycle → **Recovery** (train left ~**2.5k** toward L6; desk / volume finish the climb). Friction and Ned tips feed this; do not skip knitting by always potion-washing every mark.
