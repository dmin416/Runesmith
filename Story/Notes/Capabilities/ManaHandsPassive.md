## How the drain works

```
μ(INT) = (INT / 15)^0.8
mana/s = (J/s) / (10 × μ(INT) × A) × R
```

- Hold rows (`ManaCast.md`): **1 kg → 1 J/s**, **20 kg → 20**, **80 kg → 78**, **500 kg → 490**.
- Hands **L9** → `R = 0.1` (10× cheaper than unskilled).
- Higher Int → more joules per mana → more mass on the same tip-sip.

One-shot **lifts** (raise mass by height) pay `m × g × h` once from the pool. They are **not** this passive line. Passive = continuous **hold**.

## What ~290 kg feels like (clothed, all day)

He can keep this up while walking, scribing, hunting setup, or idle, as long as tip-sip matches the grip and he does not also dump the pool on other casts.

| Example | Mass band | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Quill, ink pot, scroll stack, tools | grams–few kg | Trivial; many Hands free |
| Full pack / crate / small furniture | tens of kg | Easy; several Hands spare |
| Adult human (restrain / carry assist) | ~**60–80 kg** | Comfortable; still **~200+ kg** headroom |
| Two adults worth of grip | ~**150–160 kg** | Fine |
| Near the clothed ceiling | ~**290 kg** | Full tip-sip; no spare regen for other continuous drains |
| Horse-class single lift-hold | **~500 kg** table row | **Not** passive; needs pool burn or still/skin tip + less else |

Seven Hands at Multitasking **L7** let him split that budget (hide taut + pen + ink feed + carry + brace, and so on). Same-intent copies under one focus can look like more effectors without seven separate tasks (`FocusCapacity.md`).

## Refill band (why tip-sip)

Ch **23** lock: empty→full in a **couple of hours**; still / rest faster; more bare skin faster. Curve `rate = a × (M + b × P)` (`Energy.md`). Near **~99%** is the fast band.

| | Desk enter soft (`Chapter21_5`) | Mid-hunt scale |
|---|---|---|
| Pool | **2148** (formula) | **2414** |
| Clothed empty→full | **T ≈ 2.0 h** | same couple-of-hours lock |
| Clothed tip ~**99%** | **~21.1** MP/min | **~23.7** MP/min (scales with P) |
| Still + skin tip | **~28.5** MP/min | **~30–32** MP/min |

Mage card + Blessed by Mana stay **narrative** recovery; no extra numeric mult locked on this math. **Ned silk** clothes / scarf are mana conductive and raise ambient absorb vs ordinary cloth (`../../References/People/NedSilkMana.md`); soft tip headroom when he is wearing them.

Other continuous drains (Heat grill, Shield hold, scroll tip-sip pours, Ch 23 forest **omnidirectional Reinforcement pressure** + jump lifts) **compete** for the same regen. Passive Hands at ceiling means those other drains must wait or the pool falls.
