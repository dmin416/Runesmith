## What counts (clean uses)

| Skill | 1 clean use |
|---|---|
| **Pain Resistance** | Endure a real pain spike (wound sting, stick hit, hangover, grill) long enough to register. Soft repeats of the same petty sting slow after the first days. |
| **Blunt Resistance** | Take blunt trauma: stick, fist, fall, hammer, bruise through armor. |
| **Sharp Resistance** | Take cut / thrust / point trauma: blade nick, needle, claw. Leather that turns a slash into a bruise may split credit: **Sharp** for edge contact + **Blunt** for the bruise, or dominant type only. Prefer **one primary** per hit unless both are clear. |
| **Recovery** | A small wound (or bleed unit) that is **allowed to clot and close**. Knit cycle = use. Big potion-instaheal may steal Recovery credit. |

Exercise alone (pushups, runs, DOMS) pads **Running / Sprint / End** and may teach grit, but does **not** replace wound uses for Pain / Blunt / Sharp / Recovery.
