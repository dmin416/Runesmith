## What he does

### Leave Edelgard (Ch 52–55)

1. Survives the cult assault; decides dungeon cities over staying.
2. Pays Solaria High Priest **9 SG** to clear the Abyss curse.
3. Packs Southtown: traps off, illusion stash into bags, tools + anvil; leaves smelter.
4. Severance at Marlo’s mansion: **150 SG** (+50 over the **100 SG** list) + Mana Reinforcement book. Refuses capital workshop.
5. Picks **Dragnis / Albrook** (new volcanic dungeon boom). Plan: train → caravan → Luden → sail.
6. Boards train; **destroys adventurer card** so the cult cannot follow the paper trail.

### South to the port (Ch 56–65)

1. **3-day** train to a red-brick trade hub; pays as tourist; skips a new card here.
2. Learns **Basic Mana Reinforcement** from the book (lotus ambient pull) to **L2** in ~2 days; keeps grinding on the road.
3. Joins merchant caravan toward Luden (~**1 week**, 20+ wagons); rides with crates.
4. Mountain bandit ambush → rockslide → cave with collared Aredhel, Logon, Golgrim.
5. Alias **Carmine**. Cuts deep-steel slave collars; fights out along the gorge (Bocanach, Mantodea); repairs Fire Arrow paddle.
6. Night-clears bandit-held village; party splits (elves west to Bolia). **L71**.
7. Short caravan remainder (~**2 days**) into **Luden**.
8. Sells junk gear; books Albrook passage (**1 SG** + food, below deck) with 1st Mate Isabela; means to verify the ship first.

### Sail (Ch 66)

1. Hammock nights on **Sleep Resistance**.
2. Ship’s runic cannons kill a three-eyed tentacle shark-fish; he sketches the schematic.
3. Last-night party; landfall next. Edelgard and Louis left behind.

Total travel more than **2 weeks** before the island port night (Ch 67).
