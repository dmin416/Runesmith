## Prompts

### Full prompt
```
Keep the same creature, camera angle, forest floor setting and lighting. Change the design as follows.

Body: giant caterpillar about 1 m long. Keep the light green color with darker green watermelon-style banding. Change the surface from glossy to a soft matte suede-rubber finish with fine skin texture and slight folds at the segment joints. Add a faint elastic band running lengthwise along the back.

Pose: body coiled into a tight C shape like a loaded spring with the head lowered and aimed forward, ready to launch.

Head: rigid fused armored head plate. Spikes angled forward in a charging cone instead of radiating in every direction. Spikes are thick at the base and taper to needle tips with the longest ones at the center. Cream-white hardened cuticle with slightly translucent tips. Keep the small black eyes and dark mandibles.

Legs: four pairs of short thick tardigrade-style legs with black claws gripping the dirt for launch traction.

Scale: leaf litter, twigs and undergrowth at true size so the creature reads as about 1 m long.

Style: photorealistic, natural sunlight through the trees, shallow depth of field.

Avoid: hedgehog-style radial spikes, glossy plastic look, cartoon proportions.
```

### One-line add-ons
- **Skin:** Make the skin a matte soft suede-rubber finish with fine pores, subtle wrinkles at segment folds and faint visible fibers under the surface with no glossy plastic shine.
- **Legs:** Give it four pairs of short thick tardigrade-style legs with soft wrinkled skin and small black claws digging into the dirt, sized small against the huge body.
- **Spikes:** Make the spikes thick at the base and taper to needle tips, angled forward in a charging cone with the longest at the center, in cream-white hardened cuticle with fine growth ridges and slightly translucent tips, rooted in the armored head plate.
- **Pattern:** Replace the watermelon blotches with a lighter green base that fades to pale green on the belly, a thin pale dorsal line down the back, diagonal darker stripes along each side like a hawk moth caterpillar and a row of small dark oval spiracles along the flanks.
- **Segmentation:** Show 13 distinct body segments that are narrower and more numerous, separated by deep skin creases with soft folds, with each segment ringed by fine transverse wrinkles and the body tapering slightly toward the head and tail.
