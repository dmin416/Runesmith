# Silent Casting

Rewrite lock scrape for body-cast chant modes. Homes: `../../References/Runes/Magic.md` · chapter prose · `Notes.md` / `Skills.md` · `../../References/Progression/SkillsRedesign.md` (evolve park).

**True silent** = shape / draw the spell circle with **no audible chant** (including words only in the head). For a **normal magic spell**, that **never works**, no matter how hard Roland tries. Quiet floor = **near-silent whisper** (breath leaves the mouth; power down a notch).

---

## Locked details

### Tier 1 cast loop (`Magic.md`)

1. Imagine the spell circle (formula).
2. Shape the mana.
3. Chant while shaping (audible: spoken, loud, or near-silent whisper).

Fail any step and the spell sizzles. Must chant while shaping. **Shaping alone evaporates.** Intelligence helps hold and follow the circle.

### Two passwords (Ch 9 prose)

Spells need **both**:

1. An **audible chant** (the words must leave the mouth somehow).
2. **Shaping** the mana for that spell.

Controlling mana alone (Heat rub, pressure, force) is easy. Mimicking a full spell flow like Mana Bolt with no audible chant always dies at the last shaping step. **Mental-only** words fail the same as true silence.

### Chant volume dial (rewrite)

| Mode | Works? | Power / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Full spoken chant** | Yes | Normal designed power. On the move OK while chanting (mana almost guides itself). |
| **Near-silent whisper** | Yes | Quieter floor; power down a notch. Someone a few steps away may miss it. |
| **Loud shout** | Yes | Supercharges; costs more mana (~**>10%** pool on one heavy Bolt in Ch 9). |
| **True silent** (no sound / no breath of words) | **Never** for normal magic spells | Evaporates no matter how hard he tries. |
| **Mental-only chant** (words only in the head) | **Never** for normal magic spells | Same fail as true silent. |
| **Heat / utility rub** | Separate | Rubbing mana for fire / pressure is not a Bolt-circle cast. Not proof of silent spellcasting. |

### What the chant does (Ch 10 prose)

- Chant makes the spell circle **appear in the mind** and easy to trace.
- While chanting, mana almost guides itself → casts work **on the move**.
- Mangled chant **interrupts** unless he already consciously controls every part of the mana along the circle.
- Even basic T1 circles are **moderately complex**.
- Quiet floor is a near-silent whisper. True silent / mental-only never lands a normal magic spell.

### Skill / evolve park

- **Basic Incantation:** chant speed and proficiency. Does **not** unlock true silent cast.
- **SkillsRedesign.md:** evolve park → shorter / quieter (still audible) lesser casts. **Not** true silent.
- Runic **scroll** activation (page crumple, no mage chant) is a different system. Not silent body casting.

### Word scrolls vs body cast

Magic (word) scrolls must be **read aloud** to mobilize the reader's mana (`MagicScrollMaking.md`). That is scroll use, not silent body casting.

## Soft / do not mix

1. **“Silently” in narration** for body spells before Ch 20 should mean **near-silent whisper**, not true silent.
2. **Mana Hands / Heat / Sense** without spell words are not Incantation silent unlocks.
3. Body-cast “spells can form from the body alone, usually at low level” (`Magic.md`) is a separate narrative channel (accident / reflex), not trained silent Bolt spam.
