# Cold Resistance — cold night sleep

**Big lock.** Through the Edelgard volume stretch, **Cold Resistance** climbs only from **cold night sleep**, not from frost grids, freeze patches, or spare-mana cold.

## What counts

- He **slept** that night (any real sleep, including short Sleep Resistance nights).
- The lodging was **cold enough to feel** while he slept (bare windy Carwen inn, thin Crow straw room, cloak + Ned as main warmth). Heated luxury / summer-warm rooms do **not** count.
- **One clean use per cold night** (soft). Continuous overnight chill does not spam uses the same night.

## Months that make sense

Calendar soft-lock: **Edelgard arrival = early autumn** (chapter climate). Count backward and forward from that.

| Place | Cold months | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Carwen** (temperate) | **Winter + early spring** only | Bare inn + cloak + Ned. Of the **9** Carwen months, about **~100** nights credit. Mid/late spring and summer nights do **not** count. |
| **Train** | Soft **~0–3** | Cooling into the mountains; only if he actually slept. |
| **Edelgard** (mountain) | **Autumn through winter** | Crow room. Arrive early autumn → desk **4** mo + early intense-grill months still cold. About **~180** nights across the first **~6** Edelgard months. Spring / summer Crow nights do **not** count. |

No estate-mansion cold grind (shelter/heat enough). No deliberate freeze self-train.

## Rank path (1 night = 1 use)

Field curve: **1 / 10 / 50 / 200 / 1,000 / 5,000…**

| Mark | Cum nights (soft) | Cold rank |
|---|---:|---|
| First Carwen cold night (Ch 13) | **1** | unlock **L1** (was Temperature Resistance) |
| Leave Carwen / enter train | **~100** | **L3** (clears **50**; short of **200**) |
| Train exit | **~100–103** | still **L3** |
| Desk exit / Ch 22 (into cold season) | **~220** | **L4** (clears **200**; short of **1,000**) |
| Ch 23 cold months (intense grill still winterish) | **~280** soft | **L4** with bar toward **L5**; **L5** only if more cold-season nights stack later |

Former frost-grill energy stays on Heat / Suffocation / Recovery / Fabrication / Taming (`FrictionGrill.md`, `Skills.md` Ch 19).

## Attr

**Vitality +1 / Endurance +1** per Cold level. Unlock **L1** on Carwen sheet. **L2–L3** by leave Carwen / train enter. **L4** on desk exit.

## Do not

- Frost grid / freeze patch / spare-mana cold (off).
- Credit warm-season nights “for the grind.”
- Invent Ned Cold from Roland’s sleep (Ned has no parallel night-sleep Cold lock here).
