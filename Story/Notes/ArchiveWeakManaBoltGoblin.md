# Archive. Weak Mana Bolt goblin (Ch 23)

Internal scrap only. Not live prose.

## Why it was written

The kill was softened under the idea that runic scrolls were really weak. Later locks disagree. Street Trox / original hide-and-ink Mana Bolt still sits around a **~250 J** materials ceiling. Goblin orbit skull failure is about **~32 J** (`References/World/Science/Energy/ManaCast.md`). A face or head hit should kill. His-blood seats hit harder (~**1.7 kJ** at normal Bolt). The soft beat below is kept only as a record of that wrong turn.

## Archived prose (replaced)

The goblin did not have much of a chance to react as a mana bolt smacked into the side of its face, stunning it briefly but not doing much damage. Roland clicked his tongue before channeling as much mana as he could into another scroll. Pushing mana into the scroll until he could sense it ready to burst, Roland activated the rune, sending another mana bolt flying toward the goblin. This one broke its jaw but still didn't kill it.

*Hmm, that is annoying.*

Roland leaned his head back on Ned as he considered the performance of the scrolls as he watched two of them crumble into dust. The highest level mana bolt scrolls he had made with the original hide and ink were severely limited in power, even when overcharged. Quickly muttering the chant, he launched another mana bolt that vanished as it blew a hole straight through the goblin's head.

Originally, Roland thought something was broken with the scroll but he quickly realized that he had basically never seen attack magic being used in this world and when he first used it, he already had nearly five times the Intelligence of an ordinary person. Compared to the rigor of runes, regular magic was extremely freeform and adaptable. When he focused on the pathways the mana formed as he chanted the spell, Roland could sometimes see vaguely similar pathways to the runes he scribed and sometimes the mana flowed in completely arbitrary directions. He couldn't make sense of it and decided not to push the issue. The original Roland had already died absorbing mana which meant that it was significantly more volatile and dangerous substance than he had hoped.
