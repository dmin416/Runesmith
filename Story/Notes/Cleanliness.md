# Cleanliness (story habit)

Roland wash / wipe habits printed in chapters. Earth biology and history: `../../References/World/Science/Body/Cleanliness.md`. Chapter wins if a beat disagrees.

## Habit lock

- After he **secures lodging**, he finds where guests wash (well, inn corner, paid tub) and rinses before he treats the place as home.
- On **journeys** he does not wait for a full bath. Morning or stop wipes of face, neck and underarms with a damp cloth / canteen water keep the worst road smell down.
- On **long hunt days** he wipes before walking back into town so the guild desk does not get the full forest smell.
- Desk grind still rinses **hands and face** when he goes down for a meal (ink and sweat).
- World hygiene is only so-so (Carwen party smell). He still wants a proper bath when he can pay for one. Full baths are not assumed with cheap rent.
