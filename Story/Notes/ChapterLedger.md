# Chapter Ledger (Ch 1–20)

Single place to read **time**, **sheet**, and **skill deltas** for every chapter through 20. Chapter prose wins when a full screen is printed. Detail and pad math: `Status.md`, `Skills.md`, `Experience.md`, `Timeline.md`, `StatusBreakdown.md`.

**MP:** `(Int×10)+(Will×4)`. Class levels permanently raise attributes. Class cards (Mage mana recovery) and Blessed by Mana (recovery multiplier) are narrative only. Printed sheet MP stands; do not retally off Mage %.

**HP** = `Vit×10 + End×3`. **SP** = `End×10 + Str×3 + Agi×3`.

**Men metaphors:** Int lines ≈ `round(Int/10)` men; Str lines ≈ `round(Str/15)` men.
