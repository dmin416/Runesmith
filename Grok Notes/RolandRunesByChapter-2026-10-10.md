## Purpose and Scope

Chapter-ordered list of runes Roland Arden (D) has crafted or created in completed Story chapters 1-80, drawn from `Story/Chapters/` prose and cross-referenced with `Story/Notes/`, `References/Runes/`, and existing `Grok Notes/`. Each entry includes: chapter number, rune name, rank/quality level, characteristics/effects, and cite (chapter file or notes path). Unknowns marked as unset rather than invented.

**Source limits:** Completed story chapters 1-80 only. `Story/Undone/` placeholders (Ch 81-711) not scanned; they are empty headings only.

**Note on terminology:**
- **Rank**: Lesser, Common, Greater, Grand, Legendary (complexity ladder; Lesser = linear chain, Common = shared modules, etc.)
- **Quality**: Lowest, Low, Intermediate, High, Highest (build quality within rank)
- **Notation**: `[craft quality, rune quality]` when both appear (e.g. `[Intermediate, High]`); single token is rune quality alone
- **Score**: `s = (rank index - 1) + quality fraction`; Lesser Highest and Common Lowest both score s=1

## Notable Craft Progression & Skill Context

### Runecraft Skill Leveling
- **Ch 35 trial**: Basic Runecraft unlocked (Runic Blacksmith class)
- **Ch 41-42**: Runecraft L2 via practice, then L3 via skill book (came with Lesser Fortitude diagram)
- **Ch 47-48**: Basic Runecraft L7 by ~1.5 year mark; enables lesser runes on arrows (~L7 needed); common runes still need tier 2 for arrow-scale compression
- **Supporting Skills**: Runic Blacksmith's Eyes L5 (see cracks/fissures real-time; guide inscription); Blacksmith's Heat Sense L6 (measure right heat); Basic Rune Compression L3 (condense runes smaller without power loss; needed for fitting runes on small items); Ethereal Pathways L6 (link separate parts on another wavelength; wireless send/receive)
- **Cite**: `Story/Chapters/41-50.md` Ch 48 lines 1112-1140; `Story/Notes/Skills.md`; `References/Runes/RuneCraftScrapes.md` skill section

### Progression Gates & Limits
- **Lesser runes**: Basic Runecraft covers Lesser setup; solo scrolls weak; metal + timing makes Lessers bite
- **Common runes**: Harder scribing (~L4+ before manageable); shared modules; double schematic size vs Lesser; Highest Common ≈ half-step into Greater (Ch 22)
- **Compression limits**: ~L7 Runecraft to fit Lesser runes on arrows; Common arrows need tier 2 compression
- **Metal gates**: Regular steel degrades fast with runes; deep iron/steel needed for durability (10× charges vs regular iron); better metal = harder inscription but longer item life
- **Mana costs**: Common Runecraft still drains heavily; deep iron harder without forge (magical fire softening preferred); 1.5 years to make Common-grade weapons feel comfortable
- **Cite**: `References/Runes/RuneSystem.md` rank ladder; `Story/Chapters/41-50.md` Ch 41-48 progression

### Crafting Techniques Evolution
- **Paper schematics**: Quill + paper; Diagnosis red/green/blue overlay; Calligraphy redraw ~1 hour
- **Scrolls**: Quill + magical ink + monster-skin parchment; mana into tip → ink → pathways; large→fine continuous pass
- **Metal (early)**: Heat + glowing hammer; blue sparks; symbols bit by bit; whole-length traces needed
- **Metal (advanced)**: Chisel pre-engraving (larger structures, near-finished look, missing traces) → hand Runecraft on unheated metal → Ethereal Pathways link parts
- **Dual runes**: Similar runes sharing components can combine; connected for separate activation via user's mana control
- **Mana stone integration**: Slot rune creates socket; stones lower spell mana cost (~20% per Lesser stone; limit ~3 per item before short-circuit risk)
- **Cite**: `References/Runes/RuneCraftScrapes.md` LOOK and MAKE sections; `Story/Chapters/41-50.md` Ch 47-49

## Notes & Limitations

1. **Schematic XP**: Lesser quality ladder is 100/200/400/600/1000 for Lowest/Low/Intermediate/High/Highest. Common runes give 2000 XP for Highest. Raising quality pays the difference only (e.g. High→Highest is +400, not another full 1000).

2. **Borrowing technique**: Ch 44/46 show Diagnosis "borrowing" by staring through glass at store weapons for ~10-20 minutes; Knowledge Retention trait + memory reconstruction at home; saves money but risky if caught; limited to visible runes (not regular enchantments).

3. **Trial items**: Class-change trials (Ch 16 Fire Orb scroll, Ch 35 Fire Resistance ladle) are pass/fail items, not sold. Ladle rated `[Lesser: Lowest, High]` split shows item craft vs rune quality can differ.

4. **Chapter 21.5 timeskip**: ~6 month desk grind (rewrite-only) between Ch 21 and Ch 22. Major inventions: homemade magic ink (goblin cores + blood), shape-family kit (Orb/Ball/Arrow/Bolt shared structure), component swap technique, runic Mana Bolt, Fireball/water arrow/light ball variants, Common Fire Arrow schematic + branded batch production. Basic Rune Scribing L1→L5 main grind. Full detail in `Story/Notes/Chapter21_5.md`.

5. **Missing details**: Some runes listed in `References/Runes/Runes.md` (Sharpness, Piercing, Velocity, etc.) are mentioned as knowledge or plans but not shown crafted on-page in Ch 1-80. Ch 44-45 dialogue references Sharpness but final product was Mana Slash/Thrust instead. If not in completed chapter prose, not listed here.

6. **Later Source beats**: Source (Ch 81-711) has many more runes, enchantments, and craft techniques (3D stacking, Miniaturisation, Rune Mending, Runic Restructuring, concealment, soul-path, etc.) but those are beyond scope of this Ch 1-80 compilation.

7. **Wand repairs**: Ch 61-70 show repeated wand repairs with runecrafting hammer; each repair degrades metal slightly; runes erode into steel until unusable. Better metals (deep steel, eventually mithril) needed for durability.

8. **Armor runes**: Ch 65-70 mention preparing full elemental resistance suite for armor (fire, cold, poison, curse) but completed armor not shown in detail by Ch 80. Shield dual-rune (Fire Resistance + Mana Shield) in Ch 70-73 is most detailed armor-class rune example.

9. **Utility/tool runes**: Pressure-trigger trap scrolls (Ch 37), runic powered grindstone (Ch 48+), runic grenades with timed detonation (Ch 51-60), elemental shield scrolls (Ch 51-60), detection orb (Ch 61-70), poison wand (Ch 69-70) demonstrate practical applications beyond combat weapons.
