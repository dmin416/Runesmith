# Source Chapter 334 – Mithril Smelting

Source dump: `References/Source/331-340.md` (catalog entry `# 336`).

## Design problem

- Red mithril + etherium: fine with normal mana / Mana Overflow; **Overlords Might** chaotic red mana jitters runes; combo purple smokes thin plate. Solution: much thicker armor (~3× weight), levitation buff −50% weight. Detachable floating cube drones (eye-golem core, propulsion runes) replace shoulder cannons.

## Smelting

- Recycles Emmerson mithril armor/shield via liquation + Forgefire Control; keeps sword temporarily. Steam energy from waste heat. Bernir uses Valerian seal letter to force Union dwarves to sell mithril tools. Starts gauntlets first.

## Major plot points

1. Armor must survive Overlord’s Might.
2. Enemy gear becomes feedstock.
3. Knight status breaks Union boycott.
