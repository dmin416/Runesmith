## Libra Auction House

- Large; multi-stage by rank; reject low-value stock.
- Appraisal via high Identify or costly mana tools; Percival preferred.
- Sellers: plaque number; optional back seating; anonymous; fee **25%** first-timer (can drop to **10%** with volume).
- Paid entrance for buyers; paddle bidding; settle in back.

## Scroll batch

- Crude gray wrap; lowest parchment look almost trashed.
- **10 Runic Fire Arrow** scrolls; red **shooting star / comet** logo.
- Grades: **8 High + 2 Highest** (Common tier); none Intermediate.
- Runic vs regular: buyer can **pump extra mana** to amplify (by rank).
- Percival estimates High ~10 small silver, Highest ~12–15; start bids guide auctioneer.
- Sold as **2 boxes of 5** (4 High + 1 Highest each); open **3 large silver**.
- Box1: **4 large + 5 small**; Box2: **4 large + 7 small** ≈ nearly **1 small gold** gross before cut.
