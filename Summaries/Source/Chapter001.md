## Plot beats (in order)

1. Earth repairman stays late replacing capacitors on a circuit board under a microscope; resentful of overtime and being the expendable new hire.
2. Finishes the board, tells the boss he is going home, takes coat and umbrella into the storm.
3. At a crosswalk, mind wanders; light turns green; a truck nearly runs him down; he dives to the sidewalk, scraped and soaked; umbrella crushed.
4. Premonition; same truck returns with lights off, then high beams; street empty; he runs for an alley.
5. Truck seems to vanish after clipping the building; he heads through the alley toward a police station on the far side.
6. Lights shatter; two trucks squeeze into the alley from both ends, scraping walls; empty cabins; he fails to climb the wet wall; **SPLAT** – crushed to death.
7. Void; woman’s voice calling a fragmented name he hears as **Roland**; pulled toward rebirth.
8. Wakes sick and heavy in a wooden-ceiling room; maid with damp cloth; old monocle physician says status is recovering and leaves; maid relieved, tells him to sleep, will check in a couple of hours.
9. Alone, he sits up: soft small hands, thin malnourished child’s body; realizes he is a kid and that “status recovering” was said about him.
10. Status window opens; brain dump of **Roland Arden**’s information floods in (painful); he accepts “so that’s how it is.”
