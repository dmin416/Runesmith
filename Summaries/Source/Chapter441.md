# Source Chapter 441 – Defeating Cerberus

Source dump: `References/Source/441-450.md` (catalog entry `# 443`).

## Cerberus counter-plan

- Brothers wary: self-restoring golems, Roland outmaneuvered them; must kill him not drones; flight risks **recording** exposure to **Institute**.
- **Andreas** leads charge; **Roman** hidden behind; **Elythaes** waits for opening with strongest technique.
- Golem beams illuminate swamp dungeon; Andreas **shield-sucks** projectiles; Roman evades; Elythaes holds back.

## Three-on-one melee

- Roland uses **Eyes of Mana** / mana phantoms; rock pillars vs **Roman**; sidestep + kick vs **Andreas**.
- Sustained blade/polearm exchange; terrain torn up; duo barely holds.

## Coordinated finisher

- Elythaes **“Now!”** — vanishes; slices golems; **Andreas** semi-transparent **chains** bind Roland across planes; **Roman** light-bent snake strike at hammer side.
- Roland drops **shield** and **hammer**; palm **electrical discharge** while all golems fire inward (he stays in blast core by plan).

## Aftermath of the exchange

- **Andreas** dead (chest cavity from palm blast); **Roman** alive but **sword arm** severed deflecting energy.
- **Elythaes** sonic-speed head strike: helmet off; sword in left chest/shoulder; only Elythaes standing briefly.
- Roland pulls blade out; wound **regenerates** (purple glow + gold healing radiance); armor mends; hammer/shield return (**Overlord’s Might** still active).
- Final duel: Elythaes disarmed into swamp; **“I’m just a simple Runesmith…”**
- Elythaes recognizes his **unarmored face** mid-kill: **“You… that face… are you actually…”** (cutoff).

## Major plot points

1. Cerberus binds Roland with Andreas’s chains so Roman and Elythaes can land killing blows.
2. Roland trades mutual destruction for a palm-plus-golem detonation that leaves two brothers down.
3. Elythaes’s last strike fails to finish Roland; face recognition hints Arden identity before the chapter ends.
