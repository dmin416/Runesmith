## Economy / craft facts

- Potion grades like runes: lesser→legendary (mostly lesser/common in shop).
- **Mana Arrow** scroll: **3 small silver** (Intermediate-ish).
- **Fire Arrow** (Tier 2): **6 small silver**; not much more potent; maker + materials matter; **Fireball** ~**1 large silver**.
- Scroll power grade = mana infused; checked by device (Lowest→Highest). Arch-mage Mana Arrow can beat Fire Arrow.
- Blank monster-skin scrolls: **10 for 5 small silver** (~A5; Tier 1–2 size); magic wood paper rarer/costlier.
- Ink for ~10 Mana Arrows ~**10 small silver**; solo Intermediate sales after cuts → poor wages.
- **Runic** scrolls dusty: need **Runesmith**; ~3× price (Orb of Light ≥1 large silver; Runic Fire Arrow **2 large silver** vs normal 6 small silver). Examples: Runic Orb of Light, Fire Arrow, Aqua Ball.
