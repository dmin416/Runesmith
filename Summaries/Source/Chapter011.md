## Places / dungeon lore

- Carwen Dungeon entrance: stone corridors, torches, truck-wide halls (maze-like approach).
- Floor 1 true area: **Emerald Wilderness** – gigantic jungle with artificial sun; kilometers across; steel-rank grounds.
- Floors continue to **10th** = dungeon boss; guild appointment; boss **respawns ~1 week**; strong parties hog boss room.
- Mana density in dungeon higher → more/better cores.
- Peculiar mana feel vs normal forest.
