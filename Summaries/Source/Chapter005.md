## Ascension sequence

1. Blue quartz-like symmetrical crystal; sit and focus; body stays put; soul pulled into dark fall.
2. Space = recreation of his **Earth cheap apartment building**; outside pitch black; doors sealed except **apartment 19** (his old unit).
3. Room fully detailed (holey mattress, PC); faucets/windows dead; PC boots Windows 10 with old password; only icon: sword+staff+shield.
4. Pixel-art class UI: walk to grayed mage-robe sprite → confirm **Tier 1 Mage**.
5. “Tutorial” VR headset drops (never owned one); white room → grassy plain.
6. Blank-faced mage clone casts a chanted bolt that splinters a wooden dummy; clone walks through him (immaterial) → pain + knowledge dump.
