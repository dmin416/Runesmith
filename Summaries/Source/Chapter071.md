## Setting lore (Albrook dungeon origin)

- Quiet farming/fishing town → stormy night quake → new volcano/smoke.
- Scouts find patterned man-made **gate** (dungeon); possible offshoot of central **Infernal Dragon** S-dungeon (connection risk: T3+ anywhere).
- **~Half year later**: boomtown inns/shops; dungeon still **unrated**; guild sells floor maps; beginner floors partly mapped.

## Roland’s kit / goal

- Brigandine + helm; arming sword; bought shield (runecrafted); 2 side wands; ~**185 cm**.
- Train CQC (sword/shield) not pure ranged cheese; chilling rune on armor (mana drain while active).
- Bought map for traps; detection device ≠ trap sense.

## Entrance

- Potion cart outside: ~12yo twin-bun girl hawking + older man; prices **~25%** above market; shady adventurer stares.
- Gate ~5 m, columns, flaming-skull keystone; Debugger finds **no runes** (maybe enchantments).
- Stairs → lit brick hub (**safe zone**); monsters can’t leave (barrier tests: goblin crushed at boundary); corpses reabsorbed if left.
- Labyrinth type: periodic loot chests; bosses respawn; mining section deeper/hotter.

## First fight

- Corridor with flaming skull; **Fire Slime L2** (deflated basketball, red core).
- Dodges heated spit; Mana-imbued sword kills without destroying core (sellable; metal cores possible).
- **+1 XP**; more fire slimes approach for training.

## Major plot points

1. Albrook dungeon = labyrinth offshoot of Infernal Dragon complex.
2. Solo training dive begins as warrior-mage.
3. Fire slimes = floor fodder.
