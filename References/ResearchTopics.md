## Physics and energy

1. **Electrostatics.** Friction makes charge. Static shocks, dust removal, spray coating, ion wind.
2. **Magnetism.** Ore sorting, compass making, induction heating, eddy-current brakes.
3. **Piezoelectricity.** Vibration turns into electricity. Igniters, sensors, crystal resonators.
4. **Thermoelectrics.** A heat difference makes current. Camp power from a fire.
5. **Phase change and latent heat.** Ice storage, cooling by evaporation, dew harvesting.
6. **Cryogenics.** Liquid air, cold storage, brittle fracture.
7. **Plasma.** Arcs, cutting torches, neon-style glow.
8. **Superconductivity.** What mythril or aurium might do at room temperature.
9. **Radiation pressure.** Light sails, how strong light's push really is.
10. **Ultrasonics.** Cleaning, welding, cutting and medical imaging.
11. **Infrasound.** Dread, nausea, long-range elephant-style signaling.
12. **Acoustic levitation.** Holding droplets and small parts without contact.
13. **Phased arrays.** Steering sound or force by timing many emitters.
14. **Impedance matching.** Getting energy to cross from air into the body or into stone.
15. **Resonant energy transfer.** Wireless power between tuned coils or crystals.
16. **Brownian ratchets.** Turning random jiggling into directed motion.
17. **Capillarity and wicking.** Lamp wicks, dye work, self-feeding pens.
18. **Tribology of ice.** Skates, sleds, sliding on snow.
19. **Cavitation damage.** Ship props, pump wear, mantis-shrimp punches.
20. **Water hammer.** Destroying pipes, or using the surge to ram water uphill.
21. **Surface waves.** Ripple tanks, dampening a ship's wake.
22. **Granular flow.** Hourglasses, silos jamming, sand behaving like a liquid.
23. **Thermal radiation.** Black-body glow, radiant cooling to the night sky.
24. **Radiative sky cooling.** Making ice in a desert at night.
25. **Elastic energy storage.** Springs, bows, rubber, tendons and which stores the most per kg.
26. **Shock absorption.** Crumple zones, honeycomb, padding.
