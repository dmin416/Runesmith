# Defending Against a 2x Speed 2x Strength Attacker (Mage Favored)

Gist for fights where a physical specialist outruns and outmuscles a mage body. Mage wins on **Mana Hands** (mind-speed telekinesis), perception and prep. Physical backup is optional depth not a required crutch.

Related: `AttackScale.md`, `Science.md` (Mana Hands), `Spells.md`.

## Premises

- Attacker moves at **2x** speed and has **2x** strength with a normal stamina pool.
- Defender is a mage with **3x** perception and a body that moves at **1x**.
- Weapons are similar weight (about **1 to 1.4 kg** one-handed sword or equivalent).
- Attacker is trying to stab the defender to death.
- Speed does not add stamina. Fast and strong actions drain the pool faster.
- Execution speed is not reaction speed. Fast self-initiated actions carry no reaction latency while responding to a stimulus always does. Jerry Miculek and Bob Munden shoot as fast as they want but cannot catch bullets.
- Magic costs more energy than a mechanical equivalent but stays in a similar range.
- **Mana Hands** is telekinesis at the speed of the mage’s mind. No verbal component and no cast time. Actuation is not limited by body speed. Limit is mental focus and willpower.
- Mana Hands speed is paid as kinetic energy. Cost scales with the **square** of actuation speed for the same motion.

## Attack speed by technique

| Attack | Ready to contact | Peak tip speed | Added reach | Recovery to guard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short stab | 0.12 to 0.20 s | 5 to 8 m/s | none | 0.15 to 0.25 s |
| Short wrist cut | 0.15 to 0.25 s | 8 to 12 m/s | none | 0.20 to 0.30 s |
| Lunge thrust | 0.30 to 0.45 s | 4 to 6 m/s at the hand | 0.8 to 1.2 m | 0.40 to 0.70 s |
| Full swing | 0.35 to 0.65 s | 15 to 25 m/s | small | 0.40 to 0.80 s |

At **2x** speed the short stab lands in about **0.075 s** and the lunge in about **0.15 to 0.22 s**. The short stab is the fastest effective attack within arm reach. The lunge is the fastest way to cover distance with a point.

## Physics and energy

- Speed costs energy quadratically. Kinetic energy needed is about `2md²/t²`. Halving the time quadruples the energy and multiplies power by eight.
- A stab moves an effective mass of **1.5 to 2.5 kg** over **0.4 to 0.6 m** and delivers roughly **20 to 45 J**. Penetrating clothing and flesh needs about **10 to 25 J**.
- A full swing carries **50 to 90 J** in the blade alone. Most of the surplus is wasted braking the arc.
- A lunge puts **150 to 250 kg·m/s** of body momentum behind the point. Momentum does not drive penetration but it keeps the blade from stalling and adds shove.
- Muscle efficiency of **20 to 25%** makes metabolic cost four to five times the mechanical work.

**Relative energy cost per attack:**

- Short stab: **1x**
- Short wrist cut: about **1.5x**
- Full swing: **3 to 5x**
- Lunge: **4 to 8x** (mostly locomotion)

Sideways deflection of a thrust needs only **1 to 3 J** of work at the weak outer third of the blade. That work is independent of how hard the attacker drives. Doubled strength punishes head-on blocks but barely affects a redirect. At a magic efficiency of about half the redirect costs roughly **2 to 6 J** of magical energy. Mana Hands only needs enough force to shift the point a few centimeters so its strength ceiling rarely matters for a nudge.

Mana Hands speed carries its own cost. The base nudge of **2 to 6 J** assumes a normal-speed motion of **0.05 to 0.08 s**. Moving the same distance faster needs energy that scales as `1/t²`:

| Actuation speed | Time for 5 to 10 cm nudge | Energy multiplier | Approx. cost per nudge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1x (baseline) | 0.05 to 0.08 s | 1x | 2 to 6 J |
| 2x | 0.025 to 0.04 s | 4x | 8 to 24 J |
| 3x (full mind speed) | 0.02 to 0.03 s | 9x | 18 to 54 J |

A full mind-speed nudge costs about as much as the stab it stops (**20 to 45 J**). Buy speed only as needed to beat the deadline. Pre-positioning and precursor reads keep most nudges in the cheap band.

## Reaction versus execution

- **Attacker:** Self-initiated 2x speed has zero reaction latency. The first movement starts the clock.
- **Defender reacting with the body:** 3x perception cuts latency from about **0.2 s** to about **0.07 s**. A buckler tap adds **0.05 to 0.08 s** because the arm moves at 1x. Total is about **0.12 to 0.15 s**.
- **Defender reacting with Mana Hands:** Perception latency stays at about **0.07 s**. Actuation runs at mind speed so a 5 to 10 cm point nudge takes roughly **0.02 to 0.03 s**. Total is about **0.09 to 0.10 s** with no cast time in between.
- **Result against a lunge:** The lunge needs **0.15 to 0.22 s** so a Mana Hands nudge lands with **0.05 to 0.12 s** to spare even without a cue. The spare time allows slower actuation at a fraction of the energy.
- **Result against a short stab from guard:** The stab lands about **0.075 s** after the first movement. An uncued reaction is tight. A precursor cue or a **pre-positioned** Mana Hand closes the gap cleanly.

The winning defense is either already committed before the attack starts or fast enough to fire inside the attack window. Mana Hands does both.

## Why the mage has the advantage

- **Mana Hands removes the body speed limit.** Reaction is perception then mind-speed actuation. The 1x arm no longer sets defensive speed.
- **No cast latency.** Live redirects cost only the perception latency.
- **Pre-positioned Mana Hand acts as a floating buckler.** Holds the line with no hand used and follows the point at mind speed.
- **Pre-cast defenses remove reaction time.** A ward or field cast before the exchange has no latency when the stab arrives.
- **Triggered effects fire on the attacker’s motion.** Contact or proximity resolves the instant the point enters the field.
- **Strength is irrelevant to a redirect.** A few centimeters of nudge works the same against a 1x or 2x arm.
- **Coverage is total.** Mana Hands reaches any angle and distance with no arc limit. Feints lose most of their value because the guard follows the point at mind speed.
- **3x perception goes to strategy.** With Mana Hands on raw defense the mage spends perception on reading stance and choosing counters.
- **Energy favors the mage when speed matches the deadline.** The attacker burns the pool in short bursts while moderate-speed nudges cost a few joules each.
- **Range control.** Terrain and movement impairment force a readable step or a costly lunge.

## Defense options ranked

1. **Pre-cast passive ward or redirect field.** Zero latency and strength independent.
2. **Mana Hand guard.** Pre-positioned on the most direct line and nudging the point at mind speed. Covers uncued and off-line attacks. Pre-positioning allows a slower cheaper nudge.
3. **Triggered reactive effects.** Contact or proximity nudge that fires without a decision.
4. **Precursor reading with 3x perception.** Weight shifts, hip rotation and shoulder drop appear **0.1 to 0.3 s** before the blade moves. Fire Mana Hands on the cue rather than the blade.
5. **Distance and terrain control.** Range beyond the lunge forces a readable step. Difficult ground and narrow lines limit angles.
6. **Buckler and sword point in line.** Physical layer that needs no mana. Useful depth not the main plan.
7. **Tempo breaking and attrition.** Irregular timing and forced misses drain the attacker’s stamina.

## Buckler as optional backup

- Deflection needs **5 to 10 cm** of wrist movement in **0.05 to 0.08 s**.
- Tilt the face so the point skates off. Steel of **2 mm** or more resists better than wood.
- Small **~25 cm** face. Feints and off-line lines are weaker here than under Mana Hands.
- Keep it when magic is suppressed or the pool is empty. A sword point held in line still takes initiative and forces the attacker to beat it aside.

## Setup notes (not soft spots)

These are solved by how the mage fights. They are not reasons the physicalist wins.

- **Uncued short stab:** Pre-position the Hand and fire on precursor body tells. Perception already buys the early read.
- **Nudge not stop:** Redirects win. Do not try to catch a 2x thrust head-on.
- **Speed pricing:** Match actuation to the deadline. Most exchanges sit under full mind-speed cost.
- **One Hand one primary line:** Layer a simple passive field for coverage. Keep the Hand on the kill line.
- **Sustained focus:** Prefer a cheap standing field plus short nudges over constant max-speed tracking.
- **Suppression:** Buckler and armor cover the rare no-magic case. Default kit still stacks Hands first.

# 3x Everything (Same Weight) vs Meaningful Attacks

Mostly yes. **3x** tissue strength moves breach thresholds by **3x** while real attacks sit **10 to 300x** above them so the body still gets pierced. The difference is depth and survivability.

## Breach check

Breach force for a point is about **5 to 30 N** on normal skin so **3x** skin puts it at **15 to 90 N**.

| Attack | Tip force | Breaches 3x skin? |
|---|---|---|
| Finger poke | ~1 N | No (already harmless) |
| Weak sword jab | ~50 N | Sometimes |
| Typical sword thrust | ~200 N | Yes |
| Strong lunge | 500 to 1,000 N | Yes |
| Warbow arrow | 3,000 to 15,000 N | Yes |
| Bullet, bolt or spear at 2x strength | 1,000+ N | Yes |

Pressure at a sword point is about **500x** a fingertip’s for the same force so a **3x** multiplier does not close the gap. A **3x** human is still a regular human against nearly any meaningful piercing attack.

## What 3x does change

- **Penetration depth drops to about a third.** Mean resisting force rises from **100 to 250 N** to **300 to 750 N** so a **20 to 45 J** thrust reaches **3 to 15 cm** instead of **8 to 45 cm**. Many thrusts that would reach vital organs on a normal body stop in the chest wall or muscle.
- **Arrows still go through.** **100 to 150 J** drives **13 to 50 cm** at **3x** against **40 to 150 cm** at baseline. Torso hits still penetrate the body.
- **Cuts are shallower.** A **50 to 90 J** sword swing opens a wound but the depth and severity fall by roughly a third.
- **Blunt trauma tolerance triples.** Bones, organs and vessels that resist **3x** the force absorb hits that would break a normal body. This is the largest survival gain because stopped energy still transfers in full.
- **Bleeding and shock margins** also improve when blood volume and clotting scale.

## Offense at 3x

Energy per stroke is **3x** so speed for the same moving mass is **√3 ≈ 1.7x**.

- A short stab of **20 to 45 J** becomes **60 to 135 J**.
- Stab time drops from **0.12 to 0.20 s** to about **0.07 to 0.12 s**.
- Against a normal person this exceeds the **10 to 25 J** breach and depth requirement by **2 to 13x**.

## Against earlier matchups

- A **3x**-tissue defender against the **2x** speed and **2x** strength attacker still takes **400 N** thrusts through skin but each thrust reaches roughly a third of baseline depth.
- The stamina attrition plan is unchanged because **3x** energy output also costs **3x** metabolic power at the same efficiency.

## Where 3x fails completely

Firearms (**400 to 4,000 J**), explosions, vehicle impacts and falls exceed a **3x** body by wide margins. Meaningful protection from piercing needs **100 to 300x** skin strength as computed before.
