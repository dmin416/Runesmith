## Healing / recovery potions

First seen: Chapter 14 (low-grade; Roland’s bag in the watcher fight). Later chapters also say **healing potion** for the same class of brew.

Alchemical health potion. Drink and/or pour on a wound. Restores health by **closing flesh** and driving recovery through torn muscle, not by ticking an abstract HP bar alone.

**On-page (Ch 14 rewrite, low-grade):**
- Down half, pour the rest on open wounds (shoulder and calf).
- Contact **sizzles**; cuts knit shut under the liquid faster than a bandage.
- Swallow burn floods torn muscle and staunches leftover bleed.
- Pain of the brew can be **worse than the wound** itself.
- Low-grade still leaves **soreness** under sealed holes; not a full priest-grade mend.

**Grades (shop talk):** Lowest → Highest, same scale as other alchemical goods. Low-grade is the early adventurer stock. Higher grades close more and leave less residual ache (`Economy.md` for price bands). Hangover potions are a separate item.

Priest healing spells remain the expensive alternative when a common-grade potion is not enough (Ch 33 comparison).
