# Traits

System traits named in the story. Holder noted when not Roland.

## Learning and mind

### Logical Thinker
First seen: Chapter 2
Thinks calmly, can analyze problems and come to a rational conclusion. Bonus to Intelligence +5 and Willpower +5. Brought from D’s previous life.

### Fast Learning
First seen: Chapter 2
Absorb knowledge at an increased rate. Bonus to Intelligence +5. Original Roland (boy) trait kept after transfer.

### Knowledge Retention
First seen: Chapter 2
Retain more information and at a faster rate. Bonus to Intelligence +3 and Willpower +5. Original boy trait kept after transfer. Also called memory retention in prose.

### Blessed by Mana
First seen: Chapter 6
Unlocked if a person has more than 40 intelligence after reaching the mage class at the first ascension ritual. Gives **+10 MP per Mage level** on top of the Mage class’s +20% mana (see Chapter 6 status math in `Status.md`). Also described in prose as bonuses to MP after each level up. Intelligence row of the attribute perk table below.

### Parallel Thinking / Multiple Mind(s)
First seen: Chapter 148
Gained while multitasking under heavy workload. Lets the holder think about multiple things at once; scales with intelligence. After tier 3 it advances further (prose later calls it Multiple Mind / Multiple Minds). Not separate personalities.

### Brilliant I
First seen: Chapter 164
Intelligence past 200. Flat +1000 MP. Aids memory and Parallel Thinking.

### Creativity
First seen: Chapter 196
Helps develop and express ideas in new ways. Gained during Runic Engineer trial work.

### Dexterous I
First seen: Chapter 217
Dexterity past 200. More nimble hands; faster, more graceful fine work.

## Body and stamina thresholds

### Fortitude I
First seen: Chapter 251
Endurance past 200. Stamina recovery up; stamina use down.

### Vigour I
First seen: Chapter 289
Vitality past 200. Improves HP recovery and some resistance.

### Might I
First seen: Chapter 289
Strength past 200. Reinforces musculature; can carry more weight.

### Swiftness I
First seen: Chapter 319
Agility past 200. Better balance and perception during rapid movement.

### Equilibrium I
First seen: Chapter 319
Earned when all seven main-stat 200 traits are held (the willpower-threshold trait is implied by this set but never named on-page). Cryptic; hidden resist buffs from balanced stats.

## Titles (monster / deed)

Titles are status cards separate from traits. Some give combat bonuses.

### Goblin Hunter
First seen: Chapter 10
Earned by goblin hunting. Lower-level goblins **might** take a fear debuff vs the holder. Nickname talk: Little Goblin Slayer. Later evolves to Goblin Slaughterer (Chapter 37).

### Runic Scholar
First seen: Chapter 15
Gained with the first [Highest] lesser rune schematic (Lesser Detonation Rune). Status text: knowledgeable in the runic language; has created and/or understands runes. Unlocks **Runic Mana Scrybe** at class change (Chapter 16). Later evolves to Runic Scholar II (Chapter 48; small bonus to rune-related skills).

## Notes
