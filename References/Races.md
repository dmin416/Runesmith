# Races

Sentient peoples and bloodlines from the story. Wild monsters (goblins, monster orcs, etc.) are listed in Creatures.md.

## Human
First seen: Chapter 4
Dominant population of the Kingdom of Caldris. Most nobility expect pure human blood. Roland, Elodia, and most of the Arden household are human.

## Dwarf
First seen: Chapter 8
Craft-focused people. Traditionally live underground near volcanic forges; many clans now work as craftsmen in the kingdom after a past sundering. Strong runesmithing and golem traditions (Hasim, Brylvia, Institute dwarven craftsmen). Roland’s first living dwarf is a short, wide axeman (<150 cm) in a Carwen adventurer party.

### Half-Dwarf
First seen: Chapter 87
Mixed human and dwarf blood. Often face dwarf prejudice, but many work as smiths or porters. Bernir (human mother) is the main example; Jorg and Thordin may show traits later.

## Elf
First seen: Chapter 8
Long-lived humanoids with pointed ears. Full elves are often treated differently from half-breeds in slave markets and noble society. Roland’s first living elf is a golden-haired Carwen guild archer in green with longbow and quiver. He notes blond or white hair as common; he does not spot dark elves early on, though books imply they exist. Chapter 20: Zilyana looks late teens but could be fifty-plus.

### Sun Elf
First seen: Chapter 26
Tied to the Holy Kingdom of Alexandria and Solarian faith. Default elf Identify tag around Edelgard. Longstanding rivals of Moon elves. Roland's Identify on Zilyana returns only "Sun Elf" (Chapter 26). Solana (Albrook guild receptionist) is a named sun elf.

### Moon Elf
First seen: Chapter 26 (rival talk); on-page true forms Chapter 34 / 39
Homeland associated with Bolia (council of elders rather than a single monarch). Linked to Lunaria and Lunaris. Silvery-white hair is common; prose compares them to dark elves. Do not get along with Sun elves. Arthur’s mother and her household are moon elves; Aredhel and Logon also appear (Chapter 58–64: moon-elf slaves from caravan; Bolia Irithyl household; Golgrim Half-Orc ally; part ways after village rescue). Edelgard: Zilyana (Exeor attendant; Chapter 39 true form white hair, dark brown skin, silver eyes, shadow travel) and Ziron (Chapter 39; gold eyes, dual curved short swords, runic black leather; Chapter 52: moon-elf male fights Abyssal Warlock outside Exeor).

### Half-Elf
First seen: Chapter 100
Pointed ears shorter than full elves. Often stigmatized as half-breeds. Lobelia is a half-elf; Arthur carries moon-elf blood and is treated as half-elven in noble politics.

### Half-Moon Elf
First seen: Chapter 422
Explicit Moon-elf mixed lineage: shorter pointed ears, silver hair. Guest swordsman at the Institute.

### Green-skinned Elf
First seen: Chapter 622
Unnamed plant-affiliated elf type. Headmistress Yavenna Arvandus has green or mossy skin and hair and a plant-grown tower (Lady of Thorns). Not labeled “wood elf” in the text.

## Goliath
First seen: Chapter 8
Sometimes called smaller giants. Prefer mountains; rare in warm climates. Massive frames and warrior reputation; book talk: strong but lower intelligence, solve problems by fighting. Roland’s first sighting is at the Carwen guild. Chapter 10 party: **Sahildr** (~2 m, brown skin, shield warrior). Named later: Aurdhan, Agthak, Kabir, and various knights or wardens.

## Dragon-kin
First seen: Chapter 8
Lizard-like people said to carry dragon blood. Can be confused with lesser lizardman monsters. Roland sees them in the Carwen Adventurer’s Guild.

## Beastmen / Beast Race
First seen: Chapter 10 (animal-eared adventurers at Carwen guild); broader Chapter 11
Umbrella race of animal-tribe peoples. Pure beastmen show more animal faces, fur on limbs, and claws. Lesser beast races look mostly human with ears and a tail (Kassia, Dyana, Mary, Reyna). Tribes often keep apart (feline, ursine, lizardlike, wolven, and others); varied looks within a tribe (e.g. lionlike vs tigerlike felines) make unity hard.

### Feline Beastman / Puma Beast Race
First seen: Chapter 11
Cat ears and tails. Range from mild traits (Mary, Kassia, assassin-maids with black cat ears) to near-full puma humanoid (Professor Arion). Lionlike and tigerlike variants exist within the feline umbrella.

### Ursine and Lizardlike Tribes
First seen: Chapter 11
Bear-like and lizardlike beast tribes are mentioned as existing divisions; not heavily featured as named characters.

### Mountain Goat Beastman Tribe
First seen: Chapter 127
Short white beards, short tails, long black horns, rectangular eyes. Famed climbers.

### Fox and Dog Beastmen
First seen: Chapter 156
Fox-like or floppy dog ears. Renny (dog) and Jasper (fox) of the thieves’ guild are called half-beastmen.

### Velvet Grove Beastmen (sheep / stag / goat)
First seen: Chapter 170
Heavily animal-bodied peoples in Arthur’s ascension trial (sheep bride Meleen, stag priest, goat butler). Contrasted with mild-trait city beastmen like Mary. Trial vision; not confirmed as a living nation in the Source dump.

### Rabbit Beast Race
First seen: Chapter 409
Speed-linked beast attributes. A rabbit-eared girl appears among Institute-area travelers and porters.

### Wolven Beast Tribe
First seen: Chapter 10 (Reyna); broader talk Chapter 479
Wolf ears and tail. **Reyna** (Carwen scout; gray hair, white bushy tail, dual daggers). Atasuna (Institute student). Silver wolf tribes are associated with silvery-white hair.

### Bullish / Horned Beastman
First seen: Chapter 527
Horns and bullish features. Dyana (Bernir’s wife) is described as lesser beast race with bullish traits.

## Gnome
First seen: Chapter 19
Small, brilliant, and dexterous. Favor alchemy and mage work over front-line combat. Chapter 19: invent magic trains; specialize in intricate machinery, trinkets and magical accessories (vs dwarves' heavy weapons/armor). Pure gnomes often have rounder heads and larger eyes. Rastix is a main later named gnome; others appear in guilds and auctions.

### Half-Gnome
First seen: Chapter 24 (Helci)
Taller than pure gnomes (**Helci** ~**140 cm**), finer features, more human proportions. Long orange hair in her early look. Orphan / Solaria orphanage path common for abandoned kids.

## Halfling
First seen: Chapter 29
About half human height, otherwise human-like proportions. Senna and various Albrook clerks and adventurers.

## Orc bloodlines
Wild orcs appear as monsters (see Creatures.md). Civilized people with orc blood live and work in cities as adventurers and laborers.

### Half-Orc
First seen: Chapter 40
Strong orc-blooded people, often stereotyped as brutes. Korgak, Golgrim, and other Albrook adventurers.

### Quarter-Orc
First seen: Chapter 650
Thinner orc blood than half-orcs; still large and muscular. Example: the quarter-orc man who confronts Millie’s father.

## Dracs / Dragon-kin
First seen: Chapter 67
Lizard-like people rumored related to dragons. Slightly taller than humans; eloquent. Distinct from mindless lizard-men monsters. Seen in the Dragnis Island port town. Calling them lizards risks a fight.
