# Adventurer Ranks

Guild adventurer ranks. Canon order from Source glossary (`../Source/1-10.md`). Population falloff: `../World/Society/Population.md`. Chapter detail: `AdventurerRanksDesign.md`.

## Ladder (lowest to highest)

There are **eight** ranks:

**Bronze → Steel → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Mithril → Orichalcum → Adamantium**

Source uses the spelling **Mithril** for this rank. No iron or copper adventurer rank. Those metals appear as materials or currency only.

## Rough class-tier mapping

| Adventurer ranks | Typical class tier |
|---|---|
| Bronze, Steel | Tier 1 |
| Silver, Gold | Tier 2 |
| Platinum, Mithril | Tier 3+ |
| Orichalcum | Tier 4 territory |
| Adamantium | Highest; rare titans (Tier 4 or 5 talk) |

Most adventurers stop at Silver. Gold is above average. Past Platinum is elite. Mithril / Orichalcum / Adamantium appear on-page in mid–late Source. Adamantium is countable on one hand in early prestige talk. Chapter 13 talk: real money opens at Silver.

## Rank notes

### Bronze

Starting rank. Ascension + Tier 1 class. Guild measuring crystal reads name, class and stats (guild-confidential). Card fee **2 LS**; thumb blood binds the card. Bronze and Steel jobs. Cannot enter dungeon alone without party or Steel. Showing the card can skip city gate exit fee (Ch 9). Albrook can issue Bronze when prior-card info is missing (Wayland, Ch 70).

### Steel

Monster work opens. Floor-1 hunting grounds. Promotion after second Tier 1 + missions + about a half-year trial (Ch 18 Miss Cellica). Steel map discount **5%** at Edelgard guild (Ch 19). World talk: Steel is not "serious" until Silver.

### Silver

Better pay. Needs Tier 2. Guild gets stricter from here.

### Gold

Above average. Lead small expeditions. Formal rank-up test from Silver. Roughly levels 100–150 in talk.

### Platinum

Tier 3 territory. Separate platinum test after Tier 3 bump (~level 150+). Common elite mid Source.

### Mithril

Above Platinum. Brighter card. Often second Tier 3 / ~level 250+ talk. One step from Tier 4 in some narration.

### Orichalcum

Super elites. Tier 4 class-holder territory. Needed hope vs S-rank (above Tier 3) monsters.

### Adamantium

Highest guild rank. Extreme S-rank content still kills parties at this level.

## Access and confidentiality

- Bronze: party or Steel for dungeon entry (Carwen early rules)
- Some shops and floors need Silver+
- Late capital / Isgard guild floors split beginners / Tier 3 / Mithril–Orichalcum–Adamantium / guild master
- High-level tamed beasts may need a matching master rank for city entry
- Guilds will not give out member locations to ordinary nobles; only large Ducal houses or the King have that pull (Ch 19 Wentworth)
