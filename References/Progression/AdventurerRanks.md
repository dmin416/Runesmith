# Adventurer Ranks

Guild adventurer ranks. Canon order from Source glossary (`../Source/1-10.md`). Population falloff: `../World/Society/Population.md`. Old design duplicate retired into this file (`AdventurerRanksDesign.md` is a stub).

## Ladder (lowest to highest)

There are **eight** ranks:

**Bronze → Steel → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Mithril → Orichalcum → Adamantium**

Source uses the spelling **Mithril** for this rank. No iron or copper adventurer rank. Those metals appear as materials or currency only.

## Rough class-tier mapping

| Adventurer ranks | Typical class tier |
|---|---|
| Bronze, Steel | Tier 1 |
| Silver, Gold | Tier 2 |
| Platinum, Mithril | Tier 3+ |
| Orichalcum | Tier 4 territory |
| Adamantium | Highest; rare titans (Tier 4 or 5 talk) |

Most adventurers stop at Silver. Gold is above average. Past Platinum is elite. Mithril / Orichalcum / Adamantium appear on-page in mid–late Source. Adamantium is countable on one hand in early prestige talk. Chapter 13 talk: real money opens at Silver.

## Rank notes

### Bronze

First seen: Chapter 8 (registration).

Starting rank. Ascension + Tier 1 class. Guild measuring crystal reads name, class and stats (guild-confidential). Card fee **2 LS**; thumb blood binds the card. Card shows first name (and optional family name/title), photo and rank. Bronze and Steel jobs. Prefer own rank. Failed job → fine. Odd jobs common on the bronze board. Cannot enter dungeon alone without party or Steel. Showing the card can skip city gate exit fee (Ch 9). Albrook can issue Bronze when prior-card info is missing (Wayland, Ch 70).

### Steel

First seen: Chapter 8 (job board / dungeon access talk); hunting grounds named in early Source.

Monster work opens. Floor-1 hunting grounds. Promotion after second Tier 1 + missions + about a half-year trial (Ch 18 Miss Cellica; measuring orb again). On-page rewrite reaction is warm (`"*You know, it's pretty nice.*"`); Source added that Steel is not serious until Silver (still true as world talk later). Steel map discount **5%** at Edelgard guild (Ch 19). Chapter 27: Roland still Steel after ~1 year idle in Edelgard (occasional goblin grind); accepted on Manstos Grotto with no rank gate (mage value helps). Chapter 68: Roland claims Steel at Albrook as Wayland after a "lost" card; status-hide pendant garbles name/classes on the measuring orb. Chapter 70: guild still issues Bronze for lack of verified prior info.

### Silver

First seen as concept: early Source (~Ch 11–20); tests later.

Better pay. Needs Tier 2. Guild gets stricter from here.

### Gold

First seen: early crisis talk (~Ch 31–40).

Above average. Lead small expeditions and command lower ranks. Formal rank-up test from Silver (often a travel quest). Roughly levels 100–150 in talk.

### Platinum

First seen as concept early; common elite rank mid Source.

Tier 3 territory. Separate platinum test after Tier 3 bump (~level 150+). Roland holds platinum identity cards later. Horse rental and gate access improve with high rank.

### Mithril

First seen: glossary; cards and grades mid–late Source.

Above Platinum. Brighter card. Often second Tier 3 / ~level 250+ talk. One step from Tier 4 in some narration.

### Orichalcum

First seen: glossary; as rank mid Source (~Ch 271+).

Super elites. Tier 4 class-holder territory. Needed hope vs S-rank (above Tier 3) monsters.

### Adamantium

First seen: glossary; rarest top rank.

Highest guild rank. Extreme S-rank content still kills parties at this level. Countable on one hand in early prestige talk.

## Access and confidentiality

- Bronze: party or Steel for dungeon entry (Carwen early rules)
- Some shops and floors need Silver+
- Late capital / Isgard guild floors split beginners / Tier 3 / Mithril–Orichalcum–Adamantium / guild master
- High-level tamed beasts may need a matching master rank for city entry
- Guilds will not give out member locations to ordinary nobles; only large Ducal houses or the King have that pull (Ch 19 Wentworth)
