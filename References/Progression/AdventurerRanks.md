# AdventurerRanks

Guild adventurer ranks. Canon order from Source glossary (`Source/1-10.md`).

## Ladder (lowest to highest)

There are **eight** ranks (more than five):

**Bronze → Steel → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Mithril → Orichalcum → Adamantium**

No iron or copper adventurer rank. Those metals appear as materials or currency only.

## Rough class-tier mapping

From early narration (`Source/11-20.md`):

| Adventurer ranks | Typical class tier |
|---|---|
| Bronze, Steel | Tier 1 |
| Silver, Gold | Tier 2 |
| Platinum and above | Tier 3+ |
| Orichalcum | Tier 4 territory |
| Adamantium | Highest; rare titans (Tier 4 or 5 territory in talk) |

Most adventurers stop at Silver. Gold is above average. Past Platinum is elite. Top ranks are very few (countable on one hand; countries defer to them). Chapter 13 talk: real money opens at Silver; Adamantium may need Tier 4 or 5.

## Rank notes

### Bronze
First seen: Chapter 8 (registration).
Starting rank. Requirements: completed ascension and a Tier 1 class. Guild measuring crystal reads full name, class and stats (confidential to the guild). Card fee **2 large silver**; thumb blood binds the card. Card shows first name (and optional family name/title), photo and rank. May take bronze and steel jobs. Prefer own rank. Failed job → fine. Odd jobs common on the bronze board. Cannot enter dungeon alone; needs a party or Steel. Chapter 9: showing the card skips the city gate fee on exit. Chapter 70: Albrook issues Wayland a Bronze card despite a Steel claim because prior-card info is missing; unmapped Albrook dungeon still allows free entry.

### Steel
First seen: Chapter 8 (job board / dungeon access talk); hunting grounds named in early Source.
Monster work opens. Carwen dungeon floor 1 is steel hunting ground in early party arc. Promotion from Bronze: second Tier 1 class + finished missions + about a **half-year** trial period (Chapter 18: Roland via **Miss Cellica**; measuring orb again). Steel still not taken seriously until Silver. Chapter 19: Steel map discount **5%** at Edelgard guild. Chapter 27: Roland still Steel after ~1 year idle in Edelgard (occasional goblin grind); accepted on Manstos Grotto with no rank gate (mage value helps). Chapter 68: Roland claims Steel at Albrook as Wayland after a "lost" card; status-hide pendant garbles name/classes on the measuring orb. Chapter 70: guild still issues Bronze for lack of verified prior info.

Guild confidentiality: Adventurer's Guilds will not give out member locations to ordinary nobles; only large Ducal houses or the King have that pull (Chapter 19 Wentworth).

### Silver
First seen as concept: early Source (~Ch 11–20); tests later.
Better pay. Needs Tier 2 class. Guild gets stricter from Silver on.

### Gold
First seen: early crisis talk (~Ch 31–40).
Above average. Can lead small expeditions and command lower ranks. Formal rank-up test from Silver (often a travel quest). Roughly levels 100–150 in talk.

### Platinum
First seen as concept early; common elite rank mid Source.
Tier 3 territory. Separate platinum rank-up test after Tier 3 bump (~level 150+). Roland holds platinum identity cards later. Horse rental and gate access improve with high rank.

### Mithril
First seen: glossary; cards and grades mid–late Source.
Often tied to a second Tier 3 class / ~level 250+ talk. Brighter card than platinum. One step from Tier 4 in some narration.

### Orichalcum
First seen: glossary; as rank mid Source (~Ch 271+).
Super elites. Tier 4 class-holder territory. Needed hope vs S-rank (above Tier 3) monsters.

### Adamantium
First seen: glossary; rarest top rank.
Highest guild rank. Parties at this level still die in extreme S-rank content. Countable on one hand in early prestige talk.

## Access examples

- Bronze: party or Steel for dungeon entry (Carwen early rules).
- Some shops and dungeon floors need Silver+.
- Isgard guild building floors split by rank/power (beginners / Tier 3 / Mithril–Orichalcum–Adamantium / guild master).
- High-level tamed beasts may need a matching master rank for city entry.
