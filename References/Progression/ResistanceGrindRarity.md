# Resistance Grind Rarity

> **Rewrite feel lock.** Use curve: `SkillRanks.md`. Roland tracks: `RolandResistanceTracks.md`. Ordinary clocks: `NormalPersonSkills.md`. Kingdom headcounts: `../World/Society/Population.md`.

## Purpose

How common is it for people to **deliberately hurt themselves** to level resistance skills? Answer: almost nobody does Roland’s grill. Regular adventurers get low resists from living. Intentional self-torture for ranks is a freak habit.

## Curve pressure

| Rank | Cum clean uses | Feel for a resistance |
|---|---:|---|
| L3–L4 | 50–200 | A bad season, a forge job, a drinking habit |
| L5 | 1,000 | Months of *daily* controlled hurt |
| L7 | 20,000 | Years of dedicated exposure |
| L9 | 500,000 | Career obsession |
| Evolve | 2,000,000 | Monster / Roland / cult training |

Semi-diligent adventurers who spend most days off **drinking** climb **Alcohol** (and maybe light **Sleep** / **Pain**) without meaning to. They do **not** spend rest days burning, freezing, shocking, or cutting themselves for another tick on a resist bar.

Combat still hands out incidental **Pain / Sharp / Blunt** uses. That is not the same as sitting down to mutilate on purpose.

## Likelihood (order of magnitude)

Caldris-scale example: **~1M** people, **~9k** guild adventurers (`Population.md`).

| Behavior | Share of adventurers | Rough headcount / million kingdom |
|---|---|---|
| Incidental resists from work / fights / weather / drink (L1–L4 band) | Most who stay active | thousands |
| Short “toughen up” stint (weeks; maybe scrape L5 on one track) | **~1–3%** | **~100–250** |
| Sustained self-harm grind (months+, aiming L6+) | **~0.1–0.3%** | **~10–30** |
| Multi-track Roland-style torture (Heat+Cold+Pain+Poison+…) | **≪0.1%** | **single digits**, often **0–2** known |
| Pushing toward **L9 / evolve** on purpose | effectively unique freaks | **0–1** per kingdom unless a school exists |

Of **world population** (not just guild): people who spend time torturing themselves for skills are roughly **1 in 10,000 to 1 in 100,000** at any serious program, and **1 in a million+** for Roland-depth multi-resist mania. Most of humanity never unlocks those skills past “I work outside / I drink / I got burned once.”

## Who actually does it

- Forge / kitchen / ice-road **jobs** (Heat / Cold without calling it torture)
- A few **elite military / knight / monk** houses with formal iron-body drills
- Rare **obsessive climbers** and cults
- **Not** the tavern-default Silver / Gold crowd

## Story feel

If someone has high **Heat + Cold + Pain + Poison + Acid + Electricity + Blunt + Sharp** from *training*, that reads as terrifying / holy / insane, not “normal diligent adventurer.”

Roland’s early grill and later resist stack are **outlier behavior**. NPCs should react that way when they notice.
