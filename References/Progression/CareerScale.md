# Career Scale

Simple picture for an ordinary human. Not a lock. Packages and pads: `Levels.md`. What a number means on a body: `Training.md`.

```
Displayed = Body + Class + Skills
```

Luck and Charisma are the person. They are not on this clock.

## Body (time only)

Every other attribute. Same number on each.

| Effort | Per year of age | Plateau |
|---|---|---|
| Lazy | +1 | **15** |
| Extremely diligent | +2 | **30** at age 15 |

| Age | Lazy | Extremely diligent |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 5 | 10 |
| 10 | 10 | 20 |
| 15 and after | 15 | 30 |

After the plateau, time adds nothing. Further gains are class and skills only.

## Class and skills (effort × time)

These move only while the person works the trade.

| Effort | Per year of work |
|---|---|
| Lazy | +1 class level, and +1 level on each skill they actually train |
| Extremely diligent | +2 class levels, and +2 levels on each skill they actually train |

A Tier 1 class stops at **25**. A skill name stops at **L9**, then evolves. The old pad stays, and the new form keeps adding, so the pad still matches levels gained.

Splitting the day across many skills slows each one. The rates above are for skills they really train. A skill they ignore stays where it is.

Each class level and each skill level adds **+1** to its own attributes.

| Trade | Class | Class adds | Usual skill | Skill adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Warrior | Str, Vit, End | Sword | Str |
| Archer | Archer | Agi, Dex | Archery | Dex |
| Rogue | Thief | Agi, Dex | Sneak | Agi |
| Blacksmith | Blacksmith | Str, End, Dex | Forging | Str |
| Alchemist | Herbalist | Int, Dex | Herbalism | Int |

Herbalist, Archery, and Forging additions are the working set for this picture. The other rows match `Levels.md`.

## Example (10 years of the trade, age 25)

Body has already plateaued.

**Lazy warrior.** Body 15. Warrior L10. Sword L9 plus the next form at L1 (pad +10 Str).

| | STR | AGI | DEX | VIT | END | INT | WILL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Class | 10 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 10 | 0 | 0 |
| Sword | 10 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Sheet | 35 | 15 | 15 | 25 | 25 | 15 | 15 |

**Extremely diligent warrior.** Body 30. Warrior L20. Sword pad +20 Str.

| | STR | AGI | DEX | VIT | END | INT | WILL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 |
| Class | 20 | 0 | 0 | 20 | 20 | 0 | 0 |
| Sword | 20 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Sheet | 70 | 30 | 30 | 50 | 50 | 30 | 30 |

Same addition for the other trades: body plateau, plus years × effort on that class's attributes, plus years × effort on the skills they trained.
