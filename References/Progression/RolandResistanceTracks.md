**Kinetic naming lock:** do **not** split Piercing Resistance and Slashing Resistance into two skills for Roland’s sheet. **Blunt + Sharp** is the pair. Sharp = pierce **and** slash.

### Why Sharp is one skill (science)

- **Blunt** = large-area **compression** (bruise, crush, fall, hammer). Force spreads; bone and organs fail from pressure and acceleration.
- **Sharp** = **small-contact** trauma. Edge or point puts high pressure on a tiny area so tissue **parts** (slash shears along a line; pierce opens a hole). Same body answers help both: tougher skin / connective tissue, higher tear strength, denser under-layers.
- Slash vs pierce differ in *shape* of the wound, not enough to need two resistance skills for a body-toughness track. Gear can still care about stab vs cut; the **skill** does not.

So scientifically, Blunt vs Sharp is the clean split. Piercing and slashing stay the same skill.

**Concussion / stun:** Goals still want a clearer head-hit track. Until a separate Concussion Resistance is locked, heavy head impacts may share **Blunt** for tissue crush and keep stun / blackout as narrative or a later named skill.

## What these are not

- **Mana Shield / armor / runes:** gear and spells. Resists are body skills.
- **Poison Resistance:** toxins / venoms / status poisons. Acid that only burns flesh is Acid. Some goo may hit both tracks; credit the dominant harm or both if the beat says so.
- **Pain Resistance:** felt pain only. Blunt / Sharp still cut HP; Pain keeps him coherent.
- **Paralysis Resistance** (Goals): binding / paralytic lock beyond Poison. Electricity may cause temporary lock as a side effect; do not merge the skills.
