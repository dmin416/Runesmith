# Kitchen Kit

> Gear lists for camp / home cooking. Wealth locks: `Food.md`. How to make metalware: `KitchenCraft.md` (Food domain). Shop tool ladder: `../World/Science/Invent/MedievalIndustrialization.md`. Metal law: `../World/Materials/Metals.md` (do not park cookware inside metallurgy hubs).

## Gear lists

### Core (5 items)
1. Skillet
2. Pot with lid
3. Chef's knife
4. Cutting board
5. Spoon

### Full minimal kit (12 items)
1. 12 inch skillet (cast iron or carbon steel)
2. 3 qt saucepan with lid
3. Stockpot or Dutch oven (8 qt or larger)
4. Sheet pan
5. Chef's knife
6. Cutting board
7. Large mixing bowl
8. Wooden spoon
9. Tongs
10. Spatula (metal turner)
11. Measuring cups and spoons
12. Colander or fine mesh strainer

### Best upgrades (7 items)
1. Paring knife
2. Instant-read thermometer
3. Whisk
4. Peeler
5. Box grater
6. Kitchen scale
7. Blender or immersion blender
