# Sky Saturation

> **Design loot.** Not street law. Two lanes live here. The science lane prices a batch that is actually being run. The narrative lane lets a scene have a finished aged product or a magical ingredient without opening that ledger.

`CompressedTimeStimulation.md` wins when a beat accounts the speedup. `MagicalCooking.md` wins on kitchen casts. `FoodPreservation.md` wins on Earth making and home safety. `FermentedAging.md` wins on Earth peak times. `AgingMechanisms.md` wins on Earth heat, oak, and barrel levers. Wealth, seasoning, and never-eaten stay in `Food.md`. Storage bags stay in `../Runes/Magic.md`. Metal conversion stays in `../World/Materials/Metals.md`. This file does not set a sky metal ladder.

## Narrative lane

Use this when the scene only needs the food.

- A well-aged wine, spirit, cheese, ham, vinegar, or similar good can appear at its peak. Cellar years, sky weeks, or a bought cask are all fine. Do not price the joules, name the pathway, or explain why it did not spoil.
- A magical ingredient can appear as itself. Mana-fruit, charged grain, an elixir, a sky herb. It does what the scene needs. Using one does not amend the product rule in `CompressedTimeStimulation.md`.
- A storage bag can hold the finished food. The bag law stays in `../Runes/Magic.md`.

If a later beat shows someone making that same food as a worked process, switch to the science lane for that beat only.

## Mana at the peak

Fresh food reaches its peak with a slight amount of mana benefit. That benefit belongs to the peak. Past it, the food overripens or spoils by the science lane.

Preserved foods fundamentally upgrade in extremely mana-saturated environments. Jams, honey, alcohol, and the other salted, dried, sugared, fermented, or cultured keeps are this class. A cellar stay or a mild sky stay ages them along the ordinary paths in `FermentedAging.md`. Extreme saturation is what changes the food in kind.

A lasting mana-fruit, charged grain, or elixir used as a found ingredient stays in the narrative lane. The upgrade above is what extreme saturation does to a preserve that is already stable.

## Science lane

Acceleration speeds whichever process is already winning.

- Intact unripe fruit ripens, then overripens.
- A whole cut in dry moving air dry-ages. Fatty meat goes rancid sooner than lean meat.
- A cured dry root sweetens and toughens its skin. Damp roots sprout or mold.
- Salted, dried, acid, fermented, or cultured food has the wide window. Hard cheese, cured ham, miso, soy sauce, vinegar, and fortified wine in wood keep moving along the paths in `FermentedAging.md`.
- Milk with a culture can become cheese or yogurt. Crushed grapes with yeast can become wine if heat and gas can leave.
- Dry sealed grain stays stable. Damp grain sprouts or molds.
- Sealed is not a preserve by itself. Moist, low-acid, low-oxygen food is the botulism case in `FoodPreservation.md`.
- Eggs, uncultured milk, cut or bruised food, ground meat, organs, fish, shellfish, and exposed cooked leftovers spoil. Monster meat already spoils fast (`Food.md`).
- A living vine still needs real water, light, and carbon dioxide, and its stages in order. Fruit on the stem still peaks and then overripens.
- Spirits in glass have stopped the barrel reactions. In wood they can move, and they can over-extract.
- Sugar can preserve as a heavy syrup. Honey is the ordinary sweetener. Sugar stays the rare feast and medicine (`Food.md`).

Blunt heat is the failure route. Wine tastes cooked, yeast dies, spirits over-oxidize, dough tears, and plants bolt. The priced alternative is per-pathway stimulation in `CompressedTimeStimulation.md`. Totals stay fixed. Rates multiply. Heat, gas, water, and waste arrive sooner. Mana-made mass used as scaffolding leaves when the feed stops.

Mild pace is the cheap band. About tenfold on a 20 L wine sheds its heat on its own. A head of lettuce compressed from about forty-five days into one day is a few million joules of light plus cooling. A tree's century of growth is on the order of a terajoule. Figures live in `CompressedTimeStimulation.md`.

## Idealized sky

Optional assumptions for a science-lane batch that should come out right. They are not street law.

- Only the desired microorganisms are present.
- Mana steers toward the beneficial outcome. Each desired pathway is stimulated on its own. Oxygen is metered. Gas is vented. Heat is removed. Plant stages run in order. The process stops at the peak.

Under those assumptions the batch can finish as the peak version of the ordinary food. Cheese, wine, soy, dry-aged meat, cured roots, and fruit on a living vine can be brought to that peak and held. Barrel chemistry (esters, oxidation, wood extraction) is the small energy bill. A spirit in glass can be aged if wood extraction is driven without a barrel. A living ferment squeezed hard still releases the same heat and gas per gram of food, and the sky has to carry that load. Fish, organs, and bruised fruit still break down by their own enzymes until those pathways are suppressed too.

The finished food is still made of real matter. Sugar, salt, water, oxygen, and wood compounds come from the world. On a sound fresh peak the mana result is the slight benefit above. A fundamental upgrade belongs to preserved food in extreme saturation.
