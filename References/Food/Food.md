## Narrative

Caldris eats better than hard medieval hunger because draft monsters, monster herds and rail move food. Most people still cook at home. Inns are for travelers and adventurers, not daily commoner meals. Cheap inns can serve bland porridge trays as one low option. Year-long gruel-only living is not the rewrite default.

## Detail

### Wealth ladder

**Poor / laborer**
Pottage or porridge most days. Dark bread. Onion, cabbage, beans, potato where grown. Meat a few times a week at best (salt pork, offal, cheap monster cuts). Salt, herbs, onion, dried chili, honey. Weak ale and rough spirits. Filling and dull. Bad winters bite. In farm areas dairy is steady year-round from tamed monster herds.

**Ordinary town / farm**
Same base but steadier. More fresh veg. Poultry or stew meat more often. Pepper rare or absent. Big midday meal, lighter supper. Home cooking is the norm. Town workers near rail lines eat white bread most days because rail moves grain cheaply.

**Craftsman / small merchant**
Meat in rotation. Better bread. Wine or spirits sometimes. Pepper and ginger on nicer nights. Taverns and simple restaurants normal. Clear variety and fat. Coffeehouses are a hangout when coffee is not a far-ship luxury in that city.

**Wealthy merchant / minor noble**
Roasts, game, imported feast spices, sugar on feast nights. Multi-dish meals. Cooks and servants. Status shows in spice and presentation. Coffee is a luxury only when it travels far; near-source cities treat it as ordinary merchant drink.

**High noble / royal**
Same pushed harder: rare game, heavy spice, banquet theater. Out-of-season produce already moves by rail and mage-frozen cold.

### How wealth improves food

1. Calories and reliability (no hungry gap)
2. Meat frequency and cut quality
3. Bread and grain grade
4. Fat, dairy and freshness
5. Spice, sugar, coffee, chocolate
6. Service and spectacle

### Crops and drinks

Potato, tomato, maize, chili, squash, turkey, chocolate and coffee exist where the story needs them. No Earth discovery gate. Plant and process: `CoffeeAndChocolate.md`.

Rough spirits reach the poor. Aged quality spirits mark craftsman / small merchant (tier 3) and up. Distill / liqueur process: `FoodDesign.md` (Steam-era spirits and liqueurs). Tavern prices: `../World/Society/Economy.md`.

Monster meat tastes good. Ordinary tables can eat game-like cuts more often when markets sell dungeon or wild meat cheap. **Never eaten:** goblins (and goblin-kin), ghouls, zombies, liches and other undead. Parts and stones only where those drop. Nobody cooks that. Potency-preserving pans (proposal): `MagicalMeatCookware.md`.

### Inn rule

Cheap inns can serve bland porridge trays as one low option. That is not the only town food and not a year of gruel-only living.
