# Magical Meat Cookware

**Proposal / rough idea. Not law.**

How to cook magical beast meat so potency stays in the dish instead of dispersing. Kitchen metals: `../World/Science/Metallurgy/KitchenCraft.md`. Does not change `../Runes/Energy.md`.

## Best choice: silver-lined copper

Real historical cookware (French kitchens used tin- or silver-lined copper). Fits the setting's mana feel.

- **Heat:** Silver has the highest thermal conductivity of any metal (about 429 W/m·K). Copper is close behind (about 400). Heat spreads evenly with no hot spots. Mana-dense meat cooks through without scorched patches.
- **Food safety:** Silver barely reacts with food. Copper alone leaches into acidic dishes so the silver lining covers the cooking surface while the thick copper body moves the heat.
- **Mana:** For this proposal only, silver does not strip potency the way orichalcum does. The pan and meat form a closed loop where mana circulates and stays in the dish. Does not change `Energy.md`.
- **Drawback:** Silver tarnishes from sulfur in eggs, onions and some meats. Relining costs money and the pan is expensive overall. That makes it a noble, guild-chef or high-end tavern item.

## The grounding problem

A conductive pan only holds mana if the mana has nowhere to go. If it sits on a mana-stone stove or a conductive iron grate the meat's mana could bleed into the heat source. Fixes that fit the world:

- Cook over a plain wood or charcoal fire.
- Put a poor mana conductor between the pan and a mana-stone burner.
- Give the pan a wooden or bone handle so the cook's own body does not pull mana from it.

## Runesmith upgrade

A rune inscribed into the copper base could seal the loop or push mana back into the meat. That makes a potency-preserving pan a believable runesmith product with real market value.

## Alternatives by tier

| Material | Mana behavior | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Cast iron / carbon steel | Neutral: neither drains nor holds | Hunters, commoners, field cooking. Great sear. Seasoned with animal fat. |
| Silver-lined copper | Conducts and retains in a closed loop | Nobles, guild chefs, premium taverns |
| Solid silver | Same as above with better heat | Royal kitchens only. Too soft and costly for daily use. |
| Stoneware / clay | Neutral | Slow braises and stews. Mana loss is low because cooking is gentle. |

## Cooking tip (in-world)

Blood conducts mana. Basting or making a pan sauce with the beast's own blood keeps potency in the dish. Blood sausage or black pudding from edible monster meat would be prized for this reason. **Not goblins, ghouls, zombies, liches or other undead.** Those are never eaten.
