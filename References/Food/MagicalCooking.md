Kitchen outcomes magic can buy if heat, water, time, sorting or kinetics are under control. Strongest pair for hard problems: **accounted stimulation + selective heating**. Fermentation and aging speedups are never free; costs live in `CompressedTimeStimulation.md`.

## Removing heat

- **Instant chilling:** Flash-cooling stops carryover cooking exactly at the target temperature. It also skips the 5 to 60°C danger zone where bacteria multiply, so stocks and leftovers stay safe.
- **Perfect ice cream:** Ice crystal size determines smoothness. Instant freezing produces microscopic crystals, smoother than any commercial machine (same reason liquid-nitrogen ice cream is creamy).
- **Damage-free freezing:** Slow freezing grows large crystals that rupture cell walls (thawed strawberries turn to mush). Instant deep freezing preserves produce and fish almost perfectly.

## Moving water

- **Instant drying:** Jerky, dried fruit, herbs and pasta in seconds.
- **Cold reduction:** Pull water out of a sauce or juice without heat. Fresh flavors that normally cook off (tomato, citrus, herbs) stay bright.
- **Dry surfaces:** Moisture blocks crisp skin and good searing. Drying only the outer layer gives crackling and fast browning.
- **Even brining:** Push salt water evenly through meat; replaces hours or days of brining.

## Speeding up time locally

Not lazy time magic. Practitioner has no time manipulation. Acceleration is stimulation plus full input accounting: `CompressedTimeStimulation.md`. Totals stay fixed; rates × N; cooling, transport, bolstering and pathway balance set the bill.

- **Aging:** Dry-aged beef normally 30 to 60 days. Cheese, wine, whiskey, cured ham and balsamic take months to decades. Price per pathway; heat-only ages taste cooked.
- **Fermentation:** Bread rising, sourdough, kimchi, miso, yogurt, vinegar and soy sauce on demand. Moderate N near the natural ceiling is cheap; high N is industrial (mana stones / runic vats).
- **Braising:** Tough-cut / collagen problem: eight hours of breakdown in a minute. Same divergent-reaction wall as MagicalMeatCookware; blunt heat alone fails.

## Slowing time

- Freshly cut produce, fish and bread that do not go stale or spoil. End of food waste (storage magic, not free infinite meals).

## Sorting molecules

- **Instant clarification:** Crystal-clear consommé and stock without egg rafts or straining.
- **Fat separation:** Pull fat from gravy or stock in one motion. Also butter, ghee or skim milk instantly.
- **Removing unwanted compounds:** Bitterness from greens, excess salt, alcohol from wine while keeping flavor.
- **Detoxifying:** Cassava, ackee, raw kidney beans, fugu and many wild mushrooms. Magic removal would make them safe without careful prep. Does **not** override the never-eaten lock (goblins, undead).

## Selective heating by substance

- **Sugar only:** Caramelize crème brûlée tops or onions without cooking anything else.
- **Fat only:** Render fat caps and pork belly without overcooking the meat underneath (pairs with whole-animal cook in `MagicalMeatCookware.md`).
- **Water only:** Steam food from within (crude Earth analog: microwave).

## Purifying without heat

- Kill pathogens while leaving food raw: raw chicken, pork, milk and eggs become safe (e.g. chicken sashimi without the risk). Still subject to never-eaten rules.

## Kinetic control

- **Emulsions:** Mayonnaise, hollandaise and vinaigrettes that never break.
- **Whipping and kneading:** Cream, meringue and gluten development in seconds.
- **Tenderizing:** Loosen muscle fibers without mashing them.

## Crystal control

- **Chocolate tempering:** Six crystal forms; only Form V gives the glossy snap. Force that form and skip the fussy heat/cool cycle.
- **Candy and fudge:** Sugar crystallization control decides smooth, grainy, hard or chewy.

## Pressure and gas

- **Pressure cooking anywhere:** Beans in minutes; stocks extracted fully.
- **Vacuum infusion:** Lower pressure pulls air from porous food so liquid floods in (syrup/liquor fruit, deep marinades).
- **Oxygen removal:** Cut apples, avocados and potatoes never brown.
- **Carbonation:** Sparkling anything, including fruit.

## Sensing

- Exact internal temperature, ripeness, sugar content or spoilage. A chef with this sense alone would never overcook or serve something unsafe. Overlaps Analyze / Sense ideas in `../Ideas.md`.
