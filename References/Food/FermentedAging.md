# Fermented and Aged Foods and Drinks

> Earth commercial aging reputations. Named wines, regions, and the 1700s bottles are Earth anchors. They are not Caldris goods and they do not rebuild the 361-day year.

`FoodPreservation.md` wins on how these foods are made and on home safety and storage. Where a ham, salami, or pickle time disagrees, that file wins for home work. This file is how long a finished named product is said to keep improving or stay stable. Drink process stays in `FoodDesign.md`. Salt on a common table, sugar as a rare feast or medicine, and honey as the ordinary sweetener stay in `Food.md` and `FoodDesign.md`. A century wine does not move that lock. Earth levers that speed those peaks are in `AgingMechanisms.md`. Any mana speedup is priced in `MagicalCooking.md` and `CompressedTimeStimulation.md`. Neither table sets that cost.

## Indefinite Aging (Keeps Improving for Decades to Centuries)

| Item | Type | Aging Potential |
|---|---|---|
| Madeira | Fortified wine | Centuries. Bottles from the 1700s are still drinkable. Nearly indestructible, even after opening. |
| Tokaji Eszencia / Aszú | Sweet wine | 100 to 200+ years |
| Vintage Port | Fortified wine | 50 to 100+ years |
| Sauternes | Sweet wine | 100+ years |
| Traditional Balsamic Vinegar (Modena/Reggio) | Vinegar | 12 to 25+ years minimum. Some barrel sets have been kept going for centuries. |
| Sherry soleras | Fortified wine | Some soleras date back to the 1700s and are topped up continuously |
| Sheng Pu-erh | Fermented tea | Prized at 30 to 70+ years |
| Mead | Honey wine | Decades. The quality of the honey and its strength matter. |
| Shaoxing Huangjiu | Rice wine | Decades. 20 to 50 year bottlings are sold. |
| Umeboshi (traditional 20% salt) | Pickled plum | Effectively indefinite. Century-old jars have been eaten. |
| Doenjang | Soybean paste | Years to decades in clay onggi pots |

## Indefinite Shelf Life (Stable but Little Improvement After Bottling)

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Distilled spirits (whiskey, cognac, rum, baijiu, brandy) | Indefinite when sealed. Aging stops once bottled. Baijiu in clay jars is the exception. |
| Vinegar (all types) | Indefinite |
| Soy sauce | Years sealed. Darkens and intensifies over time. |
| Fish sauce | Years. Darkens and grows stronger. |
| Dark miso (hatcho, aka) | Years. Traditionally aged 2 to 3+ years. |
| Katsuobushi | Years when kept dry |
| Honey (base for mead) | Indefinite. Not fermented on its own. |

## Long Aging (Years to Decades, Then Peaks and Declines)

| Item | Type | Typical Peak |
|---|---|---|
| Fine red wine (Bordeaux, Barolo, Burgundy, Rioja) | Wine | 10 to 50 years |
| Riesling (German, Alsatian) | Wine | 10 to 40+ years |
| Vintage Champagne | Sparkling wine | 10 to 30+ years |
| Barleywine, imperial stout, Belgian quad | Beer | 5 to 25 years |
| Lambic / gueuze | Sour beer | 10 to 20+ years |
| Koshu (aged sake) | Rice wine | 3 to 20+ years. Most sake is best within a year. |
| Parmigiano Reggiano | Cheese | 24 to 36 months typical, up to 10 years rare |
| Aged Gouda | Cheese | 2 to 6 years |
| Aged Cheddar | Cheese | 1 to 20 years |
| Comté / Gruyère | Cheese | 1 to 4+ years |
| Jamón Ibérico | Dry-cured ham | 3 to 5 years |
| Prosciutto di Parma | Dry-cured ham | 1 to 3 years |
| Mukeunji | Aged kimchi | 1 to 3+ years |
| Gochujang | Chili paste | Years |
| Fermented hot sauce (Tabasco style) | Pepper mash | Mash aged up to 3 years in barrels |
| Preserved lemons | Salt-fermented fruit | 1+ years |

## Top Picks for Indefinite Aging
1. **Madeira**: the longest-lived drink in existence
2. **Traditional balsamic vinegar**: barrels passed down for generations
3. **Tokaji Eszencia**: centuries of potential
4. **Sheng Pu-erh**: the only food item regularly valued higher at 50+ years
5. **Umeboshi**: the most durable fermented food
