# Coffee And Chocolate

Plant, process and social role for Caldris tables. Wealth feel: `Food.md`. No Earth discovery gate: both exist where the story needs them.

## Narrative

Coffee is a highland tropical drink culture that becomes news-and-business hangout fuel. Chocolate is a hot humid lowland crop that starts as elite frothed drink and later becomes solid candy when presses and conching exist. Key stimulant difference: coffee is caffeine-led; chocolate is theobromine-led with some caffeine.

## Detail

### Quick compare

| | Coffee | Chocolate |
|---|---|---|
| Climate | Cool tropical highland for arabica (~1–2 km); robusta lower / hotter | Hot humid lowland, roughly within 20° of equator |
| Key process step | Roast | Ferment, then roast |
| Stimulant | Caffeine (~1–1.5% arabica; ~2–2.7% robusta) | Theobromine + some caffeine |
| Social default | Coffeehouses, talk, trade | Ritual / elite drink → later mass sweet |

### Coffee

**Plant:** Evergreen. Fruit = cherries (red/yellow), usually two seeds. Peaberry = one round seed. Frost-free, steady rain, shade or cloud. First useful crop ~3–4 years. Cherries ripen ~6–9 months after flower.

| Kind | Band | Flavor / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Arabica | Highland | Sweeter, acid, complex. Leaf-rust vulnerable |
| Robusta | Lowland | Bitter, earthy, higher caffeine. Rust tougher |
| Liberica | Lowland | Smoky / fruity. Large beans |

**Process chain:**

1. Harvest ripe (selective) or strip branch (cheaper)
2. Dry (whole cherry sun-dry → fruity body) or wet (pulp, ferment 12–48 h, wash → clean/bright) or honey (skin off, mucilage left)
3. Hull to green bean (keeps ~1 year)
4. Roast **200–230 °C** through first crack. Light = acid; dark = bitter/oily. Stale in weeks after roast
5. Grind and brew: fine boil-in-cup; drip/filter; later pressure espresso; instant and decaf are industrial extras

**Caldris use:** Near-grow cities treat coffee as ordinary merchant drink. Far-ship cities push it up the luxury ladder (`Food.md`). Coffeehouses stay the craftsman/merchant hangout when supply is local enough.

### Chocolate

**Plant:** *Theobroma*-type understory tree. Pods on the trunk. Tiny midges pollinate. Only ~1–3% of flowers set. Pod holds **20–60** seeds in sweet pulp.

| Variety feel | Role |
|---|---|
| Criollo-like | Delicate flavor, disease-prone |
| Forastero-like | Hardy bulk supply |
| Trinitario-like | Hybrid middle |

**Traditional process chain:**

1. Split pods. Heap seeds + pulp
2. Ferment **5–7 days** (kills embryo, builds chocolate flavor)
3. Sun-dry ~1 week
4. Roast
5. Winnow shells
6. Grind to paste (heated stone / mill)
7. Mix with water and spices, froth

**Industrial extras (invent / prestige when needed):** press separates cocoa butter from powder; alkali (“Dutch”) softens flavor; butter + powder + sugar → moldable bar; milk chocolate; long conching for smoothness; temper for shine and snap.

**Caldris use:** Exists as drink and feast spice/sweet where grown or imported. Solid bar tech is not required for early tables. Elite bitter frothed drink is enough until a craft city industrializes cacao.

### Story filters

- Climate mismatch kills farms: arabica wants cool highland; cacao wants hot wet lowland. Do not grow both in the same field without magic greenhouse beats
- Fermentation is mandatory for real chocolate flavor. Skip it and the paste stays wrong
- Green coffee stores. Roasted coffee does not. Chocolate flavor locks in during ferment before roast
- Stimulant scenes: coffee for wakeful talk; chocolate for rich drink / later candy, not the same buzz
