# Mounts

Creatures used as mounts, draft animals, or tamed companions in the story.

### Dragon (egg)
First seen: Chapter 7
Roland incubates a dungeon dragon egg with Agni’s mana. Dragons are noted as intelligent and not easily tamed if hatched.

### Trox
First seen: Chapter 2
Large three-horned draft beast used for plowing and farm work. Ox-like build. Caravan variants include three-tailed three-horned bulls of the same type.

### Horse
First seen: Chapter 18
Common riding mount for knights, nobles, escorts, and travelers. Guild stables rent horses by adventurer rank; slower mounts can be free for high ranks. Premium breeds run far faster than ordinary stock.

### Oversized horse-looking draft monster
First seen: Chapter 56
Larger-than-normal equine-type beasts that pull caravan wagons; tamed monsters rather than barnyard animals.

### Three-tailed, three-horned bull (Trox line)
First seen: Chapter 56
Tamed caravan draft monster with high stamina, stronger than ordinary livestock. Likely the same family as the farm Trox.

### Yellow bird (giant chicken / ostrich-like)
First seen: Chapter 57
Lanky saddle-ridden birds with sharp claws. Used by caravan riders and as street mounts; stables also rent large yellow ostrich-like variants.

### Four-eyed deer
First seen: Chapter 68
Large deer-looking animals with four sets of eyes. Kept in the Albrook market-square stable among other tamed mounts.

### Agni (mustelid line; source listed wolf forms)
First seen: Chapter 92
Roland’s imprinted sacred companion. Rewrite: mustelid, starting as a weasel or similar, not a dog or wolf. Source path was Ruby Wolf → Sunlight Wolf → Solar Wolf; keep sacred-beast treatment and tamer bond, swap species line to mustelid when rewritten.

### Needle Worm (tamed) / Ned
First seen: Chapter 12
Roland’s first **Basic Taming** bind. Core-bearing Floor-2 worm; worn as a green scarf after food offer, Mana Shield stall and Willpower stare-down. Separate path from Agni’s later egg imprint. Named **Ned**. Wild line would become **Needle Moth**; Roland steers him **Needle Worm → Greater Needle Worm → King Worm → Wyrm → Ormr** with launched needles, silk, poison (not powder), and physical strength / resilience. Levels add up (max overall **525**). Full design: `Ned.md`.

### Six-legged lizard
First seen: Chapter 408
Horse-sized or larger lizard used to pull wagons and noble carriages. One pulling a magical Valerian-linked carriage is large enough to devour a horse. Slave wagons also use them.

### Chestnut horse
First seen: Chapter 412
Muscular rentable breed, draft-horse size but built for speed (about 80 km/h). Roland rents one for a short journey near the Institute.

### Dinosaur-like mount
First seen: Chapter 412
Exotic rentable stable mount. Roland sees them available but prefers horses.

### Horned Thunder Liger (Thunderclaw)
First seen: Chapter 434
Professor Ulfine’s named tier-3 tamed companion: a lion–tiger hybrid with lightning. Stands in Roland’s path during their fight.

### Wyvern
First seen: Chapter 435
Dragon-like flyers without forelimbs: powerful wings and a long barbed tail. Valerian Wyvern Riders use them for airborne combat; nobles also tame them for aerial warfare. Wild wyverns appear in dungeons and mountains.

### Doppelganger
First seen: Chapter 439
Hard-to-tame shapeshifter companion of Ulfine. Impersonates people, uses their skills and spells, then hides among dungeon monsters.

### Panther / tiger-type felines
First seen: Chapter 440
Ulfine’s other tamed and summoned feline pack. Varied fur colors; most resemble panthers or tigers.

### Wolven tracking monster
First seen: Chapter 456
Slavers’ tamed pack used as bloodhounds alongside horseback pursuers. Hunt on foot; not ridden.

### Wind elemental horse
First seen: Chapter 479
Green equine beasts with cloud-like manes that embody wind. Unmatched speed; pull Hadley’s high-speed magical carriage.

### Griffin
First seen: Chapter 506
Graham maintains a platoon of griffin riders. Leopold orders one for aerial pursuit; the stable’s griffins are later found drugged asleep.

### Warhorse
First seen: Chapter 518
Armored cavalry steed used in battle (e.g. Alphonse, prosthetic knights). Some wear armor thick enough to shrug off artillery fire.

### White horse
First seen: Chapter 540
Ceremonial and noble mount. Arthur rides one; snow-white teams also pull noble carriages into Isgard. Julius arrives on a white horse in golden armor.

### Black stallion
First seen: Chapter 591
Roland’s parade escort mount ahead of Arthur’s carriage into Isgard, fitted with heavy runic barding.

### Gem-forehead magical horse
First seen: Chapter 597
Oversized magical mounts used by Tybalt’s personal riders. Gems sit in their foreheads; each is comparable in strength to a tier-2 class holder.

### Massive tiger
First seen: Chapter 679
City cart-puller among other large draft beasts.

### Salamander (draft)
First seen: Chapter 679
Tamed salamander used to pull city carts (distinct from wild dungeon salamanders).
