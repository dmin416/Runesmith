# Spell To Craft Gate

> **Rewrite lock.** Craft families: `Magic.md`. Segment pacing: `RuneCauseEffect.md`. Economy: `ScrollEconomy.md`.

## Two scroll families (do not mix)

These are **different products**. Same paper shape does not make them the same craft.

| Family | What it is | Fuel / who | Craft skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Magic scroll** | Temporary magical pathway: written chant + pathways that guide the user's own mana. Disposable spell imprint. | User must have mana, **put their mana in**, and **chant**. Writer caps power. | Mana Scribing / magic-scroll craft |
| **Runic scroll** | A **rune** laid on a scroll host. Rules machine, not a written spell. | **Anyone** can use. Common: user pays mana or stamina. **Special:** can be **preloaded with mana** and activated without the user casting a spell. | Rune Scribing / runecraft |

A magic scroll is **not** a weak runic scroll. A runic scroll is **not** a magic scroll with symbols. Gate below treats them as separate outputs.

Full family law: `Magic.md`.

## Purpose

Stop Roland's kit from becoming **self-sustaining** off spells he already knows. Cast knowledge feeds **magic scrolls**. **Runes** (including runic scrolls and metal hosts) stay behind samples, trade, and hard finds.

## Core gate

For any spell Roland can cast himself:

| Output | Allowed? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Magic scroll** | Yes | He already understands the cast. He can scribe that spell form as a magic scroll. Disposable ammo / sales / share a known form. |
| **Runic scroll** | **No** from the spell alone | A runic scroll is a rune on paper. Knowing the spell is not a rune pattern. |
| **Other rune hosts** (metal, gear inlay, boards) | **No** from the spell alone | Same: need a working **rune** sample (or reverse-engineer of someone else's rune). |

**One line:** castable spell → **magic scroll** OK; castable spell → **rune** (runic scroll or otherwise) **not** OK.

## Intention extras stay scarce

Body casts can carry **caster intention** that a rune does not earn for free.

Examples:

- Mana Shield or Orb of Light set to **follow** a chosen person
- An effect that **reads the caster's intent** and locks onto a specific target

Those extras are too useful to hand him as soon as he can cast the base spell. Putting the spell on a **magic scroll** can keep writer-style intention within that scroll family's limits. A **rune** that does the same job (**attach / seek / sense / follow**) can exist in the world, but Roland does not unlock it by casting Light or Shield with follow intent, and he does not get it by writing a magic scroll of that spell. He needs a **real rune sample** (shop seeker, tracking gear, armor scan, patronage board, loot) and even then it should stay **expensive, rare, or plot-earned**.

Spell cast with intention ≠ rune segment library unlock.  
Magic scroll of that spell ≠ runic scroll of that effect.

## What this does not block

- Magic scrolls of spells he knows (including for his own ammo pouch)
- Learning **runes** from external rune samples and grafting segments he has actually seen (`RuneCauseEffect.md`)
- Making **runic scrolls** or other rune hosts once he holds a working rune pattern from a sample
- Buying or commissioning seeker / follow **runes** when the story earns the cost and access
- Affinity-blind elemental rune craft once he has a rune sample (`ManaMaterials.md`)

## Story feel

Early power growth should feel like: cast what he knows, scribe **magic scrolls**, reverse-engineer **other people's runes** (including runic scrolls) when he can get them. Not: every spell he learns becomes a runic scroll or gear stamp the next afternoon.
