## How they look

### Surface vs regular enchantment

- **Ch 12:** Sahildr's hammer has a **strange symbol engraved** into the metallic head.
- **Ch 13:** Regular enchantments are **not** surface-engraved. They are **suffused through** the item. Spatial bag: no rune traces visible but mana moves under the surface. Only **runic** pieces pull Roland's pathway sense.
- **Ch 14:** Watcher's rapier has runes **engraved**; slightly more intricate than Sahildr's hammer rune.
- **Ch 39:** Runic armor shows **intricate inscriptions** that mark it as runic.
- **Ch 6 (book talk):** Runes = symbol language. Enchantments = word-like language.

### Symbols and size

- **Ch 15 (detonation tip):** Markings run handle→tip (heavier at tip). Surface symbols only; pathway structure runs **deeper** through the blade (electrical diagram / flow puzzle). Fairly **large in scope** and easy to examine.
- **Ch 16 (trial, rewrite):** Fire Orb is a **simpler linear chain** than Detonation. Naked-eye work looks like writing **five large letters**; ink under them links pathways between **five main regions** (mana collector → fire core → shape fireball → constrain so it does not explode on form → give velocity). Detonation schematic was on a large sheet; the sword original ran inch-thick to a point; the scroll must **fit** the parchment. After infusion, extra pathways sink and cannot be seen. Success glow: crimson wisps compress into a **fist-sized** hot orb. Source said **three** symbols / components, maximal size rule and 1:1 compress; rewrite does not restate max-size law on-page.
- **Ch 16 Source (kept for scrape):** Naked eye **three** symbols (fire / orb / control). Hidden pathways vanish after ink dries. Runes have a **maximal size**; too big and they fail. Success orb ~marble-sized.
- **Ch 19:** Components sit **buried deeply into** the runic symbols.
- **Ch 49:** Chisel work can look almost finished; only a Runesmith notices **missing traces**.

## How Roland makes them

### Paper / scroll (Scribe path)

1. **Materials:** Magical ink + parchment that retains magic (often **monster skin**). Quill.
2. **Mana into tip:** Focus mana onto the **quill tip** and push it into the ink so the Scribe's mana can be transcribed.
3. **Draw pathways:** Guide ink while laying pathways between stage regions. Looks like writing large letters; ink is building the working pattern under the symbol.
4. **Fit the scroll:** Large schematic / sword-scale original must land as a working pattern on the parchment (Source: hard max size + 1:1 compress ban; rewrite Ch 16 stresses fit + simpler linear Fire Orb vs Detonation; no max-size hard rule on-page).
5. **Debugger after:** Overlay red/green/blue on the finished sheet; redraw toward all-green for Highest schematics (Ch 15 Calligraphy redraw ~**1 hour**; Ch 17 memory + Debugger overlay).
6. **Ch 16 trial extras:** Do **not** mana-hand-pour a finished overlay onto thick beast skin mid-trial (cascade drain). Continuous large→fine pass; second attempt after committing the ideal (drown red in memory).
7. **Ch 21:** Quill on yellow scroll; symbols appear slowly and connect into one runic spell.

### Cross-cutting Source upgrades (ladder)

1. **Sight:** Debugger red/green/blue → purple-eyed zoom into **code** inside components → True Runic Sight / 3D particle pathways → can see runes in spells, crystal, air, concealed gear (overreach = migraine).
2. **Paper:** Quill + magical ink + monster parchment → field wood-stick + raw skin → metal pen with ink channels; mind/voice scribing variants exist later.
3. **Metal:** Heat + glowing hammer / blue fingers / patterned hammer face → hammer+chisel mana bolt → Ethereal Pathways bridging parts → Expert 3D stacking + Miniaturisation; optional metal wire buses vs ethereal.
4. **Repair / alter:** Hammer reconstruct → Rune Mending (no hammer, grade decay) → Runic Restructuring (instant reshape to known diagram) → rune transmutation (cheap, coarse).
5. **Hide:** Rune concealment skill for armor/weapons.
6. **Roles:** Runesmith ≈ outer / physical components; rune mage ≈ internal spell structure; Soulsmith = separate soul-color path.
