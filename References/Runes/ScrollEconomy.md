## Word scroll cost engine (Lock)

Coin pegs: **1 LC = 10 SC**, **1 SS = 10 LC**, **1 LS = 100 LC**, **1 SG = 1,000 LC**, **1 LG = 10,000 LC**, **1 RG = 100,000 LC**. Peg: **5 LC = 1d**.

```
craft_mana = 0.5 × cast_cost
labor_I = shop_I × 0.85 − mats          // from locked Intermediate shelf
labor = labor_I × (craft / craft_I)     // scales when designed cast differs
shop = (mats + labor) ÷ 0.85
```

| Constant | Lock |
|---|---|
| T1 maker | Mage L25 + Mana Scribe L25 on body **15/15** → INT/Will **65/65**, MP **910** |
| T2 maker | + Elemental Mage L25 → INT/Will **115/115**, MP **1,610** |
| `day_mana` | **= MP** (T1 **910**, T2 **1,610**) |
| Craft law | **half** designed cast cost (`MagicScrollMaking.md`) |
| Scroll **power** | Designed; cast cost scales; craft = half cast |
| Shop cut (fair) | **15%** (÷ **0.85**). Auction first-timer **25%** |
| Mana Arrow / Fire Arrow normal cast | **50** → craft **25** |
| Fireball normal cast | **400** → craft **200** |

**Scrolls/day (pool-bound, normal Intermediate):** T1 Mana Arrow **≈36.4**/day (910÷25). T2 Fire Arrow **≈64.4**/day (1610÷25). Fireball **≈8.1**/day (1610÷200). Stronger designed casts burn more pool per scroll.

## Development path (how to develop the craft)

### Phase A — Understand both (Ch 20–21)

1. Buy blanks. Make **one** regular Mana Arrow (fast XP, feel commodity).
2. Make **one** runic Fire Orb (slow, Diagnosis red).
3. Learn: Diagnosis blank on regulars; affinity free on runic; markets differ.
4. **Do not** plan income on regulars without a shop contract (cartel).

### Phase B — Runic product-market fit (Ch 22–23)

1. Buy a cheap runic Fire Arrow → perfect schematic (**2×** XP).
2. Grind Common Fire Arrow to **High/Highest only**.
3. Sell via **auction** to mana-capable buyers (bundles of 10).
4. Cover story: servant of a runesmith. Hide child + class.
5. Skill focus: **Rune Scribing** (craft cost/time), **Rune Mastery** later via combat use, Drawing/Calligraphy as needed.

### Phase C — Stabilize (Ch 25–27)

1. Contract or workshop: mats, quota, min price, identity shield.
2. Compaction: envelope/card size for carry; accept **less amp headroom**.
3. Catalog grafts (elemental arrows) without being an elemental mage.
4. Optional: keep Mana Scribing for personal panic regulars; not the brand.

### Phase D — Beyond scrolls

1. Scroll → plate / paddle / weapon (same rune law, reusable).
2. Mines, grenades, multi-scroll arrays (engineering, not Scribe commodity).
3. Prepaid runic line (P9) only if you want a warrior-facing runic SKU; that is a **new product**, not the default.

### What not to develop

- Regular scroll brand as Roland’s main arc (no Diagnosis, affinity wall, cartel).
- Low-quality runic volume (dust economics).
- Teaching that word / regular scrolls work for zero-mana users (they do not; that is the runic job).

### Scroll activation (`Magic.md` / `Ideas.md`)

- **Word / magic scrolls:** need mana; read aloud; not for no-mana users.
- **Runic scrolls:** anyone with energy. Activation costs **mana or stamina**.
- Safe path can run that energy through a **filtration sequence**. Tax is real paid energy (MP or SP).
- **Rune Mastery** lets the holder **bypass filtration** and feed energy straight into the pattern (−10%/level activation, Lesser on Basic form).

### Who paid the shot (`RuneSetup.md`)

## How to price without inventing LC/mana

1. **Empty runic:** blank + ink + labor time + **setup mana burden** (pool/time at pour rate ~20 mana/min in RuneSetup examples) + quality/rarity. Compare to shelf multiples already in story.
2. **Preloaded runic:** empty price + the **extra pour** of `1.31 × activation` (and time). Do not convert that pour through a fake LC/mana. The premium is “crafter spent an extra activation’s worth of pool and time.”
3. **Stones:** pay **stone street price** when a job actually consumes or sockets a stone. Do not divide rice **20 LC** by **19 mana** and call that the world’s mana wage.
4. **Amp (runic):** user feeds extra **mana or stamina** into empty (or remaining headroom). Rank-capped (`RuneSystem.md`). Compression cuts overload headroom (Ch 27).
