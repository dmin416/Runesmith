> **Rewrite direction (locked intent).** Narrative tier / power is a **mixture**, not joules alone. Machine ranks and quality seating stay in `RuneSystem.md` / `Energy.md`. This file owns **how many concepts the runic language can name**, and how composition grows with rank.

Do **not** rename ranks to Uncommon / Rare / Epic. Live names stay **Lesser → Common → Greater → Grand → Legendary**, each with **Lowest → Highest** quality.

## World lexicon size (soft counts, ~4× per rank)

About **100–150** base concepts cover most everyday magic **if runes combine**. Without combination the working set would need roughly **800–1,000** isolated glyphs. Higher ranks add precision and domain coverage more than brand-new capability once combination exists.

| Rank | New named glyphs (order-of-magnitude) | Cumulative | Earth-ish compare | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lesser** | **~10** | ~10 | Mnemonic core | Elemental / body primes: fire, water, earth, air, life, death, light, dark, self, other (names soft; Fire Orb / Impact / Detonation sit here as machines built from primes) |
| **Common** | **~30** | ~40 | Elder Futhark | Concrete forces and kit nouns: strength, ice, journey, gift, protection, harvest, arrow-body, harden… |
| **Greater** | **~120** | ~160 | Toki Pona / semantic primes | Everyday threshold: actions, quantities, qualities, negation, time, cause; craft can **say** almost any street idea |
| **Grand** | **~500** | ~660 | Working cuneiform / hieroglyph sets | Trades, social roles, specialized domains; precision and efficiency |
| **Legendary** | **~3,000** | ~3,660 | Chinese functional literacy | Near-complete expression including abstract / technical; myth and politics |

Exact glyph lists for Common+ are **not** filled yet. Do not invent Uncommon/Rare catalogs. Grow `Runes.md` as samples appear.

**Soft rule:** Tier-up in lexicon is not required to craft a rank’s machine. A Common Fire Arrow can exist while the world only widely teaches ~40 named glyphs; rare masters hold deeper lists.

## Look (visual lock)

**Simple runes** (Lesser, early Common) read like a **control block diagram** — grossly simplified on the page, but the gist is clear boxes and arrows, not a single mystic letter.

Typical feel (not required labels in prose):

- Reference / intent in → filter or intake → sum / compare (optional) → controller → actuator → process → output
- Optional **feedback** loop (sensor back to the sum) once the machine needs to hold, aim, or correct
- One main trunk left-to-right; few modules; you can name each stage

**Complex runes** (hard Common couples, Greater+, Grand arrays) look like a **dense industrial schematic** — modules, housings, thick grey conduits, fine orange pathway traces, junctions, stacked sub-units, labels everywhere. Same grammar as the simple diagram, exploded into a board you reverse-engineer region by region.

| Simple | Complex |
|---|---|
| Few clear blocks on one chain | Clustered modules + buses |
| Easy to sketch as boxes and arrows | Looks like plumbing + circuit board + machine at once |
| Diagnosis can walk stage by stage | Diagnosis shows overlays, crossings, over/under paths |
| Fire Orb four-region chain sits here | Armor OS / forge hearts / vault arrays sit here |

Surface ink may stay neat calligraphy; the **working pattern** under magical vision / Diagnosis is what looks like the diagram. Beauty at high quality can make the overlay itself into art without losing the machine read.

## How higher ranks get meaning (preferred mix)

Locked preference: **radicals + compounds** and **combination depth**, not five disconnected dictionaries.

### 1. Radical and compound

- Lesser–Common hold most **base glyphs** (on the order of **~40–200**, Kangxi-adjacent feel).
- Greater+ are mostly **compounds** read through lower components (meaning part + structure part), not thousands of unrelated new shapes.
- Learning cost stays human: a Grand inscription is hard because of **layout and coupling**, not because every stroke is a unique logogram.

### 2. Combination depth (matches geometry ladder)

| Rank | Stack / coupling feel |
|---|---|
| Lesser | **1** linear chain (no free stack) |
| Common | **2–3** coupled modules (shared arrow body + element, etc.) |
| Greater | **3–5** in one working machine (3D / ethereal allowed) |
| Grand | **Arrays** of greater machines |
| Legendary | Effectively **unlimited** composition inside one mythic work |

Power from composition is why ~100–150 bases can still reach “say anything” once Common/Greater coupling is real.

### 3. Grammatical function (optional color, not a second ladder)

Use for story beats if useful: early ranks **name and force**; mid ranks **modify and relate** (not, because, all); high ranks **condition and abstract**. Do not hard-gate “no verbs until Common” unless a chapter locks it.
