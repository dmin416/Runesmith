## Regular spell scrolls (word / mana scribing)

### Craft

- Skill: **Basic Mana Scribing** (Mana Scribe / Runic Mana Scribe trial grants it).
- Method: spell out the **incantation** in the correct language while focusing mana into quill and ink. **No** runic pathway drawing.
- Materials: magical paper and/or magical hide blanks; magic ink (`Paper.md`).
- Grade: **not** “how well drawn” like runes. **How much power was injected during creation.** Shop uses a device; Lowest → Highest.
- Roland (Ch 13 parting thought / Ch 20): more mana infused while writing → stronger spell. His high INT / pool should grade high if he scribes Mana Arrow / Bolt.
- Elemental example: Fire Arrow (T2) maker must be advanced Scribe **and** fire mage. Affinity matters for **making** elemental word scrolls.

### Use

- One-time item. Adventurers buy for occasions where casting without being a mage (or without knowing that spell) matters.
- Ch 20 rewrite: “*enabled those without magic to cast it either as a curiosity or as a life-saving measure*” (and not cost-effective vs goblin kills unless life-save / you make them).
- Identify on fresh L1 scroll: **name only** until scribing skill rises.

### Limits

- Diagnosis cannot see structure → cannot Diagnosis-upgrade regulars.
- Strength fixed at craft; no Source beat saying the user dumps extra mana to amp a regular scroll.
- Independent profit hard: mats priced so only intermediate+ grades pay (`Economy.md` / Ch 21 shop cartel).

## Runic spell scrolls

### Craft

- Skills: **Basic Rune Scribing** (draw rune to scroll); **Basic Rune Mastery** (understand / cheaper **activation** later). Class: Runic Mana Scribe eases learning (rewrite: no cost term); shop world often says **Runesmith**.
- Method: layout pathway regions (Fire Orb trial: five linear stages). Mana into ink while drawing. Pathways sink into the scroll when infused.
- Time: much slower than word scrolls. Lesser Fire Orb practice ~45 min; Common Fire Arrow much harder / ~1 hour class with mana left for a second at mid Scribe; compaction later **5–6**/day.
- **Affinity:** runic Fire Orb activates fine at **0%** fire. Runic scrolls do not need the maker’s element.
- Setup mana is large (RuneSetup math; Common Fire Arrow draining). Blank + ink cheaper than finished shelf price.

### Use

- One-shot (or mine / grenade cores that spend the scroll).
- **Anyone can activate** (Ch 21 explicit reason they ignore affinity).
- **Amplification:** user may feed **extra mana** into the scroll; output rises to a limit set by **rune rank** (and quality / headroom). Percival / Source auction explanation: this is the big difference vs regular scrolls.
- Tiny compressed scrolls: power holds but **overload headroom drops** (Ch 27). Card-size needs only a little mana to start (farmer boy beat).
- **Rune Mastery:** −10% MP to **activate** runes / runic scrolls per level (cap −90%). Levels by **combat use** of runic scrolls / gear, not sofa casts.
- Mana Shield–type runic scrolls, elemental arrow packs, detonation mines, grenade inserts (Compression + Ethereal Pathways) appear later as the same family.

### Market

- Harder / slower → expensive. Shelf often dusty because bulk regulars are “good enough.”
- Roland’s High / Highest Common Fire Arrow brand undercuts “support caster” needs vs regular prices once quality is real (`Economy.md` Ch 27 note).

## Prefill / who pays mana (clarified)

### At craft (both)

- Maker spends mana **into the writing / pathways**. Blanks are not pre-charged spell batteries sitting on the shelf with a full cast already paid for the user in the early canon read.
- Regular: that pour **is** the spell power (grade).
- Runic: that pour **builds the machine**; activation is usually a **separate** user (or stone) payment (`RuneSetup` P1 default).

### At use

| | Regular | Runic |
|---|---|---|
| Start cost | Softly “use the scroll”; non-mages can. Exact MP not pinned early. | User mana through the pattern (filtration tax in energy notes). Tiny cards need very little. |
| Extra mana | Not a listed amp channel. | **Yes** → amplify up to rank ceiling / overload skills. |
| Prepaid craft (P9/P10) | Research options: crafter pre-pays activation or filtration so non-mages pay less. Marked **differs from canon default**. | Same table. Do not treat as locked for Edelgard arc. |

**Bottom line:** neither type is “fully prefilled so the user pays zero and cannot add more” in locked early story. Regulars are **craft-powered one-shots**. Runics are **user-powered machines** you can **overfeed**.

## Hard split (lock intent)

| | **Spell scroll** (word / Mana Scribing) | **Runic scroll** (Rune Scribing) |

| **What it is** | A **frozen mage cast** written as an incantation. | A **disposable rune machine**. Pathways. |
| **Laws** | Spell / enchantment language. Diagnosis blank. | Rune law. Diagnosis works. |

**One sentence:** word scrolls are **mage cross-affinity patches**. Runic scrolls are **anyone-with-energy panic / tool shots**.

### Market feel

- Pure-mana mage (0% fire) buys **word Fire Arrow** if he has MP and wants a fixed cast he cannot learn.
- Warrior with fat SP and thin MP buys **runic** and pays stamina.
- Civilian with tiny pools buys cheap runic utility if they can spare the SP hit.
- Dusty expensive Low runics still fail if price ≫ word scroll and the buyer is a mage who would rather spend MP on a word scroll.

### Conflict to fix later

`Ideas.md` Rune Mastery blurb (“activation still costs mana”; filtration for non-mages) and the older Temp one-liners. Align them to: word = mana-only; runic = mana or stamina; filtration may still apply to the safe path but the paid pool can be SP.

## Economy redesign

Coin pegs stay: **10 LC = 1 SS**, **10 SS = 1 LS**, **10 LS = 1 SG**. Blank lock: rough magical hide **~4–5 LC**. Finished prices below are **proposal bands** for Ch 20+ rewrite (replace mushy “several×”).

### Cost stack

**Spell scroll cost**

```
price ≈ blank + ink + labor_time + craft_mana_opportunity + grade_premium + shop_cut
```

- Fast craft (minutes). Academy labor cheap → commodity.
- Craft mana is the big variable: Intermediate+ needs a real pour → pool/regen limits daily volume.
- Cartel prices mats so **Lowest/Low** barely break even; profit starts **Intermediate**.

**Runic scroll cost**

```
price ≈ blank + ink + long_labor + setup_mana + skill_rarity + quality_premium + amp_headroom_premium + shop_cut
```

- Hours not minutes at early skill. Runesmith scarcity.
- Setup mana high but **repeatable schematics** amortize design cost (Roland’s Diagnosis cheat).
- Quality steps (Low → Highest) are the sellable ladder; rank (Lesser → Common) is a tech jump.

## Development path (how to develop the craft)

### Phase A — Understand both (Ch 20–21)

1. Buy blanks. Make **one** regular Mana Arrow (fast XP, feel commodity).
2. Make **one** runic Fire Orb (slow, Diagnosis red).
3. Learn: Diagnosis blank on regulars; affinity free on runic; markets differ.
4. **Do not** plan income on regulars without a shop contract (cartel).

### Phase B — Runic product-market fit (Ch 22–23)

1. Buy a cheap runic Fire Arrow → perfect schematic (**2×** XP).
2. Grind Common Fire Arrow to **High/Highest only**.
3. Sell via **auction** to mana-capable buyers (bundles of 10).
4. Cover story: servant of a runesmith. Hide child + class.
5. Skill focus: **Rune Scribing** (craft cost/time), **Rune Mastery** later via combat use, Drawing/Calligraphy as needed.

### Phase C — Stabilize (Ch 25–27)

1. Contract or workshop: mats, quota, min price, identity shield.
2. Compaction: envelope/card size for carry; accept **less amp headroom**.
3. Catalog grafts (elemental arrows) without being an elemental mage.
4. Optional: keep Mana Scribing for personal panic regulars; not the brand.

### Phase D — Beyond scrolls

1. Scroll → plate / paddle / weapon (same rune law, reusable).
2. Mines, grenades, multi-scroll arrays (engineering, not Scribe commodity).
3. Prepaid runic line (P9) only if you want a warrior-facing runic SKU; that is a **new product**, not the default.

### What not to develop

- Regular scroll brand as Roland’s main arc (no Diagnosis, affinity wall, cartel).
- Low-quality runic volume (dust economics).
- Teaching that runics replace regulars for zero-mana users.

# Two runic SKUs (existing system only)

Sources: `../Ideas.md` (scroll filtration / Mastery bypass), `RuneSetup.md` (P1 default vs P9 prepaid, setup, 1.31 store), `RuneSystem.md` / `../World/Science/Energy/ManaCast.md` (**1 mana ≈ 10 J**), `../World/Science/Energy/ManaStones.md` + `../World/Society/Economy.md` (stone capacity and street prices).

## What was already locked

### Mana as energy (not coin)

- **1 mana ≈ 10 J** paid. Cast / path / ceilings use that. There is **no** locked LC-per-mana tariff in the project files.

### Stones (commodity, specialist use)

- Street prices locked (rice **2 SS**, tiny **1 SS**, leader **10 SS**). Capacity **~1 mana/mm³**. Rates in `ManaStones.md`.
- Like gold: valuable to trade. Usable by **craftsmen** and people with **runic weapons / mana tools** that can draw them. Not a public drink-mana item for every warrior.

### Scroll activation (`Ideas.md`)

- Scrolls stay **anyone can use**.
- Activation **still costs mana**.
- Safe path runs the user’s mana through a **filtration sequence** so a non-mage does not wreck the pattern. That safety tax is **real MP**.
- **Rune Mastery** lets the holder **bypass filtration** and feed mana straight into the pattern (−10%/level activation, Lesser on Basic form).

### Who paid the shot (`RuneSetup.md`)

| Model | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 Spent structure (default)** | Setup builds pathways and is gone. **User pays activation** every cast (filtration on scrolls). | Canon default |
| **P9 Prepaid scroll** | Crafter also pours the activation charge. User pays only start cost. Total ≈ setup + **1.31 × activation**. | Optional variant; marked as differing from default user-pays |
| **P2 / store** | Optional reservoir: **1.31 mana spent per 1 mana stored** | Used when you actually preload |

So the two runic SKUs are not a new invention:

| SKU | RuneSetup | User pays |
|---|---|---|
| **Empty** (default) | P1 | Filtered activation (+ amp up to rank ceiling) |
| **Preloaded** | P9 (+ store tax 1.31 on the charge) | Start / trigger; amp only if headroom left and they add more |

### Price tracking that already exists

- `RuneSetup.md`: **scroll price can track setup mana** (example note: runic Fire Arrow **6–7×** a regular scroll). Skill S cuts setup (L9 = 10% of L0).
- Shop / auction coin bands: `Economy.md` / `Paper.md` / Ch 20–27 beats. Those are **item prices**, not a mana exchange rate.
- Regular (word) scrolls: grade from **power injected at craft** (separate spell-law product; Diagnosis blank).

## How to price without inventing LC/mana

1. **Empty runic:** blank + ink + labor time + **setup mana burden** (pool/time at pour rate ~20 mana/min in RuneSetup examples) + quality/rarity. Compare to shelf multiples already in story.
2. **Preloaded runic:** empty price + the **extra pour** of `1.31 × activation` (and time). Do not convert that pour through a fake LC/mana. The premium is “crafter spent an extra activation’s worth of pool and time.”
3. **Stones:** pay **stone street price** when a job actually consumes or sockets a stone. Do not divide rice **20 LC** by **19 mana** and call that the world’s mana wage.
4. **Amp (runic):** user feeds extra **mana or stamina** into empty (or remaining headroom). Rank-capped (`RuneSystem.md`). Compression cuts overload headroom (Ch 27).
