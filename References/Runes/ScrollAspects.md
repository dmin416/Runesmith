# Scroll Quality vs Rune Quality (Lock)

> **Do not conflate.** Scroll device grade and rune quality are different measures. Same five grade names (Lowest → Highest) do not make them the same dial.
>
> **Craft / durability / ink / power law:** `MagicScrollMaking.md` (definitive).

## Rune quality

**What it is:** how perfect the rune is (pathway seating, form fidelity).

**What it does:** sets **efficiency** on the rune / item path: `η_cond` in `Energy.md` and the activation **and runic craft-pay** ladders in `MagicScrollMaking.md` (Lowest **20%** → Highest **100%** in 20% blocks). Clean craft pay = `(cast / η_cond) × S_scribing`. Botches can burn past Lowest (Ch 16 cascade ~**4%** effective). Good→bad band: `MagicScrollMaking.md` **Runic scroll craft: paid cost**.

**Skill / XP weight (locked):** rune quality also multiplies clean skill-use credit (and can weight craft XP): Lowest **1** · Low **2** · Intermediate **4** · High **8** · Highest **16**. Same ladder for other graded actions. `../Progression/SkillRanks.md`.

**What it is not:** spell tier / rune rank. Not magic-scroll attacking strength. Not host-metal "feel."

## Scroll quality (magic scrolls)

**What it is:** a device-checked grade of **designed attacking value** / cast cost band (Lowest → Highest on shop tags).

**What it is not:** rune perfection / `η_cond`. Not seating-inefficiency waste.

**Craft:** `paid = cast_cost × S_mana_scribing` (`MagicScrollMaking.md`). Stronger designed scrolls cost more to cast and the same list to make before skill.

**Power:** materials lock in `MagicScrollMaking.md` § Scroll power from materials. **Mostly narrative.** Blood **quality + amount** gate how large a designed spell the ink will seat (peasant/no-mana → trash/fail; Roland quality = his **max MP**, now **2414**; monsters narrative). **Hide hold** caps amp (cheap Trox/boar ~**200**). Activator scales inside the ceiling: `μ(INT)` on mana, physical skills on stamina. Mastery / η cut **payment**. No full hide / blood law book.

## Paper / blank

Does **not** have to be magical. Magic stock: harder focus to imbue (latent mana), multi-use until mana-threshold burn. Mundane: easier imbue, single use on cast. Full law: `MagicScrollMaking.md`. Price ladder: `../World/Science/Invent/Paper.md`.

## Forbidden

- Do not treat scroll quality as rune quality or as `η_cond`.
- Do not treat rune quality as magic-scroll attacking strength.
- Do not use bare INT/Will **15** as a classed scribe.
- Do not use `spell × I` or INT-only power for magic-scroll craft.
- Do not invent LC-per-mana tariffs.
