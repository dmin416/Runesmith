## Regular spell scrolls (word / mana scribing)

### Craft

- Skill: **Basic Mana Scribing** (Mana Scribe / Runic Mana Scribe trial grants it).
- Method: spell out the **incantation** in the correct language while focusing mana into quill and ink. **No** runic pathway drawing.
- Materials: blank need **not** be magical. Mundane: easier imbue, burns after one cast. Magic stock: harder focus, **2–5** uses until threshold. Magic ink preferred for channel capacity (`../World/Science/Invent/Paper.md`, `MagicScrollMaking.md`).
- **Scroll quality:** Lowest → Highest designed attacking value. Craft: full cast × Mana Scribing S. Power: designed. T1 Mage+Scribe / T2+ mage. `ScrollEconomy.md`, `MagicScrollMaking.md`.
- **Rune quality:** not this product. Word scrolls have no `η_cond` path.
- Elemental example: Fire Arrow (T2) maker needs **T2 fire Mage** + Scribe (`MagicScrollMaking.md`).

### Use

- One-time item. Buyers are people **with mana** who need a spell / affinity they do not have (`Magic.md`).
- Old Ch 20 line about “those without magic” is scrape residue. Live lock: word scrolls need mana and reading aloud.
- Identify on fresh L1 scroll: **name only** until scribing skill rises.

### Limits

- Diagnosis cannot see structure → cannot Diagnosis-upgrade regulars.
- Strength fixed at the designed cast; user pays that cast cost when reading.
- Independent profit hard: mats priced so only intermediate+ grades pay (`Economy.md` / Ch 21 shop cartel).

## Runic spell scrolls

### Craft

- Skills: **Basic Rune Scribing** (draw rune to scroll); **Basic Rune Mastery** (understand / cheaper **activation** later). Class: Runic Mana Scribe eases learning (rewrite: no cost term); shop world often says **Runesmith**.
- Method: layout pathway regions (Fire Orb trial: four linear stages). Mana into ink while drawing. Pathways sink into the scroll when infused.
- Time: much slower than word scrolls. Lesser Fire Orb practice ~45 min; Common Fire Arrow much harder / ~1 hour class with mana left for a second at mid Scribe; compaction later **5–6**/day.
- **Affinity:** runic Fire Orb activates fine at **0%** fire. Runic scrolls do not need the maker’s element.
- Setup mana is large (RuneSetup math; Common Fire Arrow draining). Blank + ink cheaper than finished shelf price.

### Use

- One-shot (or mine / grenade cores that spend the scroll).
- **Anyone can activate** (Ch 21 explicit reason they ignore affinity).
- **Amplification:** user may feed extra **energy** (mana or stamina) into the scroll; output rises to a limit set by **rune rank** (and quality / headroom). Percival / Source auction explanation: this is the big difference vs regular scrolls.
- Tiny compressed scrolls: power holds but **overload headroom drops** (Ch 27). Card-size needs only a little energy to start (farmer boy beat).
- **Rune Mastery:** −10% activation energy per level (cap −90%). Levels by **combat use** of runic scrolls / gear, not sofa casts.
- Mana Shield–type runic scrolls, elemental arrow packs, detonation mines, grenade inserts (Compression + Ethereal Pathways) appear later as the same family.

### Market

- Harder / slower → expensive. Shelf often dusty because bulk regulars are “good enough.”
- Roland’s High / Highest Common Fire Arrow brand undercuts “support caster” needs vs regular prices once quality is real (`Economy.md` Ch 27 note).

## Prefill / who pays mana (clarified)

### At craft (both)

- Maker spends mana **into the writing / pathways**. Magic scrolls: craft = full designed cast × S (`MagicScrollMaking.md`). Blanks are not free batteries with the user's cast already paid.
- Regular: imbue at full cast × Mana Scribing S; power designed; device grades = attacking value.
- Runic: craft mana **builds / seats the machine**. **Absorbing:** user pays activation (η table). **Preloaded:** stored spell at craft; user activates. Rune quality (perfection) seats `η_cond`.

### At use

| | Regular | Runic |
|---|---|---|
| Start cost | **Mana only.** Read aloud. Pay designed cast cost. | Absorbing: mana or stamina ÷ η. Preloaded: activate (may need a symbolic spark). |
| Extra energy | Stronger design = higher cast cost. | Absorbing: yes, up to rank ceiling / overload. Preloaded: usually fixed release. |
| Prepaid craft (P9/P10) | Not the magic-scroll model (user still pays cast). | Preloaded style is the special battery SKU. |

**Bottom line:** magic scrolls make the user pay the designed cast. Absorbing runics make the user pay activation energy. Preloaded runics release a stored spell on activate.

| Role | Regular scroll | Runic scroll |
|---|---|---|
| **Maker** | Yes: Mage → Mana Scribe (elemental mage for elemental spells). | Rune craft class (Runic Mana Scribe / Runesmith). Mage helpful for pool / side skills; elemental affinity **not** required. |
| **User** | Needs a **mana pool** (read aloud). Not for no-mana users. | No magic class required. Need enough **energy** (MP or SP) to trigger (tiny for card scrolls). |
| **Shop worker contracts** | Academy mage kids often fill Scribe seats. | Rare specialists; Roland hides Runic Mana Scribe early. |

## Overcharge / overload (runic only in early lock)

1. **User amplification:** pour extra energy (mana or stamina) while activating → stronger effect up to **rank cap** (High/Highest quality sits near next-rank jump).
2. **Compression trade:** smaller scroll → less overload headroom (Ch 27).
3. **Skills:** Empower Rune / Rune Overload (later Source) push past normal limits; risk burning pathways / metal.
4. **Overrun ceiling:** design note ~**5×** working ceiling (`RuneSystem.md` / RuneSetup P11). Not fully locked how quality stacks with overcharge.
5. **Regular scrolls:** no parallel “user overcharge” line found.

## Roland first-scroll ramp (rewrite clocks)

Pattern matches his tests: **first try messy, second same sitting often works** when the image exists. Materials add variables; new hosts reset a short learning loop, not a year.

### First night (Ch 21, monster-skin + magic ink)

| Piece | Time | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Word **Mana Bolt** | **few minutes** | Works first try (L1 pay ~cast×0.9). Identify = name only |
| Practice runic **Fire Orb** | slower than word; same evening | Seats enough for XP; Diagnosis **red** (~**75** MP / ~3 Bolts). Ink viscosity + cheap hide roughness + latent mana; thin-sheet schematic overlay **does not** work on thick hide |

Trial Fire Orb (Ch 16) was already a working seating on beast skin under hourglass pressure (fail → Hands cascade → success). Ch 21 is the first **real-world** Orb on bought stock: still red, not a multi-week wall.

### Half-year desk (Ch 21.5 → Ch 22)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Buys Orb of Light / Fire Arrow / Aqua samples. **Fire Arrow** schematic perfected first (Common **2×** XP) |
| **~Month 1** | Common Fire Arrow **possible at Lowest** (Rune Scribing climbing) |
| **Mid skip** | Quality climbs Low → Intermediate → **High**; fails burn blanks/ink |
| **Exit (~6 months)** | Rune Scribing **L5**; comet batch **8 High + 2 Highest** FA; Orb/Aqua boards when desk allows; Mana Scribing still low (**L2** by Ch 23) |

### Throughput after sellable quality

| Beat | Pace |
|---|---|
| Ch 26 contract | ~**1 hour**/Common FA; min **10**/week |
| Ch 27 compacted | **5–6**/day |

### Other materials (soft, same growth shape)

| Host | Feel vs cheap monster skin | Learning reset |
|---|---|---|
| **Mundane paper** | Easier imbue; **1 cast** then burns | **Same day** for known rune (smoother draw; less multi-use forgive) |
| **Better / magic hide or magic paper** | Harder focus (latent mana); multi-use threshold | **1–3 evenings** to seat cleanly after skin mastery |
| **New rune pattern** (Frost/Gale Arrow, etc.) | Schematic first (hours → Highest board), then seating | **Days**, not months, once FA technique exists (Ch 26 maps Highest same stretch) |
| **Hands pen / press** on known pattern | See `../Progression/ManaHands.md` | **Same day → ~1 week** steady; press wins batch once matrix does not cascade |
| **Metal / wood later** | Hammer / heat / different wear | New craft family (Blacksmith era); not scroll-desk months |

**Rule of thumb:** known pattern on a **new blank type** ≈ trial-shaped loop (ugly → clean in **one sitting to a few days**). Known blank type on a **new pattern** ≈ schematic hours + seating **days**. Both at once stretches toward **a week**, not half a year.
