# Family

## Narrative

House Arden is a Caldris knight house under Baron Wentworth. Roland is the fourth son and illegitimate. The house wants a warrior name and does not want crafting tied to Arden in public. Martha is the one kind constant from his childhood.

## Detail

**House:** Arden (Baron's house)

**Kingdom:** Caldris

**Head:** Baron Wentworth Arden

**Public stance:** Combat-focused Baron house. Wentworth does not want the Arden name publicly tied to Roland's crafting path. This is house pressure on Roland, not a world-wide knight-house law.

**Roland crystals:** His first class crystal path is shaped by being that Baron's son (estate freebie / arrangements). Not a universal freebie law for every commoner.

**Roland's place:** 4th son. Illegitimate. Birth mother is an unnamed commoner not at the estate.

### Wentworth Arden

**Role:** Baron. Military official. Later called Silver Wolf.

**Look:** Large build, mustache, scar from left eyebrow to lip. Looks early forties, near mid-sixties.

**Stance:** Combat and army first. Approves Runesmith only if Roland earns keep and keeps the Arden name off craft work. Sends him to Carwen with no money and army enlistment as fallback.

**Later:** Called Silver Wolf. Silver armor. Border ceasefire with Hatfordian Empire ends a posting. White-silver locket: dark-haired woman holding a baby whose face is scratched out.

### Tabitha Arden

**Role:** First wife / main wife

**Children:** Reyner, Edwin, Sophia

### Francine Arden

**Role:** Second wife / mistress living in the house

**Children:** Robert, Dianna, Lucienne

**Stance:** Dislikes Lucienne spending time with Roland.

### Household

**Martha:** Personal maid. Older. Kind to the original Roland. Tends sickness, food, packing. Not allowed to leave with him. He hopes to hire her later.

**Adam:** House butler. Gray hair, black tux. Tests, tutors, ascension crystal, departure arrangements.

**Drill instructor:** Unnamed bald scarred soldier. Physical training and goblin bravery tests.

### House Dreux (Edelgard)

Count's house ruling Edelgard. Louis Dreux (second son) hires Abyssal Cult against the merchant council while heir Armand is at the Hatfordian border. Armand returns with the ceasefire and hosts Silver Wolf (Wentworth). Not yet named heir.
