# Ned Silk — Mana Conductive

**Lock:** Ned's silk / threads conduct ambient mana. Worn as cloth or scarf contact, they **help absorption** instead of blanketing it the way ordinary fabric does.

**Homes:** `Ned.md` · `NedStatus.md` · Earth silk anchors `../World/Science/Biomaterials/BiologicalSilks.md` · refill curve `../Runes/Energy.md` · Ch 23 weaver clothes `../../Story/Notes/Chapter23QuietRoom.md`.

---

## What it does

- **Ordinary cloth:** covers skin and slows ambient pull (more bare skin → faster refill; Ch 23 prose / `Chapter21_5.md` tip band).
- **Ned silk:** mana moves through the fiber. Threads on skin, woven clothes, and his small cape act as extra **conductive surface**, so he absorbs **more** while dressed than the same cut in linen or leather would allow.
- **Scarf Ned:** live worm scarf is the same family of silk contact on neck/shoulders whenever he is worn.

Does **not** grant a mana pool to Ned. Does **not** replace Mage / Blessed recovery cards. Does **not** change max MP. It only eases **ambient absorb** into a pool he already has (Mana Absorption / Regulation path).

## Math (soft)

Refill uses `S` (surface) and `Rob` (permeability) in `Energy.md`. Ned silk:

- Does **not** cut `Rob` the way thick ordinary cloth does when it covers skin.
- Soft-raises effective absorb when a lot of fiber is on him (full thread outfit + scarf), still / rest, and thick ambient.

No fixed % mult locked yet. Feel: dressed in his threads he keeps a strong tip-sip; stripping still helps a little, but linen-level choke is gone.

Passive Hands hold budget (`../../Story/Notes/Capabilities/ManaHandsPassive.md`) rides the same refill. Better absorb → more tip-sip headroom for continuous drains when he is wearing Ned silk.
