## Core Principle

Every protection should work in layers. Magic gear handles most threats. Trained senses and skills take over when magic fails, especially inside anti-magic zones. Mundane backups cover what both miss.

## Skill Goals

### Echolocation → Vibration Sense → Energy Sense

- **Current:** Chapter 19 exits Echolocation at **L7** (~**22,000** spaced clicks). Vibration Sense waits for **2,000,000** uses. Energy Sense is the evolve after that.
- **Goal:** Train Vibration Sense until it evolves into a general energy sense. That read covers heat, mana and living fields. It is not a separate life-only skill.
- **Sending:** Pulses vibration out through the body, bones and skin rather than the voice alone.
- **Receiving:** Reads returning echoes through skin and hair, the way whiskers and fine body hair pick up air movement.
- **Protection value:**
  - Works when deafened or when the ears are damaged and healing.
  - Keeps working during a flash-and-bang attack that takes out both eyes and ears.
  - Works inside anti-magic zones since it's a trained body skill, not gear.
  - Reads ground tremors through the feet, giving warning of footsteps and approaching threats.

### What Energy Sense covers

Living-field reads sit inside Energy Sense, not beside it.
- **Speed:** Electromagnetic fields travel at the speed of light. Sound travels about 343 m/s in air, nearly a million times slower.
- **Real-world basis:**
  - Sharks and rays sense the electric fields of hidden prey through pores on their snouts.
  - Platypuses hunt by sensing the muscle signals of prey in muddy water.
  - Birds sense Earth's magnetic field to navigate.
- **What it detects:**
  - **Heartbeats:** Location, count and state of every living creature nearby. A racing heart reveals fear, stress or a lie.
  - **Nerve signals:** The brain fires the command to move before the muscle moves. Reading that signal gives early notice of every strike, step and draw.
  - **Muscle tension:** Shows where weight is shifting and which limb is loading for an attack.
  - **Hidden creatures:** Finds living things behind cover, in darkness, underground or under invisibility, since magnetic fields pass through most non-metal materials.
- **Combat value:** Nerve signals lead movement by a fraction of a second. At 10 to 15 times thinking speed, that fraction becomes a long, clear warning of every attack before it starts. Earth RT / anticipation floors: `../../World/Science/Body/SpeedPerception.md`.
- **Passive:** Unlike echolocation, it sends nothing out, so it never gives away his position.
- **Anti-magic:** Works in anti-magic zones if trained as a body sense, like a shark's.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - **Short natural range:** Body fields are faint and fade fast with distance. Training and magic extend the range.
  - **No signal from non-living foes:** Golems, constructs and some undead give off little or nothing. Vibration sense covers them.
  - **Interference:** Iron-rich volcanic rock, magnets, lightning, his own forge and his runic gear can add noise.
  - **Still targets:** A resting, calm creature gives a weaker signal than an active one.

### Thermal Sight (Optional Companion Sense)

- **Goal:** See infrared heat the way pit vipers do.
- **Speed:** Infrared is light, so it also travels at light speed.
- **Value:** Longer range than the bioelectric sense. Spots warm bodies, fresh tracks, heated weapons and hidden fires.
- **Fit:** Pairs naturally with his heat immunity and forge work.

### Concussion and Stun Resistance

- **Goal:** Stay conscious and functional through head impacts, choke attempts and stun effects.
- **Why:** Fast thinking is worthless while unconscious.

### Mental Resistance

- **Goal:** Resist charm, fear, illusion, compulsion and confusion.
- **Why:** A mind 10 to 15 times average is his greatest asset and a prime target.
- **Driver:** Willpower.
- **Ideas:** Fear/Charm → Unshakable Mind line in `../../Progression/OtherUsefulSkillsSystem.md`.

### Breath Control

- **Current:** Chapter 19 breath-holds between grill sets. Exit **L5**. Leveling unit is **1 minute** held = **1** use. Hold length = **T0 × M × skill level** (~**16.7 min** comfortable at his Vit/End with L5). Live law: `../../Progression/SkillsDesign.md`.
- **Goal:** More held time, higher skill multiplier, and the same skill covering smoke and toxic gas. Poison stays its own track.
- **Why:** Heat immunity doesn't stop smoke inhalation or suffocation. The volcanic island has gases that pool in low ground plus vog.
- **Idea park (unread):** oxygen-efficiency M ladder / Suffocation Immunity / Abyssal Body pairing: `../../Progression/ResistanceImmunitySystem.md`. Do not replace Breath Control until that file is accepted.

### Mana Overload Resistance

- **Goal:** Resist mana poisoning, mana burn and hostile mana attacks.
- **Why:** Mana poisoning killed the original Roland. Heavy runic casting adds strain.

### Paralysis Resistance

- **Goal:** Resist paralytic toxins and binding effects beyond what Poison Resistance covers.

### Electricity Resistance

- **Goal:** Resist shock and lightning.
- **Why:** Not covered by heat or cold. Causes paralysis and heart failure.
- **Lock:** Roland levels this track. Live blurbs: `../../Progression/SkillsDesign.md`. Full set with Acid / Blunt / Sharp: `../../Progression/RolandResistanceTracks.md`. Idea current thresholds: `../../Progression/OtherUsefulSkillsSystem.md`.

### Blood Regeneration

- **Goal:** Faster blood volume recovery on top of wound recovery.
- **Why:** Many small wounds in a long fight can drain him even while each one heals.

### Balance

- **Goal:** Stay stable through vertigo, inner ear hits, spinning effects and bad footing.

## Equipment Goals

### Intensity-Filtering Glasses

- **Function:** Magic shield lenses that only let light below a set intensity through.
- **Protects against:** Flash attacks, blinding spells, sudden bright light.
- **Bonus uses:**
  - Forge work with molten metal and Lili's lava.
  - Looking at volcanic vents, lava and bright magical effects.
  - Normal vision stays clear since ordinary light passes untouched.
- **Anti-magic weakness:** The shield fails inside an anti-magic zone. Full-body vibration sense takes over there.
- **Mundane backup:** A flip-down physical tinted lens for anti-magic zones.

### Sound-Limiting Ear Protection

- **Function:** Magic seals that only let sound below a set intensity through, like modern active hearing protection.
- **Protects against:** Sonic attacks, explosions, thunderclaps and shrieks.
- **Normal hearing:** Speech and quiet sounds still pass through.
- **Anti-magic weakness:** Vibration sense through skin and hair covers him when the seals fail.
- **Mundane backup:** Physical plugs made from Ned's silk or wax.

### Breathing Gear

- **Function:** A respirator or rune-powered air filter.
- **Protects against:** Smoke, volcanic gas, vog, poison clouds and drowning for short stretches.
- **Mundane backup:** Layered filter mask made from Ned's silk.

### Heart and Brain Armor

- **Function:** The heaviest protection placed over the heart and head.
- **Why:** Mages work mana through the heart and brain. A hit to either kills him and cuts off his magic at the same moment.
- **Options:** Star steel plates, Ned's silk padding, Lili as living armor.

### Runic Gear Redundancy

- **Problem:** His runic magic is equipment heavy, so losing gear means losing power.
- **Goals:**
  - Backup copies of key runes on separate items.
  - Runes inscribed in places that are hard to strip, such as armor linings, bracers and rings.
  - Anti-theft binding so gear can't be taken and used against him.
  - Shielding against mana blockers and dispels.
- **Known counters to plan around:** Orihalcum blocks mana and aurium damps flowing mana.

### Mundane Weapon Backups

- **Function:** Weapons that work with no mana at all.
- **Why:** Inside anti-magic zones, rune weapons may go dead. His strength, swordsmanship, throwing and marksmanship still work.

## Vehicle Goals

Science ladders stay in `../../World/Science/Vehicle/`. These are D's builds.

### Rune Bike

- **Drive:** Runes turn mana directly into kinetic motion with little loss, driving the wheel hubs.
- **Frame:** Star steel tubing or a needle worm silk and shellac composite. No aluminum exists, so light strength comes from silk composites, chitin and steel.
- **Tires:** Dandelion rubber reinforced with needle worm silk cords and cloth.
- **Suspension:** Resilin springs and dampers from Ned for rough lava rock and mountain trails.
- **Power storage:** A small flywheel or mana stone pack stores energy for bursts and recovers energy when braking.
- **Anti-magic backup:** Pedal drive so it still moves when the runes go dead.
- **Terrain:** Built for trails, lava fields and switchbacks between farm zones.

### Hover Vehicle

- **Lift:** Mana-powered lift holding it off the ground.
- **Altitude effect:** Ambient mana thickens with height, so the vehicle gathers more power and runs more efficiently in the uplands and over the summit.
- **Uses:** Crossing fresh lava, flooded loʻi, swamps, reefs and open water where wheels fail.
- **Weakness:** Drops when mana runs out or inside an anti-magic zone.

### Hybrid Hover Bike

- **Concept:** Combines both. Rolls on wheels to save mana on roads and trails, then lifts into hover mode over water, lava and broken ground.
- **Anti-magic fallback:** Drops back onto its wheels and pedals instead of crashing.

### Glider Add-On

- **Wings:** Folding needle worm silk membrane stretched over resilin and chitin spars, clipping onto the hover bike.
- **Lift sources:**
  - **Ridge lift:** Trade winds rising up windward slopes.
  - **Thermals:** Heat rising off sun-warmed lava fields and volcanic vents.
  - **Mana assist:** Extra thrust and lift, stronger at altitude.
  - **Balloon buoyancy:** An optional gas cell for slow, long-range drifting.
- **Uses:** Descending from the summit to the coast in minutes, scouting from above, escaping pursuit and crossing between islands.
- **Pairing:** Matches the thermal-gliding path imagined for the Nēnē.

### Personal Glider Backup

- **Concept:** A wingsuit or packable silk paraglider worn separately.
- **Why:** Lets him survive a fall or bail out if the vehicle fails mid-flight or enters an anti-magic zone.

## General Goals

- **Never be senseless:** Sight, hearing, vibration sense and bioelectric sense backing each other up so no single attack blinds him completely.
- **Never be surprised:** Energy Sense reads attacks before they start, and fast thinking turns that warning into time to act.
- **Never be powerless:** Magic, runic gear, mundane weapons and physical skill each able to carry a fight alone.
- **Never be knocked out:** Head protection plus concussion and stun resistance.
- **Never be controlled:** Strong mental resistance protecting his mind.
- **Never be choked out:** Breath control plus breathing gear.
- **Never lose his kit:** Redundant runes, anti-theft binding and a way to rebuild gear with Lili.

## Unchecked Ideas

### Clothes

- Needle worm silk base layer: cut-resistant, light and breathable
- Aurium-thread lining: damps hostile mana and casting burn
- Mythril-thread rune embroidery: backup rune surfaces on sleeves and collars
- Rune-lined long coat: inner panels hold spare runes
- Mana-insulated casting gloves: protect hands during Hands and tool casting
- Chitin-soled boots: grip on lava rock with resilin insoles
- Wax-sealed hooded cloak: waterproof and camouflage-dyed
- Built-in spatial storage pocket: emergency gear sewn into the coat
- Tear-away fastenings: clothing rips free when grabbed
- Utility harness: fast access to throwing weapons, rune plates and mana stones

### Armor

- Star steel breastplate: heaviest protection over the heart
- Suspended helmet: resilin straps spread impact to prevent concussion
- Silk-shellac laminate plates: light composite armor
- Lili as living armor: covers vital zones and self-repairs
- Orihalcum ward plates: blocks mana, placed only where he never casts through
- Adamantium inserts: indestructible, backed with silk padding
- Inscribed bracers: instant shield runes
- Chitin scale mail: flexible scales over silk backing
- Gorget: protects the throat from chokes and strikes
- Mundane backup layer: works when every rune is dead

### Weapons

- Runic crossbow or bow: silk string and pre-inscribed bolts
- Rune-tipped arrowheads: store Bolt or Arrow for release on impact
- One-handed star steel sword: mana-insulated grip
- Venom-grooved throwing knives: coated with Ned's toxins
- Mythril casting rod: longer range and accuracy for Bolt and Arrow
- Heat-forged dagger: wielded red-hot barehanded thanks to heat immunity
- Blowgun or dart launcher: silent, loaded with paralytic or sleep toxins
- Weighted silk net or bolas: capture and tangling
- Sling with forged slugs: silent and immune to anti-magic
- Earth-knowledge firearm: niche personal gun for special situations

### Tools

- Portable forge kit: field smelting and repair with Lili
- Rune scribing kit: compression stencils from A4 down to 1/512
- Mana stone reserves: spare monster cores for power
- Silk harvesting tools: collect Ned's silk, wax, resin and venom
- Climbing kit: silk rope, grapnel, pitons and hooks
- Assay kit: tests conversion grades of mythril and star steel
- Mana detector: spots orihalcum, aurium, mana blockers and anti-magic zones
- Field diagnosis kit: supports the Diagnosis transfer skill
- Field medical kit: sutures, splints and antidotes
- Spatial storage bag: carries forge kit, materials and backup gear

- Acrobatics: rolls, flips and fall recovery
- Swimming and Diving: reef and open ocean

- Quick Casting: shortens time from decision to spell
