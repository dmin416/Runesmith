## What he needs

| Need | Note |
|---|---|
| A full starter colony, not a lone queen | Queen does not forage or build. Alone she dies. First hive on an empty island needs workers. |
| Mated queen already | Virgin needs drone congregations. Mate on the mainland. |
| Gentle stock | Italian, Carniolan, or Buckfast-type *Apis mellifera*. Skip Africanized lines. |
| Travel kit | Closed nuc or package (queen + thousands of workers), sugar syrup / honey water, ventilation, insulation. Shade from heat; buffer from cold drafts. |
| Landing site | Farm peg / shed shade out of trade winds; water source; flowers within flight range. |
| Early trust care | Feed and keep the cluster comfortable from day one. Colony is the partner, not one bee. |
| Optional second path | Tropical stingless bees (*Melipona* kin) if he wants no-sting pot honey instead of or beside *mellifera*. |

**Do not bring:** queen in a jar alone; unmated virgin; mean Africanized stock; hope of taming wild island *Hylaeus* into a honey farm.

## Early trust (starts on the road)

Trust is not a farm-only phase. Feeding and steady care begin as soon as he owns the nuc. That maps to the low end of the magic bond ladder (`HoneyBeesMagic.md`: honey offerings, calm visits → **Tolerated** / **Accepted**).

| Care | What he does | Why it builds trust |
|---|---|---|
| Feed | Sugar syrup or diluted honey on a schedule while sealed or short of forage | Full, fed bees are calmer; offerings are the base “honey for care” deal |
| Ned hemolymph (optional boost) | Small drip diluted into syrup / honey water; Ned allows the draw | Trehalose + mana + mild toxin load; richer offering; warrior / defense budget (`HoneyBeesMagic.md` §12.7) |
| Warm / buffer | Insulate the box on cold nights and mountain passes; keep out of wet wind; never bake them in sun on deck | Cluster spends energy shivering if chilled; comfort reads as a safe home |
| Quiet handling | Slow moves, no crushing, soft voice / no bangs on the crate | Workers learn his scent and routine; fear-sweat and jerks raise defense |
| Smoke only when needed | Light cool smoke for checks, not constant fog | Masks alarm without teaching him as a threat |
| Return surplus later | When they make honey, leave plenty; give some back as honey water | Same offering habit, scaled up |

Deep tiers (Hive-kin, Queen-bound, Aware speech) still need time and queen-rearing. The road care is enough to start **Tolerated** early and reach **Accepted** faster once the hive is parked.

## Ned hemolymph (middleman)

Ned is the fluid middleman, not prey. Bees do not hunt him.

| Step | Do |
|---|---|
| Draw | Small voluntary harvest vs his **~1.5–2.5 L** tank (`../Ned.md`, `../../World/Fauna/Species/NeedleWorm.md`) |
| Dilute | Mix into water or honey water so it feeds like syrup |
| Offer | Same calm schedule as other early feedings |
| Result | Hive refines sugars / amino load / mana / mild poison into stores, jelly, and venom budget |
| Defense | Supports warrior bees and swarm defense against hive predators; not one bee drinking a foe dry |
| Limit | Flower forage still carries the colony when blooms exist; constant fighting still empties stores |

Full design: `../../World/Fauna/Domestic/HoneyBeesMagic.md` §12.7.

## What he does (stretch checklist)

### Before leaving the mining city / on the mainland

1. Buy a **nuc or package** (mated queen included), or arrange one at a southern trade hub / Luden before the sail.
2. Prefer gentle *A. mellifera*. Stingless colony only if a tropical keeper sells one.
3. Pack smoker fuel, spare syrup, cloth or straw wrap for cold, and a sealable travel box with air.
4. Learn basic open-hive calm (smoke, stand beside not in front, no crushing the queen). Start the feed-and-warm habit the day he buys them.

### On the road and sail

1. Feed syrup on a calm schedule. Keep the cluster warm enough to hold brood heat, shaded from deck sun, dry from spray.
2. Soft check when safe (queen alive, bees clustering, food left). Do not pry them open in a crowded wagon unless something is wrong.
3. If the queen dies in transit, the package fails unless he can requeen from another colony (he will not have one yet). Treat the queen as cargo that cannot be replaced mid-trip.

### After Albrook / farm peg

1. Set the nuc in shade, entrance clear, water nearby. Keep feeding until local flowers carry them.
2. Build a proper hive body when the workshop allows (`FarmBuildings.md`).
3. Continue the same care into **Accepted / Hive-friend**; queen grafting later if he wants Hive-kin.
4. Real cooperation talk (trade honey for protection) sits at magical **Aware** colony mind and up. The food-and-warmth deal is already running before that.

## One-line

Buy a mated gentle nuc on the mainland, feed and keep them comfortable from day one (syrup, warmth, optional Ned hemolymph dilute), park them at the farm peg, and let deeper bond tiers grow from that. A queen alone does not start Dragnis bees.
