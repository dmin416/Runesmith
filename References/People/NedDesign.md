## Diet (Ch 13 half-year)

Lock: `Ned.md`. Leaves, discarded parts, parsleaves, magic biology, about **150 mL**/day whole blood, no human digestion model on his blood or flesh.

Eats various **leaves** and discarded **monster parts**. Hides a preferred leaf stash: those leaves are a **mild poison source** (weaker than his spikes) with a vibrant refreshing aroma like **parsley** (**parsleaves**). Roland samples one afternoon and locks that link. Besides occasionally harvesting Ned’s ever-growing **needles** and **blood**, Roland also **dries and stockpiles** parsleaves. Planned later: detachable back **gel nodules** (nodule skin → magical hide; gel fluid → magical ink). After Roland harvested Ned’s blood for BBQ seasoning, Ned pressed to know why; Roland started feeding him small drips of his own blood via Mana Hands (Ch 13). Ned does not taste like a humanoid: he mainly senses how much power the flesh holds and the experience it grants. Monsters can level by eating (unlike humanoids who need actions); that feeds their presence and faster climb. Roland only feeds his blood in-room (**150 mL**/day whole blood, about **5 oz**); he uses Ned’s blood and needles as seasoning and keeps the skill-leveling cheat quiet. Worm blood is armpit-smelling but incredibly nutrient-rich even when Ned only ate magic stones; Roland extracts as much as he wants comfortably. That **150 mL** is total volume (plasma + cells). At ~40–45% hematocrit it is only **~60–70 mL** of red cells, which sits inside Ch 13 rewrite daily RBC restore (**50–83 mL**/day; `../Progression/Progression.md`). Plasma side refills in hours at his M. Sleeps with Ned as a cool ectothermic pillow. He talks to Ned as an outlet for self-talk: future plans, skill thought process, training ideas (sneaking, throwing, adapting, healing and more). Dislikes bark-colored **Needle Moths**; shows Ned how weak that evolution line is while feeding him poisonous wings and occasionally stones. Drills the vision: speed, penetration, toughness, recovery, poison, stealth, ranged attack. After taming, Ned can pass **stair throats** (wild monsters cannot); strangers assume a Floor-2 trophy scarf and Roland never corrects them. Once strong enough, Ned solo-hunts dungeon rats and other Needle Worms; monsters cooperate in packs but also fight each other for strength.

## Development notes (training / growth)

How Roland steers Ned’s loadout as he levels. Not automatic freebies; practice and exposure unlock or deepen them.

- **Throwing:** Practice hurling spikes and rocks → ranged needle launch / improvised rock throw accuracy and force.
- **Stealth:** Focus on staying hidden → camouflage (leaf-green blend, stillness, ambush timing).
- **Climbing:** Trees, dungeon walls, cabin verticals (seat backs / panels / luggage rack) → grip and vertical travel.
- **Poison:** Eat more poisons and foreign spikes / venoms → broader toxin repertoire and **Poison Resistance** (he is already immune to his own).
- **Pain / Heat / Cold / Seal:** Shared with Roland’s exposure drills (Ch 19 train grill on plates/segments). Seal is Ned’s **Recovery** parallel; Pain / Heat / Cold dose-gate like Roland’s resistances.
- **Attack build:** Push harder spikes, faster regeneration, and higher durability so charges and needle strikes hit harder and recover between fights.
- **Toxin-free write spike (planned):** A later spike variant with **no toxin** so shed / cut tips can serve as clean cores or punches for Roland’s homemade pencils (`../World/Science/Invent/WritingTools.md`). Poisoned needles stay combat / seasoning only.

### Fastest recovery (seal first)

Combat priority is **close the bag fast**: living epidermis + hemolymph / glue seal, cast off dead scrap, neighboring cells stick the edges. That makes him **hard to kill** on the Dex/Int dump trade. Deeper remodel of spring and spike bed can lag; a sealed Ned is still a durable explosive animal, not an open corpse.

**Tun / cryptobiosis** is survival stasis (dry shell, revive with water). It is **not** the fast recovery mode.

## Skill training cycle (Ned ↔ D)

Action counts and aptitude: `../Progression/CompanionSkillLeveling.md`. Acid ceilings: `../World/Fauna/BiologicalAcid.md`. Exit locks stay in `../../Story/Notes/NedStatus.md`.

**Goal:** one rotating week that touches every Ned skill, with D as the safe training partner (Recovery / Pain / Heat / Cold / Poison on D’s side pay for the contact work).

### Skill → drill map

| Ned skill | Drill with D | What counts as 1 action |
|---|---|---|
| **Spike Throwing / Needle Launch** | Short launch at glove / board / catch-and-return | One aimed launch |
| **Needle Formation** (body) | Harvest tips on purpose; wait for regrow | One tip bed fully replaced |
| **Needle Absorb** (body) | Eat spent tip / rival needle / mineral spike feed | One tip (or equal mass) swallowed and broken down |
| **Acid Production** | Digest spike feedstock into gland acid; hold fill | One fill pulse to usable spray volume |
| **Acid Spray / Aim** | Controlled spit onto board, then dilute splash on D’s forearm (Recovery-capped) | One aimed spit |
| **Silk Production** | Bind D’s wrist, trip line, scarf wrap, catch net | One useful strand / wrap |
| **Stealth / Camouflage** | Hide under furniture / leaf pile / D’s scarf; D finds him | One held hide until found or timer ends |
| **Climbing** | Seat backs, wall panels, trees, D’s body as moving pole | One climb segment |
| **Spring Charge** | Soft coil shove into padded D / bag; full lane on hunt days | One full coil and release |
| **Bash / Impact** (feeds Pain + Seal) | Tip-poke, plate-rub, coil-bump, blunt tip tap on D; D may return light Mana Hands taps on Ned’s plates | One real impact dose survived (Ned side) |
| **Seal / Regeneration** | Harvest nick, tip wear, bash rebound, dilute acid kiss on his own plate edge | One wound sealed |
| **Poison Resistance** | Eat parsleaves, foreign spikes, moth wings, new toxin first doses | One dose (own toxin = soft or free) |
| **Pain / Heat / Cold** | Shared grill patches on Ned’s plates; bash sting; hot/cold acid optional | One real exposure survived |

**D’s parallel grind (same session):** Recovery, Pain, Heat, Cold, Poison (Ned toxin / dilute acid), Multitasking, Dodging / Catch (needle return), Breath if holds during hide-and-seek.

### Daily block order (~2–3 h room + hunt)

Run in this order so materials flow: needles → absorb → acid → spray → seal.

1. **Warm hide (10–15 min).** Ned Stealth. D counts finds. Soft Stealth volume.
2. **Climb circuit (10 min).** Verticals on furniture / walls / D’s arm-as-pole.
3. **Needle harvest → form (15–20 min).** D cuts or plucks a few tips (Mana Hands). Ned **Needle Formation** while D bags tips for seasoning / absorb feed.
4. **Absorb (10 min).** Ned eats spent tips + optional mineral/toxin feed. **Needle Absorb** + **Poison Resistance** when the feed is foreign.
5. **Acid make (10–15 min).** Spike feedstock → gland fill. **Acid Production**. No spray until full.
6. **Throw block (20–30 min).** Launches at mark; D catch-and-feed-back (train pattern). Misses that sting D count for D Pain / Recovery and Ned Seal if tip snaps.
7. **Bash set (15 min).** Rotate contact types so Pain does not soft-repeat only one mode:
   - tip poke (shallow)
   - plate grind / rub
   - coil shove (no full charge)
   - blunt tip tap
   - D light return taps on Ned plates
8. **Acid on D (5–10 min, Recovery-capped).** Dilute gland spit on a marked forearm square, then neutralize / potion / knit. Ned **Acid Spray** aim. D Heat/Pain/Poison as the splash warrants. Never full-strength room spray at D’s face.
9. **Silk tidy (10 min).** Bind the drill kit, wrap scarf, one trip line. Low volume but every day.
10. **Seal finish.** Any open harvest / bash / acid-edge nick closes before sleep. **Seal** bottleneck = injury supply; do not invent free wounds.

**Blood drip:** usual **150 mL**/day whole blood sits outside the skill blocks (diet + Seal soft assist). Extra blood from pokes/misses is paid from D’s Recovery, not free.

### Weekly rotation (keep Charge and acid ceilings honest)

| Day | Extra focus | Skip / soft |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Throw + absorb volume | Soft Charge (coil shove only) |
| **2** | Hide spam + climb | Soft acid (fill only, no D splash) |
| **3** | Bash variety + Seal | Soft throw |
| **4** | Acid make + acid on D (dilute) | Soft hide |
| **5** | Full **Spring Charge** lane (yard / dungeon corridor) | Soft acid splash |
| **6** | Hunt day: real Charge, real throw, real Stealth, foreign toxin eat | Room cycle shortened |
| **7** | Light review + Silk + Formation catch-up; rest spring | No D acid splash |

### Intensity locks

- **Acid on D:** dilute only until D’s Heat/Pain/Poison and Recovery say otherwise. Full gland strength is for boards, scrap metal, and enemies (`BiologicalAcid.md`: hot 20%+ HCl does not instant-melt steel; it wrecks flesh fast).
- **Bash:** shallow on purpose. Same rule as Ch 19 grill: trauma for skill bars, not maiming.
- **Charge:** room is for coil technique; power shots need a lane (Day 5–6).
- **Own toxin / own acid on Ned:** self-immune for poison; acid still counts as Heat/Pain/Seal if it damages his lining edge during a bad spit.
- **Aptitude:** Greater **4x** and later body jumps raise every bar via √(stat/15). Do not invent a second Ned XP curve.

| Needle Absorb | 5–15 |
| Acid Production fills | 3–8 |
| Acid Spray (board + 1–3 D splashes) | 10–30 |

Spring Charge: **handful** on Day 5–6 only (~6–12), not every room day.

### What this is not

Not a replacement for dungeon self-hunts. Hunt days still carry real Charge, Stealth, and Poison variety. The room cycle is the **controlled** half that lets Acid / Formation / Absorb / Bash-on-D exist without waiting for wild scrapes.
