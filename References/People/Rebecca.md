# Rebecca

## Narrative

Rebecca, called Becky, is the redhead archer who recruits Roland into his first adventurer party in Carwen. She holds the backline with him, keeps the group practical, and stays one of the early friends worth bringing back later.

## Detail

**Name:** Rebecca

**Nickname:** Becky

**Role:** Archer / backline

**Height:** About 170 cm

**Look:** Redhead. Tight kit with quiver belt.

**Party:** Carwen trio with Sahildr and Reyna. Recruits Roland as fourth.

**Formation:** Backline with Roland. Sahildr and Reyna front.

**Temper:** Practical. Calls him Little Goblin Slayer. Takes his bastard confession with humor.

**Notes:** All-women party after their prior fourth left with a man. Distrusts men at night camps early on. Already on a second Tier 1 class when they meet him. Promised future greater-rune gear.
