# Elodia

## Narrative

Elodia is a guild-side practical friend, not the love interest. She helps Roland with papers, cover, warnings, logistics or guild leverage. That favor makes her a close friend and later a hook into other conflict. Debt and loyalty, not wedding spine. Helci stays the romantic and emotional center.

## Detail

**Role:** Guild worker / found-family stabilizer

**Lane:** Friend and later conflict tie. Not romance.

**Orbit:** Armand, Lobelia and Solana stay attached through her when useful. They are conflict fuel more than core cast.

**Story job:** Concrete help early. Cost later (guild heat, family mess or forced choice). Do not leave her as a soft NPC with no teeth.

**Open:** Exact first favor and later bill when the spine beat lands.
