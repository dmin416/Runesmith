# Sahildr

## Narrative

Sahildr is the Goliath frontliner of Roland's first Carwen party. Loud, teasing, and hard to move, she tanks with a two-handed hammer whose lesser impact rune helps pull Roland toward runecraft.

## Detail

**Name:** Sahildr

**Race:** Goliath

**Strength:** Strength of 5 men. Hammer baseline: `../Combat/ImpactRune.md`.

**Role:** Warrior / frontline

**Height:** About 2 m

**Look:** Brown skin. Squashed nose.

**Weapons:** Shield at first meet. Two-handed hammer in the dungeon with a lesser impact rune. Stroke math: `../Combat/ImpactRune.md`.

**Party:** Carwen trio with Rebecca and Reyna.

**Formation:** Front with Reyna.

**Temper:** Loud. Teases. Forceful when worried. Blushes when praised.

**Notes:** Already on a second Tier 1 class when they meet Roland. Won't casually lend the hammer. Gets the watcher's boots after the ambush. Promised future armor.
