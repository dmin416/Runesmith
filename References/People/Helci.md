# Helci

## Narrative

Helci is a half-gnome orphan who starts as a Scout because that is what she can get, not what she wants. At awakening she did not have the strength to take Warrior. Leveling unlocks the body and options she lacked at the start. She works through Hunter into Ranger, then has no clean path forward and takes Assassin as her fourth class. Her true passion is a comically massive hammer used as a burst assassin. The fight style is Rita Vrataski-like: she is strong enough to swing it, but the real focus is burst power, flexibility, momentum, and dance. She is Roland's romantic and emotional center, and she becomes his forge partner once she sees him work runes.

## Detail

**Name:** Helci

**Race:** Half-gnome

**Height:** About 140 cm

**Look:** Orange hair (ponytail early on), long ears and brows, high cheekbones, fine features, more human proportions than pure gnomes

**Background:** Solaria orphanage. Leave church by 14 (acolyte or out). Two years of work to buy a class crystal.

**Age notes:** Late bloomer. Scout L1 around 16. **18** during the workshop / Mana Slash stretch (passion lock; both 18 for first sex beat).

**Early job:** Waitress at The Singing Crow in Edelgard

**True style:** Burst assassin with a comically massive hammer

**Fight style:** Rita Vrataski-like. Strong enough to wield the hammer, but not a slow tank swing. Focus is burst power, flexibility, momentum, and dance. Close in, load the hit, explode through, keep moving.

**Awakening limit:** Too weak for Warrior at first class. Scout is what she can take.

**Unlocks later:** Leveling raises her strength and opens options she could not use at awakening. That is what makes the heavy hammer path real.

**Class path:** Scout → Hunter → Ranger → Assassin → unknown fifth

**Early kit:** Dagger, cheap leather (chest and wrists), later short bow; broke after gear and potions; often lives meal to meal

**Role with Roland:** Main romantic and emotional focus. Workshop assistant / forge partner. Gear hunger. Sees him runecraft (first reads him as Enchantsmith). Silence deal for a Mana Slash shortsword.

**Temper:** Stubborn. Distrusts male adventurers for party help early on. Tsundere read (irritable then grateful). Cooks and cleans around the shop when she is there.

**Key places:** Edelgard, The Singing Crow, Manstos Grotto expedition, Roland's warehouse workshop, later leaves for a dungeon city
