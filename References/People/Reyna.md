# Reyna

## Narrative

Reyna is the quiet wolven scout of Roland's first Carwen party. She reads air and threat, fights up close with dual daggers, and says little unless drink or need pulls it out of her.

## Detail

**Name:** Reyna

**Race:** Wolven (lesser beast race)

**Role:** Scout and Thief / frontline skirmisher

**Height:** About 160 cm

**Look:** Gray hair. White bushy tail. Wolf eyes. Dual daggers.

**Party:** Carwen trio with Rebecca and Sahildr.

**Formation:** Front with Sahildr.

**Temper:** Quiet. Rare sleepy high voice. Sniffs the air. Gets chatty when drunk.

**Notes:** Already on a second Tier 1 class when they meet Roland. Flags the amateur Arden watcher before the ambush. Takes throwing daggers from assassin loot. Promised legendary daggers later.
