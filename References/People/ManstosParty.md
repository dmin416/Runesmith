# Manstos Party 4

## Narrative

Manstos Grotto Party 4 is a temporary expedition group. Roland and Helci are thrown in with a dwarf tank, a sun-elf tracker, and a human two-hand warrior. They clear ants, survive a sealed shaft, and part ways in Edelgard.

## Detail

**Job:** Manstos Grotto insectoid nest clear

**Party label:** Party 4

**Members:** Dalrak, Selanar, Orson, Roland, Helci

**Lead around them:** Wells leads the wider muster. Priest Elric is with a higher tier team, not theirs.

### Dalrak

**Race:** Dwarf

**Role:** Tank

**Kit:** Halberd and shield

**Notes:** Drinks hard. Snores. Frontline with Selanar.

### Selanar

**Race:** Sun elf

**Role:** Tracker / spotter

**Kit:** Bow

**Notes:** Quiet. Reads paths and nearby monsters. Helci watches him as a scout model.

### Orson

**Race:** Human

**Role:** Two-hand warrior

**Kit:** Two-handed sword

**Notes:** Rear with Helci against sneak attacks. Loud and crude. Backs Dalrak in melee.
