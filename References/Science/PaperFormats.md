# Paper Formats and Rune Heat

Physical sizes, stack weights and channeling-heat math for runic sheets and cards. Economy and four stocks: `Paper.md`. Writing kit: `WritingTools.md`. Cast unit: **1 mana ≈ 10 J** (`ManaCast.md`).

**Earth anchors:** A-series and index/poker sizes are math pegs. Caldris uses similar sheet bands, not brand names. Renaissance rag paper (linen / hemp) varies a lot by grade.

**Heat / magic-hide `f` tables below are a working model** for story scaling. Lock stock choice in `Paper.md` first.

## Card stock or pasteboard (rune cards meant to survive handling)

Thick card at 0.5 to 0.8 mm and 300 to 400 gsm: A6 weighs 4.7 to 6.2 g, so 100 cards weigh 470 to 620 g and stack 50 to 80 mm.

## Scrolls

- Paper scrolls use the same sheet weights. A 100-sheet paper scroll rolled tight weighs the same 0.6 to 1.6 kg but stores as a tube about 6 to 10 cm across.
- Parchment or vellum is heavier and stiffer, about 0.2 to 0.5 mm and 200 to 500 gsm. A4 vellum weighs about 12 to 30 g per sheet, so 100 sheets weigh 1.2 to 3 kg.

## Practical numbers for the mage

A quick-draw case of 20 to 30 pre-inked single-use rune cards on thin pasteboard weighs **100 to 200 g** and fits in a coat pocket. A 100-page booklet of A4 rune sheets is a **1 to 2 kg** bag item and up to about **1.4 inches** thick on heavy stock. That fits a bandolier satchel, not a belt pouch.

## Pipe volume scaling (drawn pipe on A4 vs smaller formats)

Example pipe drawn to fill the A4 page: **250 mm** long, **50 mm** OD, **40 mm** ID (drawn size).

### Volume on A4

| Measure | Volume |
|---|---|
| Outer solid volume | 490.9 cm³ |
| Bore (hollow inside) | 314.2 cm³ |
| Wall material | 176.7 cm³ |

`V = π/4 × (OD² − ID²) × length` for the wall, or `π/4 × D² × length` for a solid cylinder.

### Conversion factors from A4

Length scales by **k**. Area scales by **k²**. Volume scales by **k³**.

| Target | Area ratio | Length factor (k) | Volume factor (k³) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A5 | 0.500 | 0.707 | 0.354 |
| A6 note card | 0.250 | 0.500 | 0.125 |
| A7 | 0.125 | 0.354 | 0.044 |
| A8 | 0.0625 | 0.250 | 0.0156 |
| 3 × 5 in index card | 0.155 | 0.394 | 0.061 |
| Poker playing card | 0.0905 | 0.301 | 0.0272 |

### Same pipe on a poker card (k = 0.301)

- Drawn at **75.2 mm** long with **15.0 mm** OD and **12.0 mm** ID
- Outer solid: **13.4 cm³**; bore **8.6 cm³**; wall **4.8 cm³**
- Volume is about **37×** less than on A4 while face area is only about **11×** smaller

### Pipe volume vs paper volume (0.20 mm paper)

| Sheet | Paper volume | Pipe outer ÷ paper volume |
|---|---|---|
| A4 | 12.47 cm³ | 39.4 |
| Poker card | 1.13 cm³ | 11.8 |

Paper thickness stays fixed while the drawing shrinks, so the pipe-to-paper volume ratio changes by **k** (39.4 × 0.301 = 11.8). Multiply any A4 pipe volume by **k³** for the target card.

## Heat from channeling magic through paper

Assumption: the large A4 sheet can burst into flame from power channeled through it. Same stock and thickness, so mass and heat capacity scale with area. Poker card area ratio vs A4 ≈ **0.0905** (about **0.85 g** vs **9.4 g**).

### Same power through the card as the A4 sheet

Temperature rise scales with power / mass (and with cooling, power / area). Both give:

**Heat multiplier = 1 / 0.0905 ≈ 11× hotter**

Example: A4 ignites (~20 °C to 230 °C, **210 K** rise) with roughly **2,760 J** (9.4 g × 1.4 J/g·K × 210 K). The same **2,760 J** through the card is a ~**2,330 K** rise. The card flashes almost instantly.

### Scaled power

| Power scaling rule | Power factor | Card ΔT vs A4 |
|---|---|---|
| Scaled by area (0.0905) | 0.0905 | **1.00×** (same heat) |
| Scaled by length (0.301) | 0.301 | **3.33×** hotter |
| Scaled by volume (0.0272) | 0.0272 | **0.30×** (cooler) |

### Energy to ignite (~210 K rise, 1.4 J/g·K)

| Format | Energy to ignite |
|---|---|
| A4 | about 2,760 J |
| A5 | about 1,380 J |
| A6 note card | about 690 J |
| Index card 3 × 5 in | about 430 J |
| Poker card | about 250 J |

### Rules at the table

- Same power on a smaller card: heat multiplier = A4 area ÷ card area
- Power matched to card size by area: identical temperature
- Power scaled by volume (**k³**, following the pipe): card runs cooler by ~**0.30** vs A4
- Thicker card stock adds mass; **0.6 mm** pasteboard poker card takes roughly **3×** the energy of **0.2 mm** paper to the same temperature

## Paper vs parchment vs hide vs scroll

Scroll is a **format**. Parchment, hide and paper are **materials**.

| Factor | Paper (rag) | Parchment or vellum | Hide (tanned leather or rawhide) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thickness | 0.10 to 0.40 mm | 0.20 to 0.50 mm | 1 to 3 mm |
| A4 sheet weight | 6 to 16 g | 12 to 30 g | 60 to 200 g |
| Cost | Cheapest | High per skin | Moderate per piece, expensive per writing area |
| Labor | Pulp mill scales | Every skin handled individually | Tanning weeks (vegetable) or days (rawhide) |
| Yield | Hundreds of sheets per batch | Few sheets per skin | Large pieces but thick |
| Writing | Takes ink readily | Excellent; scrapable palimpsest | Better for carve / brand / paint |
| Durability | 500+ years if dry | 1,000+ years | Very high; poor fine detail |
| Water | Poor | Good; cockles | Best |
| Fire | Ignites near **230 °C** | Chars before flaming | Burns slowly |
| Flexibility | Folds and rolls well | Stiff; tends to unroll | Stiff; cracks if folded dry |

Shop prices for Caldris stocks: `Paper.md` (magical hide can undercut crappy paper as byproduct).

### Scroll versus flat sheets

- Scrolls suit one long continuous text or rune sequence and carry well in a tube.
- Finding a specific passage means unrolling.
- Repeated rolling cracks ink; worst on parchment and hide.
- Flat sheets, cards and booklets allow instant random access for quick-cast.

### Fit for the runic mage

- **Paper:** best for single-use runes. Cheap, light, burns clean when magic flows. Matches scaling and heat math here.
- **Parchment:** best for reusable or high-power runes. Survives channeling longer; can be scraped and redrawn. Costs more.
- **Hide:** best for permanent rune items (bracers, pouches, armor panels). Too heavy for a carried stack of sheets.
- **Scroll format:** rituals or long multi-rune sequences. Flat cards win for quick-cast.

Early rewrite lock still prefers **lowest magical hide** for early runic *blanks* in shops (`Paper.md`). Paper stays valid for practice, single-use burn runes and cheap experiments.

## Magic hide heat handling (working model)

Magical hide handles channeled power far better than paper. Heat fraction **f** = share of channeled power that becomes heat in the material.

```
ΔT = f × E / (m × c)
```

- `E` = energy channeled (J)
- `m` = card mass (g)
- `c` ≈ **1.5 J/g·K** leather, **1.4** paper

| Grade | f |
|---|---|
| Paper or mundane hide | 1.0 |
| Common magic hide | 0.5 |
| Fine magic hide | 0.25 |
| Rare magic hide | 0.1 |

**Hide assumptions:** **1.5 mm** at about **1.0 g/cm³** → **0.15 g per cm²** of face. Destroyed near **250 K** rise.

### Channel capacity per card (J before destruction)

| Format | Hide mass | Paper | Mundane hide (f=1) | Magic f=0.5 | Magic f=0.25 | Magic f=0.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A4 | 93.6 g | 2,760 | 35,100 | 70,200 | 140,400 | 351,000 |
| A5 | 46.6 g | 1,380 | 17,500 | 35,000 | 69,900 | 174,800 |
| A6 note card | 23.3 g | 690 | 8,700 | 17,500 | 35,000 | 87,400 |
| Index card 3 × 5 in | 14.5 g | 430 | 5,400 | 10,900 | 21,800 | 54,400 |
| Poker card | 8.5 g | 250 | 3,200 | 6,400 | 12,800 | 31,900 |

A fine magic hide poker card (**f=0.25**) handles **12,800 J**. The A4 paper sheet that flashes at **2,760 J** is outclassed by a much smaller card. The same **2,760 J** through that poker card is only about **54 K** rise.

Most of the gain is **f**. Mundane hide holds ~**12×** more than paper on a card but weighs ~**10×** more, so per gram it is only ~**1.3×** better. Fine magic hide reaches ~**1,500 J/g**, about **5×** paper per gram.

Thin magic hide (**0.3 mm**) keeps weight down: poker card ~**1.7 g** and ~**2,550 J** at **f=0.25** (~**10×** the paper card).

### Weight for 100 cards (1.5 mm hide)

- Poker: **850 g**
- Index: **1.45 kg**
- A6: **2.3 kg**
- Thin **0.3 mm** poker: **170 g**

## Consumable vs rechargeable magic hide

### Consumable quality

- Destroyed by the discharge itself, not only by burning
- Capacity above is a ceiling. A spell well under the ceiling can still consume the card
- End state can be ash, glowing crumble or shrivel (visual separate from paper ignition)
- Match spell power to card size so headroom is not wasted

### Rechargeable quality (high-quality magic hide)

If the hide withstands the heat of a cast without damage, it is reusable and needs mana recharge between uses.

- Reusable when rise per cast stays well under **250 K**. Safe working limit here: **100 K**
- Each cast drains stored mana by the energy channeled
- Overdriving past **250 K** destroys even a rechargeable card
- Repeated casts without cooling stack heat

### Safe reusable power per cast (J at 100 K rise, 1.5 mm hide)

| Format | Mundane (f=1) | Magic f=0.5 | Magic f=0.25 | Magic f=0.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A4 | 14,000 | 28,100 | 56,200 | 140,400 |
| A5 | 7,000 | 14,000 | 28,000 | 69,900 |
| A6 note card | 3,500 | 7,000 | 14,000 | 35,000 |
| Index card 3 × 5 in | 2,200 | 4,350 | 8,700 | 21,800 |
| Poker card | 1,300 | 2,550 | 5,100 | 12,800 |

### Tier summary

| Tier | Typical grade | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Paper rune | f=1.0 | Single use. Ignites at low power |
| Consumable hide | f=0.5 (common) | Single use. Ash or crumble. Higher power than paper |
| Rechargeable hide | f=0.25 to 0.1 (fine to rare) | Survives casts under the safe limit. Needs mana recharge |
| Overdriven rechargeable | any | Cast above 250 K rise → destroyed |
