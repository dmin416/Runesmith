# ART (Autonomic Response Testing)

Hub: `Science.md`.

**Premise (locked):** ART, muscle testing, dowsing and body lean / sway are the same family of valid readouts. They all report autonomic and tissue state before conscious awareness. Implications below follow from that premise.

## Gist

A healthy human nervous system receives and ranks weak field and resonance cues: magnetic orientation, nearby substances, environmental EMF and spatial threat (for example open water underfoot). The answer shows up as a change in motor tone: indicator muscle, rod or pendulum motion or a forward / back lean. Dysbiosis, neurological switching and heavy ANS load scramble the signal. Clear testing needs a body that is organized enough to answer.

## Readout family (same channel)

Different outward forms. Same ANS / biofield answer.

| Form | What moves | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **ART / muscle test** | Indicator muscle or finger ring (strong vs weak) | Substance, organ, field and yes/no challenges with an examiner |
| **Dowsing** | Rods, stick or pendulum | Locate water, objects, boundaries or rank yes/no at a distance |
| **Lean / sway** | Whole-body balance (forward vs back, or side sway) | Solo check: ask and feel which way the body drifts |

- Treat agreement across forms as corroboration, not separate magics.
- Switching and dysbiosis break all three the same way.
- Solo lean / sway needs the same clean question discipline as examiner ART. Leading intent still muddies the answer.

## Human ability

- **Detection without awareness:** toxins, pathogens, allergens, fields and other stressors register below conscious perception. The ANS shifts before symptoms show.
- **Motor as instrument:** indicator muscle, finger ring, dowsing tool or lean / sway reports that state in real time. A trained examiner or a self-tester reads the change.
- **Proximity / resonance:** a substance on or near the body can change the response. The body reads objects and fields through resonance or biofield interaction, not only by smell or touch.
- **Biofield reception:** tissues emit and receive ultraweak light and electromagnetic signals. The ANS integrates that traffic.
- **Organ-level map:** responses can localize to organs, systems and pathogens. The body keeps a detailed map of its own dysfunction.
- **Ultra-low threshold:** responses sit far below ordinary sensory floors (Omura-type reports include detection with applied skin forces under **1 g/mm²**).
- **Magnetic polarity:** in most body regions magnetic north tends to strengthen the indicator muscle and south tends to weaken it.
- **Surroundings and threat:** when organized, people can feel open danger (for example depth or void under a surface) and rank nearby stressors without instruments. This is ANS / biofield sensing, not mysticism bolted on later.
- **Individual calibration:** each ANS gives its own answer. Food, drug and environment compatibility are person-specific.
- **Goods quality:** rank foods, herbs, medicines, materials and crafted goods for **biocompatible** fit and for **potency** (how strong a real substance or remedy reads). Better lots and cleaner stock retest stronger for the person being tested. Potency here means biological / field charge of the item, not market price.

## Limitations (no magnetic / field information)

The channel reads **magnetic and related field / resonance information**, not arbitrary facts.

- If two options are **biologically and materially identical** to the sensor, there is nothing to rank. Example: face-down playing cards. Ink pattern on identical stock carries no differential magnetic or biofield signature the ANS can use. You cannot dowse or muscle-test which face is under the back.
- **Exception that is not useful:** if the subject already knows a face and focusing on it triggers a real ANS storm (terror, “I am dying” load), a tester can read **that body’s stress**, not the ink. At that point you can also see them stare or lock on the card. The channel is still reading physiology, not the print.
- Pure labels, codes, passwords and other symbolic distinctions fail the same way unless a real substance, organism, field, spatial threat or a known emotional / somatic charge differs.
- **Blinded checks** only work when the hidden difference is real in field or tissue terms (live vs dead source, substance A vs B, room load). Card-trick and number-guess protocols are outside scope and do not falsify the premise.

## What breaks the readout

- **Dysbiosis:** gut and systemic microbial imbalance muddy the signal. Retests jump or contradict.
- **Switching:** neurological disorganization (mixed laterality, unreliable indicator). Same challenge yields opposite answers until corrected.
- **Stacked load:** toxins, infections, scars, trauma, deficiency and chronic EMF push the ANS into noise. Sensitive or already ill people fail first.
- **Examiner error:** poor challenge design, leading pressure or an uncorrected subject produce false maps. The premise is that a clean protocol works, not that every casual test is true.
- **Empty challenge:** asking about something with no magnetic / field difference (card faces, pure symbols). Failure here is expected.

## Field and EMF load

- Pulsed RF and similar EMF register as stressors and bias the ANS toward sympathetic dominance. Testing near the source or its frequencies shows weakening.
- Response strength tracks proximity. Sleeping areas and rooms on a meter wall or wiring hotspot rank worst.
- Repeated bursts act as chronic load. Cumulative exposure matters more than any single weak pulse.
- ANS-governed systems take the hit first in story terms: sleep, heart rhythm feel, immune tone, hormones, detox and clear thinking.
- Sensitivity varies with baseline ANS state. Children and people with existing dysregulation show earlier and stronger test changes.

Do not hang plot or reference locks on red-cell membrane damage claims. Use ANS / muscle readout and symptom load instead.

## Practical use

- **Screening:** ART, BDORT, dowsing or lean / sway can rank rooms, substances, exposure and goods without meters.
- **Goods:** compare lots or items for biocompatibility (does this body accept it) and potency (how strong the sample reads). Use for food, remedies, reagents and materials when conventional labels do not settle the choice.
- **Mitigation check:** distance, shielding, removing a source or moving sleep space should retest stronger if the load was real.
- **Clearance:** retest after changes to confirm recovery and how much reduction is enough.
- **Blinded checks:** active vs inactive source or coded **real** samples should give consistent indicator changes across trained examiners when the subject is not switched. Do not use identical card faces or pure symbols as the blind.

Not a clinic profession first. Distinct from **Mana Sense** and from **Basic Perception** (eyes/ears/attention).

### Name

**Gut Check** (single skill, L1–L9). No Basic prefix and no Expert evolve form.

Rods, lean / sway and muscle tests are just ways the same gut answer shows. Formal ART / dowsing drills can level it. They are not the point of the skill.

**Attribute pads (draft):** **Willpower +1**, **Vitality +1** per level (hold the question / stay with the body). Int is optional flavor for deliberate testing; danger feel is Will + Vit.
