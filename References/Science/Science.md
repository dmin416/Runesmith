# Science

Earth-physics and rewrite cast anchors for story beats. Folder: `References/Science/`.

## Cast law (locked)

```
Useful (J) = mana × 10 × η(L) × μ(INT)
Mana = J / (10 × η(L) × μ(INT))
Mana/s = Power (W) / (10 × η(L) × μ(INT))
```

- **1 mana ≈ 10 J** is the locked standard for cast law and path formulas (`ManaCast.md`, `../Runes/Energy.md`, `../Runes/RuneSystem.md`).
- **η(L):** spell level efficiency. L1 = **0.3**, L2 = **1.0**, … L9 = **3.0** (`ManaCast.md`). Not a separate soft quality cap.
- **μ(INT):** `(INT / 15)^0.8`. INT 15 = 1×.
- Skill ranks for diagnosis / echolocation / craft precision cap at **L9**.

## Living resistance (raw mana vs people)

Vitality and the target's own magic resist raw mana manipulation aimed at their body or the air shell around them. There is **no cheap air-deny** on a resisting person. Vacuum / suffocate on a living target must be a **full-body shell sealed to the ground** (or equivalent sealed volume) unless the target has **no** ability to resist except sitting inside otherwise impenetrable armor. Same resistance principle applies to other body-directed raw-mana tricks (micro-compression kill paths, internal sonic harm without contact, etc.).

## Flight

No locked “flight is expensive” or “flight is cheap” fiat. Cost is whatever the chosen physical model’s power equation yields at the caster’s η and μ (`Flight.md`). Static Mana Hands **hold** rows stay grip/restraint only.
