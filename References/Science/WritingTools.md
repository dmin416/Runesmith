# Writing Tools and Graphite

Story canon for writing and drawing implements, plus Earth research used to keep the invent beats honest. Surfaces: `Paper.md`. Folder: `References/Science/`.

## Story lock (rewrite)

### What already exists on Terra

Common writing and drawing kit before Roland invents anything:

| Tool | Who uses it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Quill** | Most scribes, clerks, Roland | Goose (or similar) feather + ink. Messy; needs constant refill. Roland uses **Mana Hands** to refill while focused (multitask practice). |
| **Soft metal stylus** | Scholars, draftsmen | Lead / soft metal rods for faint lines (Roman *stylus plumbeus* analog). Not a wood-cased pencil. |
| **Chalk** | Teachers, drafts, street marks | Natural chalks (red / black / white). Soft shading; smudges. |
| **Charcoal** | Artists, rough layout | Burnt twigs; easy wipe; dirty. |
| **Silverpoint / metalpoint** | Fine artists (rare) | Needs prepared ground; hard to erase. |
| **Fountain pen** | **Wealthy** only | Possible among rich clerks and nobles. Rare and costly. Not mass-market. |
| **Pencil (wood + graphite core)** | **Does not exist** as a common good | Roland invents it to make schematic work easier. |

Carwen is a poor place for specialty craft supplies. Edelgard has better shops for quills, ink, paper and soft-metal styluses but **no ready pencil** and **no large native graphite deposit** in the mountain mines.

### Roland invent path

1. **Ch 19 want:** Quill is fine for ink work and worse for focused schematic redraws. He wants a pencil once he has Edelgard access.
2. **No Borrowdale windfall:** Edelgard mines are iron / deeper rare metals (`../World/Places.md`). They do **not** yield large lump or flake graphite beds he can just saw into sticks.
3. **Make graphite:** He synthesizes usable graphite (powdered carbon + clay / binder, kiln or heat-rune fire; Conté-style cores). Exact recipe locks when the craft chapter is written. Heat runes and forge work make the high-temperature step plausible without Earth industrial plant.
4. **Ned toxin-free spike:** A later Ned spike evolution / variant that is **toxin-free**. Shed or cut tips become clean hard cores (or core molds / punches) for pencil leads. Poisoned spikes are useless for writing tools people put near their mouths and skin.
5. **Pencil:** Wood (or equivalent) case + synthetic graphite core. First personal use for schematics; later a sellable civilian product.
6. **Pen (after metalworking):** Once he has real smith / precision metal time, he invents a practical pen (steel-nib dip first, then a better sealed / fountain or ball design). See also `../Ideas.md` civilian product shortlist (**ball pen** sits after steel process).

## Earth research (reference only)

Not copied into Terra wholesale. Use for timelines, materials and what “inventing a pencil” actually requires.

### Pencil history (Earth)

- **Ancient:** Soft lead stylus for faint marks; lead disks for ruling.
- **1560s Borrowdale:** Pure lump graphite sawed into sticks; wrapped in string or set in wood. Mistaken for lead (why cores are still called “lead”).
- **Late 1500s:** Early wooden-cased pencils (hollowed wood + graphite).
- **1795 Conté:** Powdered graphite + clay, kiln-fired. Clay ratio sets hardness. Makes pencils cheap and consistent without solid lump deposits.
- **1830s+:** Mass wood pencils in North America; **1858** eraser attached; mid-1800s brands standardize.

### Renaissance kit (Earth) before common pencils

Silverpoint, natural chalks (sanguine / black / white), charcoal, quill + iron gall or bistre, brush wash, wax tablets. Graphite sticks appear late (Gesner 1565) as a novelty.

### Graphite geology (Earth)

| Type | Host | Terrain / mining |
|---|---|---|
| **Flake** | Metamorphic schist, gneiss, quartzite, marble | Open pits; tropical laterite or northern shield |
| **Amorphous** | Metamorphosed coal seams | Coal basins |
| **Vein (lump)** | Rare high-purity veins in ancient basement | Underground shafts |
| **Borrowdale** | Unusual volcanic pipe pockets | Small mountain tunnels; exhausted |

Common companions: pyrite / pyrrhotite (rusty outcrops), quartz / mica / feldspar / garnet, sometimes rutile, vanadium, base metals in black-shale precursors, rarely gold or uranium in graphitic belts.

**Story use:** Edelgard’s iron-mountain geology is a bad match for a Borrowdale windfall. Roland’s Conté-style **synthetic** route is the intended path, not discovering a secret graphite mine under the city.

### Synthetic graphite (Earth pointers for craft beats)

- Conté: graphite powder + clay, fire in kiln, grade by clay ratio.
- Later industrial synthetic graphite (Acheson-type) needs very high heat on carbon feedstock. Heat runes / forge magic can stand in for the furnace once he can hold temperature and atmosphere.
- Binder + wood casing + sharpening = a usable pencil without a natural lump deposit.
