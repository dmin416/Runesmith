# Places

Locations from the rewrite. First-seen chapter is for this rewrite, not the source dump alone.

## Earth

### Medical practice
First seen: Chapter 1
Clinic building with an office and a separate lab unit behind it, built for Roland a few years ago. He is the only technician.

### Roland's apartment
First seen: Chapter 1
Directly behind the office. He walks around the block instead of jumping the fence. Hobby bench is here. Ascension space (Chapter 5) recreates the building and unit **19** (top floor): pitch-black void outside, sealed photocopied doors, working gaming PC and mattress hole from memory. Not the real Earth apartment.

### Ascension space (first class)
First seen: Chapter 5
Personal trial realm shaped from the user’s memories. Roland’s is his old Earth apartment building plus a VR-headset tutorial field (grassy plain, training dummy, mage clone). Real body stays in place; long subjective time can equal seconds outside. First crystal use grants class; crystal becomes dust after.

## Terra

World with **two moons** (one red, one blue). First noted: Chapter 4 night sky.

### Kingdom of Caldris
First seen: Chapter 2
Human-majority kingdom. Roland wakes here as a son of House Arden. Fairly large by Earth comparison. Bordered by four other countries on the continental map he finds. Chapter 24: **24-hour** day cycle; calendar has **13 months** (not 12).

### Arden estate / mansion
First seen: Chapter 2
Baron Arden's home. Roland's room starts on the top floor in a side wing. After Mana Sense standing rises (Chapter 4), he moves into better quarters in the family inner circle. Servants bow; brothers resent him. Fields outside use Trox for plowing. Mansion **dungeon** holds training goblins for the age-9 bravery test and weekly clears. Hedge gap near the ladies’ flower garden is Roland’s sandwich hideout (Chapter 7; Lucienne finds it).

### Arden library
First seen: Chapter 3
Old dusty wing of the mansion. Rarely used by the family. Holds history, geography, a continental map and the magic / class books Roland trains from when the house will not hire a tutor.

### Arden dining room
First seen: Chapter 3
Family meals with the Baron, wives and children. Roland sits furthest back by rank.

### Carwen
First seen: Chapter 7
Mid-kingdom walled adventurer town with a dungeon nearby. Mixed Victorian-ish civilian clothes and full-plate knights. Roland arrives by mana train after Lustile is denied. Goblin forest and farms sit nearby.

### Lustile
First seen: Chapter 6 (named; not visited)
City with an academy of arts. Trains Weaponsmiths; has mage facilities and a library. Roland pitches it for safe Blacksmith / Scrybe / mage progress. Wentworth refuses; he must raise levels as a working low-level mage first.

### Mana-stone trains / airships
First seen: Chapter 7
Kingdom rail looks like old steam trains but runs on mana stones. Pulls many passenger and freight wagons; multi-day rides are slow and noisy. Flying airships / fortresses exist and cost far more (above Baron Wentworth’s usual spend). Master Smiths can work on airships (book talk).

### Carwen cabin
First seen: Chapter 7
Rundown forest cabin about 30 minutes outside Carwen by coach. Arden lodging only. Letter: no further money; army enlistment if he quits. Well ~10 minutes east toward the city; spring deeper west (animals). Straw/hay mattress, stove, chairs, wooden desk, animal pelts. Dusty, moldy and windy. Weak lock. Chapter 8: Roland buries a stolen class-up crystal unmarked nearby.

### Carwen Adventurer's Guild
First seen: Chapter 8
Registration hall and job board. Sign: two axes flanking a longsword. Saloon-style doors. Female receptionists (**Miss Cellica** handles Roland's Bronze card and Chapter 18 Bronze→Steel update); bulletin board by rank; tables with food service while loot is appraised. Measuring crystal for registration and rank-ups. Roland registers as bronze under his first name only. Building visible from the Chapter 7 coach ride into town. Chapter 18 nickname talk: Little Goblin Slayer Emperor (only man in the three-girl party).

### Carwen library
First seen: Chapter 9
Sells a local area map for 1 small silver. Roland notes Identify rises with reading here and plans to return for books.

### Carwen (city notes)
Chapter 8–9: small walled town (monster defense). Gate fee 5 large copper; adventurer card skips exit fee. Stone-block main road toward the city lord’s castle. Mostly stone buildings with red tile roofs. Mixed races inside the guild (elves, dwarves, Goliaths, Dragon-kin). Farm fringe and goblin forest outside.

### Carwen inn
First seen: Chapter 9
Cheap inn where Roland eats his first town meal and later rents a room upstairs during the goblin grind (Chapter 10). Large waitress in festival-style wench dress; muscular one-eyed scarred bartender. Bard with lute. First meal: porridge + jerky for 7 large copper (bland). Lodging 2 small silver/night; +5 large copper breakfast; monthly rate about **5%** off (Chapter 13). Keepers named Hilde (bar lady) and Boris (bartender husband).

### Carwen dungeon
First seen: Chapter 10 (entry planned; first delve Chapter 11)
Active dungeon near town. Bronze rank cannot enter alone. Roland joins Rebecca's party to go in. Entrance: torch-lit stone corridors (truck-wide) with low dungeon rats before the real floors. Floor 1 is the Emerald Wilderness, a Steel-rank jungle hunting ground under an artificial sun (kilometers-wide open space; peculiar mana density). Floor 2 is similar forest with higher group-spawn chance and Needle Worms. Floor 3: more vicious multi-type fights; Wereboars are the standout (gorilla body, boar head). Stairs between floors are about **5 m** wide and monster-safe (retreat zone; torches may be magical). Floors continue to a 10th-floor boss with guild appointment and a week respawn; strong parties or guilds often hog the boss room for materials. Chapter 15 lore: dungeons may be living (dungeon core); they absorb dead monsters and humans as nourishment and spawn more foes. West woods outside Carwen have few scavenger monsters for body disposal.

### The Iron Flagon
First seen: Chapter 12 (party binge); named Chapter 17
Carwen tavern. Hard liquor only (below 20% frowned on). Site of Roland's first drunk night with the party. Reyna gets chatty when drunk.

### Edelgard
First seen: Chapter 10 (named as goal); arrives Chapter 19
Crafting-focused mountain city with mines, gorge bridges and heavy dwarf/gnome presence. Rock and brick buildings; little wood. No local dungeon. Goods ship to adventurer towns like Carwen. Craftspeople prefer it over living next to an active dungeon. Adventurers work as hired muscle and wild-monster clearers. Climate colder / autumn-like at altitude. Roland picks it for Blacksmith / Scrybe apprenticeship facilities. Guild sells city maps with a **5%** Steel-rank discount. Chapter 25: merchant **council** of top store/smith owners meets every three months; recruits promising talent or crushes independent upstarts.

### The Singing Crow Inn
First seen: Chapter 19
Edelgard inn marked for Bronze and Steel adventurers. Four floors. Pricier than Carwen lodging; straw mattress rooms. Helci works here as a half-gnome waitress (first bump at the door; boss calls her by name). Chapter 24: innkeeper tells staff not to bother the robed lodger (cult / ritualist rumor).

### Exeor's Magic Emporium
First seen: Chapter 20
Large three-story Edelgard magic shop. Sign: two potions, scroll and staff. Auto-opening doors. Floors include Manager, Potions and Scrolls and Spells. Clerk **Zilyana** (golden-hair elf in Exeor robe; looks late teens, may be much older). Manager is an advanced Alchemist gnome (~**120 cm**, taller than many city gnomes; gray beard, turban, wart on left nostril, wooden pipe; busy chemist-set office with a furnace; later regional manager of a chain with capital HQ; on merchant council). Chapter 20: dusty runic scroll shelf (Orb of Light / Fire Arrow / Aqua Ball / Frost Bolt) priced ~6–7× regular scrolls because Runesmith craft; first offered a **six-year** cursed magic scrybe contract (no outside sales; waive learned-spell rights; mana signature; breach curse). Roland hides Runic Mana Scrybe and walks. Chapter 25: manager Identifies him as Runic Mana Scrybe L10 (saw it on first visit; Fire Arrow auction trail); offers **3-year** exclusive deal (workspace, cheaper mats, 15% store cut, self-craft OK, −30% mana breach curse, merchant-council protection). Roland takes a copy overnight (will keep comet brand + hide identity). Chapter 26: signs (mana signature; identity secret; min price vs auction / no undersell; 3 years); basement office (dim, dungeon-like; key; nights OK; bed; free rent); manager confirmed as regional chain manager (capital HQ; name withheld); Zilyana "**big sis**"; status-hide armband → reads as ordinary Mana Scrybe; basic mats supplied (research samples on him); min **10 common scrolls/week** (+ bonus); buys out city runic samples (elemental arrow/bolt variants). Chapter 27: ~1 year in; weekends + 25 leave days/year; brand nicknames Crimson Comet / Red Star / Scarlet Runesmith; High/Highest scrolls shipped out-city; Fire Arrow 5–6/day compacted. Chapter 35–36: after Runic Blacksmith change, manager gives Roland a bare unused warehouse-workshop in the lower city (rent; still min 10 scrolls/week; coal forge, rusty basics, moldy office bedroom; Exeor-marked so thieves avoid it). Chapter 37: paid thugs still target the Southtown warehouse for a trash-and-kill job. Chapter 39: Zilyana reports the attack; manager sends Zilyana and Ziron into Rat Plaza and places a crystal-mirror call for outside help. Chapter 41: bails Roland, guards/repairs workshop, offers barrier, gifts Runecraft + Ethereal Pathways skill books, opens contract renegotiation. Chapter 42: +1 year contract, raise, lower store cut, harm-cancel fine, lesser mana-regen ring (~15%), moves Roland to a second similar warehouse with better tools; Helci hired as part-time assistant. Chapter 50: manager skips merchant council for alchemy; Zilyana false-alarms a door kick while Roland brings slotted sword; Identify cliffhanger on the blade. Chapter 51: cult pair hits Exeor; greater illusion traps Roland/Zilyana/manager; Roland wakes mid-assault and aims Fire Arrow paddle at the helix device. Chapter 52: helix destroyed; store wrecked by Abyssal Warlock tentacles; moon-elf male fights transformed Warlock outside; Roland cursed and plans to leave Edelgard. Chapter 53: store half-ruined; company mercs on site; Roland packs workshop and heads for Hightown payout. Chapter 54: Marlo Grimboodle named; pays Roland out and flees toward capital.

### Southtown
First seen: Chapter 37
Lower-income district of Edelgard with slums. Roland's Exeor warehouse-workshop is here. City guards rarely patrol unless ordered. Chapter 38: Thieves Guild home invasion (kill order, then burn).

### Thieves Guild
First seen: Chapter 38
Hidden city organization. Takes jobs Adventurer's Guild won't. Entry requires Thief or Bandit class. Some members also hold adventurer cards. Edelgard cell loses a tier 2 Rogue (~L60+) on Roland's warehouse job. Chapter 39: HQ accessed via sewers / Rat Plaza; Zilyana and Ziron sent to force the client's name. Chapter 40: run by Reinald the Razor (Master Blade-Rogue). Prior guild master was T3 prestige. Customer ledger ties Southtown hit to the same patron behind restaurant poison and caravan sabotage. Zilyana warns them off Exeor workers.

### Temple of Solaria / Church of the Sun
First seen: Chapter 19 (station priests); temple proper Chapter 53
Sun-goddess faith (yellow sun robes, bells, collection plates). Path talk: Acolyte → Cleric → Paladin. Churches produce class-change stones (claimed god-gift; multiple churches keep prices from spiking). Orphanages: kids choose acolyte life or leave by age **14**; stones are not free for orphans (Chapter 24 Helci). Chapter 19: Roland tips copper at the Edelgard station. Chapter 53: Edelgard temple; T3 High Priest removes Abyss curse (9 small gold). Moon elves on poor terms with this church.

### Hightown
First seen: Chapter 39
Richest district of Edelgard. Exeor gnome manager keeps a grey-stone residence with yellow pine trim here. Chapter 49: cult pair watches a mansion meeting from the roofs (strike pending). Chapter 50: Abyssal Warlock and dark-elf partner massacre merchant council in a Hightown meeting room via red double-helix greater illusion; eye-tentacle occult mark left; next target Exeor gnome (two council members still alive). Chapter 54: Marlo packing at grey-stone residence; Roland collects severance.

### Flower district
First seen: Chapter 39
Edelgard red-light district (whorehouses). Owned by a merchant-council member. Ziron often found here.

### Rat Plaza
First seen: Chapter 39
Hidden black-market web under Edelgard, entered through the sewers (rotating wall / sewage lever). Stolen goods, forbidden body parts, slave and assassin factions; Thieves Guild zone deeper in.

### Balmyr's Weapons
First seen: Chapter 44
Edelgard weapon shop (sword and ax sign). Owner is a Runesmith on the Weaponsmith path. Ground floor: mundane iron/steel. Upper floor: Silver+ enchanted commons. Lesser rune display cases on the floor Roland uses for Debugger borrowing.

### Libra Auction House
First seen: Chapter 21
Edelgard auction house marked by a scale sign. Curvy elf barker rings a bell and advertises night auctions. Entrance fee **2 small silver** (weeds window shoppers; waived for sellers with plaque). Takes stock by day and auctions at night. Multiple ranked stages (common/grand vs higher; no grand sword before lesser potion). Rejects items below a price floor. Appraiser **Percival** (analytic class with appraisal/ID; no mana tool needed) and assistant **Cynthia**. Auctioneer beauty in a red dress; paddle bidding; count of three. First-timer seller fee **25%**, can fall to **10%**. Sellers get a numbered plaque and may watch from the back; payout only after the night auction ends (buyers who refuse are reported to city guards). Chapter 22: Roland's comet-logo Common Fire Arrow set (8 High + 2 Highest) sold as two boxes of five for 4L+5S and 4L+7S (~1 SG gross). Chapter 23: seller **#64** nets **6L+9S**; later weekly ~10 Fire Arrow scrolls ≈ 1 SG.

### Manstos Grotto
First seen: Chapter 27
Iron ore mine near Edelgard with rarer metals deeper in. Beside a wide river; natural entrance partly submerged; separate landside entrance. Miners breached a Myrmeke nest; closed and barricaded after casualties. Guild expedition of ~20 under Wells; Party 4 enters an assigned shaft in Chapter 29, is trapped by a cave-in in Chapter 30, kills a Myrmeke Soldier L53 in Chapter 31 and escapes through the blasted old grotto seal in Chapter 32 after Wells collapses the shafts from outside. Myrmeke Queen L163 appears at the water exit.

### Stegend
First seen: Chapter 27
City named on a convoy-protection listing Roland rejects as too far.

### Carwen train station
First seen: Chapter 7 (arrival); Chapter 18 (departure)
Magic train hub. Roland arrives alone for Carwen lodging; later leaves after farewell with Rebecca, Sahildr and Reyna.

### Dragnis Island
First seen: Chapter 55 (named; destination); Chapter 67 (landed)
Large southern Caldris island under an aristocrat Duke. Central volcano + S-rank main dungeon (dragon last boss); five magus towers barrier. Coast warm/green. Smaller dungeons link toward the main. Roland targets Albrook boom town by a months-old side dungeon. Chapter 67: arrives via closest island port town (unnamed; no adventurer guild; travel agency); overnight then caravan inland toward Albrook. Export boom in mana stones and minerals.

### Albrook
First seen: Chapter 55 (named; destination); Chapter 68 (arrived)
Small town in a Dragnis Island valley by a new volcanic dungeon (smoke from a mountain that burst through recently; ~6 months old by Chapter 71). Early boom: wall under construction; south gate fee; poor quarter → traders' street → market square. Red-brick buildings; glass windows rarer toward the edges. Absentee noble; commoner mayor/manager. Adventurer guild renovating a large house (sign going up). Roland registers as Wayland (Chapter 68: claims Steel; Chapter 70: issued Bronze for lack of prior-card info; dungeon free entry). Chapter 69–70: buys abandoned red-brick farmhouse + log shack ~40 min outside town (>100 SG; large cellar; skips triple-price extra farmland; ~half acre). Plans cellar runic workshop + dummy shed smithy; renovation firm booked ≥3 weeks out. Chapter 77 (~1 year later): log+barbed wall; thieves hit dummy shed; Solaria church; dungeon entrance card checks after a farmer dies; potion cart → fixed store; Roland known as crimson-armored craftsman / "disciple."

### Albrook Dungeon
First seen: Chapter 71 (entered)
Labyrinth-type volcanic dungeon believed linked to the main Dragnis Infernal Dragon Dungeon (S-rank supervolcano; dragon unbeaten). Unrated; possible high-tier spill if connected. Flaming-skull gate (~5 m; spell enchantments, no runes on Debugger). Safe hub with corridors; monsters cannot leave dungeon bounds. Floor maps sold by guild; periodic loot chests; heat rises deeper; mining section past labyrinth. ~10 labyrinth floors (~+5 monster level each; floor 10 ~L50 / some T2). Floor 1: Fire Slimes, Fiery Skeletons (cap ~L10), Baby Salamanders named; deeper: Crimson Giant Rats, Baby Salamanders fought (~floor 4). Floor 7: magic trap rooms (sealed stone walls + ceiling spawn holes) with Lesser Troglodytes. Crowded upper floors with bronze/steel youths. Expansions can reshape terrain. Packing Mules support long expeditions.

### Happy Rooster
First seen: Chapter 73
Albrook poultry restaurant. Commoner seasoning mostly sugar, pepper and salt. Roland eats here while waiting on city build-out.

### Albrook auction house
First seen: Chapter 73 (under renovation)
Not open yet; adventurers still forced to sell through the guild until it finishes.

### Luden
First seen: Chapter 56 (named; destination)
Southern port city. Roland books caravan passage from a red-brick trade hub after the Edelgard train. Chapter 65: arrives with bandit survivors; sells loot; books Dragnis/Albrook sail via Isabela's ship.

### Hatfordian–Caldris border fortress
First seen: Chapter 67
Black-stone fortress between mountain peaks on the Caldris side of the Hatfordian Empire border. Ceasefire after years of skirmishes. Noble house leaders (incl. Viscount Godwin) released to return to fiefs. Armand Dreux hosts Silver Wolf (Wentworth Arden) and his soldiers en route home.
