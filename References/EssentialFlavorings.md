# Essential Flavorings

**Earth research anchors** for adventure cooking. Salt is assumed under everything below. ★ marks the core ten. Map flavor to Caldris; do not paste Earth spice-route or dish names into prose as if they are local unless the setting already has them. Pairs with `EssentialIngredients.md`.

## Savory Base
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Garlic | The universal base. Savory backbone for nearly every cuisine | meat, vegetables, soups, sauces, marinades | Raw is sharp. Roasted is sweet. Burnt is bitter. Add after onions and before liquid |
| ★ Onion | The universal sweet base. Sweetness and body once cooked down | soups, stews, braises, skewers, roasted vegetables | Slow cooking turns it sweet. Raw slices add crunch as a topping |
| Tomato | Savory sweet-sour base. Adds umami and body | stews, sauces, braises, chili | Paste and dried forms travel well |

## Heat and Bite
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Chili pepper | The universal heat. Heat, color and fruity depth depending on the type | meat, beans, eggs, noodles, marinades, dry rubs | Toast dried chilies briefly to wake the aroma. Black means burnt |
| ★ Black pepper | The universal bite. Clean sharpness that cuts through fat | everything savory | Crack it fresh. Add a second round at the end |
| ★ Ginger | The universal warm zing. Peppery warmth that suppresses off flavors | marinades, stir fries, soups, curries, braises | Grate fresh into marinades for game and organ meat |

## Gamey Meat and Roasts
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Cumin | The universal gamey herb. Earthy warmth that balances strong meat flavor | lamb, goat, boar, venison, beans, root vegetables, skewers | Toast whole seed and grind coarse. Rendered fat carries it best |
| ★ Rosemary | The universal meat herb. Piney, resinous punch | lamb, pork, boar, chicken, potatoes, flatbread | Chop fine or use a sprig as a basting brush. It burns quickly over direct flame |
| Wine | Deglazer. Adds acid and fruit and tames gamey notes | pan sauces, braises, wild meat | Reduce it before adding other liquid |

## Soups and Stews
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Thyme | The universal soup herb. Gentle, savory, slightly minty background | soups, stews, stocks, poultry, roasted vegetables | Add early. It holds up to long simmering and never turns bitter |

## Freshness and Acid
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Parsley | The universal freshness. Green, clean finish that brightens heavy food | finishing almost anything, chimichurri, gremolata, salsa verde | Add at the end. Stems carry more flavor and go into stocks. Caldris Ch 13: **parsleaves** (Ned’s mild-poison stash leaves) smell like parsley; see `Food.md` |
| ★ Lemon | The universal lift. Acid that brightens flavor and cuts richness | fish, grilled meat, greens, potatoes, beans, sauces | Juice at the end and zest for aroma |
| Vinegar | The universal acid. Cuts fat and preserves | marinades, pickles, mops, sauces | Trail substitute for lemon |

## Umami and Salt
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soy sauce | Salt and umami. Fermented savory note | marinades, glazes, stir fries, soups | Counts as part of the salt |
| Fish sauce | Hidden umami. Adds depth with no fishy taste | marinades, soups, sauces | A small splash is enough |

## Smoke
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood smoke | Campfire flavor from hardwood coals | meat, vegetables, salt | Use hardwood only |
| Smoked paprika | Campfire flavor and color without a fire | rubs, stews, eggs | Works indoors when there is no fire |

## Richness, Sweet and Finish
| Flavoring | Universal role | Use with | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Butter | Richness. Rounds sharp flavors and browns nutty | sauces, vegetables, pan-fried meat | Ghee and lard fill the same role |
| Honey | Sweet balance. Rounds heat and acid and caramelizes | glazes, grilled meat, spicy dishes | Sugar and maple syrup fill the same role |
| Toasted sesame | Nutty finish | noodles, greens, dressings | Add oil or seeds at the end |

## Quick Pairing Map

| Need | Reach for |
|---|---|
| Savory base | garlic, onion, tomato |
| Heat | chili, black pepper, ginger |
| Gamey meat | cumin, rosemary, ginger, wine |
| Soup or stew | thyme, onion, garlic |
| Roast or grill | rosemary, garlic, black pepper |
| Freshness | parsley, lemon |
| Acid | vinegar, lemon, wine |
| Umami | tomato, soy sauce, fish sauce |
| Smoke | wood smoke, smoked paprika |
| Richness | butter, toasted sesame |
| Sweet balance | honey |
