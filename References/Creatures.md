### Goblin
First seen: Chapter 4
Common early foe. Roland’s first kill is a **Goblin L1** (HP 117/117) in the Arden mansion dungeon bravery test. About **120 cm** tall, green, rusty short blade. Estate training stock for age-9 tests, weekly **1v1** clears into the pre-class bank, then weekly **2× L1** clears for ~3 months after Mage (Chapter 7 finale still uses the pen). Kill XP **50** (49+1; RaceMult 1.0); First Kill achievement adds **+200**. Low chance of a tiny **mana stone** / monster core (rice-grain size in Chapter 7). Live in groups with a strength hierarchy; chiefs are often evolved soldiers or shamans. Chapter 9 Carwen farm-forest: L2–L5 trash with clubs/spears; tribal alarm screams; Hobgoblin chiefs feared. Chapter 10: darker-green nest leaders; Roland snipes from ~100 m with Mana Arrow; guerrilla lure tactics clear many nests over three months. Bounty proof: left ear.

### Dungeon Rat
First seen: Chapter 11
Giant rat in Carwen dungeon entrance corridors. Weaker than a common goblin. Roland one-shots one with Mana Arrow on his first party delve.

### Spiked Boar
First seen: Chapter 11
Large muscular boar with thick tusks and bone spikes on the spine. Common floor-1 Emerald Wilderness foe. Charges tanks; party loot includes tusks, spine spikes and leather (meat skipped: tough and bad tasting). Mana stones often sit in the chest and run larger than common goblin stones (closer to goblin-leader size) because of dungeon mana density.

### Needle Worm
First seen: Chapter 12
Leaf-green spiky caterpillar ambusher on Carwen Dungeon floor 2. Camouflages in trees; flings itself headfirst. Green blood and strong smell. Tiny mana stones; spikes sell poorly. One-trick ambush threat, especially while the party is busy with boars.

### Wereboar
First seen: Chapter 12 (named as floor-3 target); fought Chapter 13
Floor-3 Carwen Dungeon foe. Gorilla-like body with a boar head. Strongest common threat at that depth for Roland's party. Sahildr's lesser-impact hammer can one-shot or incap with a timed mana blast; high mana cost makes it situational.

### Goblin Leader / Goblin King
First seen: Chapter 11 (size comparison only; spiked-boar stone ~goblin-leader size)
Boss or elite invasive goblin; king-tier variants can trigger culls.

### Mountain Goblin
First seen: Chapter 23
Pale goblin variant in Edelgard's mountain forest (~2h walk from city). Slightly taller and tougher-hided than green lowland goblins. Lean/muscular; wooden clubs and short swords. Alarm screams call packs. Rarely hit carriages. Late second Tier 1 is the fair fight band; surrounding is deadly. Evolved variants are a hard stop for early T1s. Roland hunts them with a scrap Fire Arrow grimoire; five chase Helci toward a ~20 m cliff (Ch 23–24).

### Myrmeke (Worker / Soldier)
First seen: Chapter 30
Monster ants in mines. Dog-sized workers swarm; advanced workers spit acid. Soldiers are horse-sized second evolutions (past L50), tier-2 threat; Party 4 kills a Myrmeke Soldier L53 in Chapter 31. Orson names them Myrmekes when they meet the first worker in Manstos Grotto.

### Myrmeke Queen
First seen: Chapter 32
Huge tier-3 nest boss. Several times Soldier size; long abdomen; fills cavern. Identified as Myrmeke Queen L163 (HP ????/????). Party escapes via blasted river tunnels; Roland's fire-pelt volley into her open mouth forces her back.

### Fire Slime
First seen: Chapter 71
Semi-transparent ball with a red core. Spits heated liquid that can melt skin. Physical hits fail on the body; mana-infused weapons or spells kill without destroying the sellable core. Weak but swarm fast. Early Albrook Dungeon corridor fodder.

### Fiery Skeleton
First seen: Chapter 72
Flaming skeleton dragging a longsword. Slow telegraphed swings; ~L10 floor-1 cap. Drops small eye-socket mana stone; bone dust possibly alchemical. Fire resists while burning.

### Baby Salamander
First seen: Chapter 72 (named); Chapter 75 (fought)
Iguana-like fire lizard ~wolf-sized on Albrook floor 4 (~L21). Fire breath; stationary after exhaling. Mana Thrust / shield bash killable if breath is blocked. Rumored to evolve toward fire drakes / fire wyverns with levels.

### Crimson Giant Rat
First seen: Chapter 75
Dark crimson giant rats ~medium dog size (~L14). Somewhat fire-resistant vs Carwen rats. Pack hunters; low XP; no mana stones; poor tooth/claw market value.

### Lesser Troglodyte
First seen: Chapter 76
Crimson-scaled reptilian humanoids (~170 cm+). Raptor legs; frilled crowns; oversized second claw finger. Floor 7 magic trap room spawns waves from ceiling holes. Drop mana stones.

### Lesser Spiked Devil
First seen: Chapter 79
Green fiends slightly taller than Pale Imps. Body spikes weep green poison liquid; can eject thorns. Partial grease-fire resist. Runesmith Lord trial wave 2 (~L40).

### Pale Imp
First seen: Chapter 79
Goblin-sized pale/white fiends: bat wings (poor flight), barbed tail, twisted horns. Demon line starters. Fire-weak vs normal imps. Trial wave 1 ~L30 (~30 count); later waves +5 level. Skin + black blood work as crude scroll media.

### Chain Devil
First seen: Chapter 80
~2 m crimson burned muscular devil. Body wrapped in chains; hooked arm chains. Slow L65 Tier 2. Runesmith Lord trial final gate. Survives edge of lightning web; killed by kneecap + head smash.

### Hell Hound
First seen: Chapter 80 (Runesmith Lord trial); later platinum test forest
Nimble fire-resist pack dogs; magical fire in bite. Smell buried flesh scrolls. Trial wave 3+ with final swarm.
