## Build-a-bear

Parts kit only. Derived products are in **Derived runes** below.

Swap converters / fills onto known shapes. Beats ratio surgery. Lazy wrong ratios still Diagnosis-readable unless the paper burns. Freeze-flip (invert fire → ice) fails. Contact triggers stay Detonation-family, not on Mana Bolt.

### Rough seat order

1. **Mana / energy input** (how much mana the spine accepts; own rune parts)
2. **Converter / fill** (what the mana becomes)
3. **Shape** (geometry only: sphere ladder **Bolt → Orb → Ball**, or Arrow body)
4. **Compression / restraint** (separate part; mana band it can hold or squeeze)
5. **Optional extras** only when he has a sample (intangible leash, contact trigger, tip concentration, tip location, **kinetic**, **mass**, direction of motion, …)

**Input, shape, and compression are different parts.** Never treat Bolt / Orb / Ball as names for soft hold, squeeze bands, or input runes. Shape = packet form and vessel grade. Compression = how hard the hold/squeeze is. Input = how much mana/energy the spine takes in. Upsizing compression alone without a matching seat tends to implode.

**Sphere ladder (shape only):** Bolt → Orb → Ball are the **same sphere geometry** at rising complexity / vessel capacity. That ladder is **not** a compression list.

**Fire Orb vs Orb of Light:** Fire Orb is truly simple (fire + **Orb** shape). Orb of Light adds an **intangible leash** to a relative object so the light can follow. That leash is not kinetic shove and not attack seek-homing. It is a soft relative attach.
