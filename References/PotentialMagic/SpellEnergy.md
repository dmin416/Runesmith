# Spell Energy

Hub: `PotentialMagic.md`. Cast law: `../Runes/Energy.md`. Science hub: `../World/Science/Science.md`.

Rough future-spell energy rungs. **Effect** is Useful joules delivered. **Mana (ημ 1)** = Effect / 10. Scale by η(L) and μ(INT) for a real caster.

Named early spells (Bolt, Arrow, Shield, Heat, Hands) use live tables in `../World/Science/Energy/ManaCast.md` and `../Combat/Spells.md`, not this ladder.
