# Piercing

Draft. Mostly piercing. A slow dense ball drills a clean straight channel about its own width. No blast or fragmentation occurs.

Depth rule: penetration ≈ kinetic energy ÷ (target resisting stress × frontal area). Density shrinks the frontal area per kilogram so the depth grows.

Example: a 1 cm ball at 4.1 kg and 100 m/s (20.5 kJ)

| Target | Resisting stress | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Flesh | ~2.5 MPa | Passes straight through. Deposits ~60 J per 30 cm |
| Wood | ~30 MPa | ~9 m deep |
| Concrete | ~150 MPa | ~1.7 m deep |
| Armor steel | ~2 GPa | ~13 cm deep |

## Special behaviors

- **Overpenetration:** damage equals the energy the target resists. Through 30 cm of body the deposit is ~60 J at 100 m/s, ~0.7 kJ at Mach 0.98 and ~6 kJ at Mach 3 regardless of mass. Extra mass adds no damage to a target the ball exits.
- **Momentum push:** applies only when the ball stops inside the target. The 4.1 kg ball at 100 m/s carries 410 kg·m/s and shoves an 80 kg person at about 5 m/s.
- **Back-face break (spall):** concrete, stone and glass blow out a cone on the exit side that is wider than the entry hole. Thin plates lose a punched plug.
- **Intact projectile:** below about 1.5 km/s the ball stays whole and drills. Above that metal targets flow like fluid and the ball erodes.
- **Punch vs shove:** a small dense ball punches through. A large heavy object at the same energy shoves and crushes so structures collapse rather than get drilled.
