# Piercing

Hub: `PotentialMagic.md`. Related: `MassBoost.md`, `SuperEnergyPowder.md`, `../World/Science/Energy/ManaCast.md` (Arrow punch / hardness).

Mostly piercing. A slow dense ball drills a clean channel about its own width. No blast or fragmentation.

Depth ≈ kinetic energy ÷ (resisting stress × frontal area). Higher density shrinks area per kilogram so depth grows.

Example: 1 cm ball, **4.1 kg** (×**1,000** mass-boosted; plain ball is **4.1 g**, see `MassBoost.md`), 100 m/s → KE = **20.5 kJ** → **2,050 mana** at ημ 1 (or less at high ημ).

| Target | Resisting stress | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Flesh | ~2.5 MPa | Passes through. Deposits ~**60 J** per 30 cm (constant resisting force; speed does not change deposited energy over a fixed path) |
| Wood | ~30 MPa | ~9 m deep |
| Concrete | ~150 MPa | ~1.7 m deep |
| Armor steel | ~2 GPa | ~13 cm deep |

## Special behaviors

- **Overpenetration:** damage equals energy the target resists along the path. Through 30 cm of flesh that is always ~**60 J**, at any launch speed, regardless of leftover mass or leftover KE. Extra mass does not add damage if the ball exits.
- **Momentum push:** only when the ball stops inside. Same ×1,000 ball (**4.1 kg**) at 100 m/s → 410 kg·m/s → ~5 m/s shove on an 80 kg person if all momentum transfers.
- **Back-face break (spall):** concrete, stone, glass blow a wider exit cone. Thin plates lose a punched plug.
- **Intact projectile:** below ~1.5 km/s the ball stays whole and drills. Above that, metal targets flow and the ball erodes.
- **Punch vs shove:** small dense ball punches; large heavy object at the same energy shoves and collapses structures.

## Magical densifying bullet (invent)

On contact with the target, the bullet becomes 2, 5 or 10 times denser for half a second. Kinetic energy stays the same. A heavier bullet carrying the same energy moves slower, so impact velocity drops and momentum rises. Penetration finishes within a few thousandths of a second, so half a second covers any impact many times over.

| Density | Impact Speed | Momentum | Kinetic Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1x | 100% | 1x | Same |
| 2x | 71% | 1.4x | Same |
| 5x | 45% | 2.2x | Same |
| 10x | 32% | 3.2x | Same |

- **Less splash and shatter:** Copper deforms less and steel stops shattering at the lower impact speed, so more of the energy goes into punching a hole instead of flattening the bullet.
- **Harder shove:** Momentum is what knocks a target back. A normal .50 hit pushes an 80 kg man at about 0.5 m/s if fully stopped. At 10x density it pushes him at about 1.5 m/s.
- **Narrower wound, deeper track:** Lower speed shrinks the temporary stretch cavity in flesh. The bullet fragments less, drives straighter and overpenetrates more often.
- **Less ricochet:** A heavier, slower bullet deflects less off angled plate.
- **Hard ceiling:** The spell adds no energy. No bullet can punch deeper than its energy pays for, so the spell helps most where a bullet was wasting energy by splashing or shattering.

### .50 rifle with normal powder (12,600 ft-lbs)

| Density | Impact Speed | Copper | Hardened Steel Core | Adamantium Needle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1x | 2,950 fps | ~12 to 15 mm | ~22 to 25 mm | ~65 mm |
| 2x | 2,090 fps | ~16 to 19 mm | ~23 to 26 mm | ~68 mm |
| 5x | 1,320 fps | ~20 to 24 mm | ~24 to 27 mm | ~72 mm |
| 10x | 930 fps | ~22 to 26 mm | ~25 to 27 mm | ~75 mm |

At normal speed a hardened steel core already converts its energy efficiently and the needle never deforms, so both gain little. Copper gains the most and approaches steel core performance at 10x density.

### Combined with super-energy powder (10x density)

| Powder | Impact Speed | Copper | Hardened Steel Core | Adamantium Needle | Shove on 80 kg Man |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3x (29,000 ft-lbs) | ~1,420 fps | ~40 to 50 mm | ~50 to 58 mm | ~190 mm | ~2.3 m/s |
| Mixed (52,000 ft-lbs) | ~1,900 fps | ~70 to 85 mm | ~85 to 100 mm | ~330 mm | ~3.1 m/s |
| 10x (70,000 ft-lbs) | ~2,210 fps | ~90 to 110 mm | ~110 to 130 mm | ~420 mm | ~3.6 m/s |

Super-energy bullets travel so fast that copper and steel splash against armor like liquid. Densifying on contact drops them back to normal rifle speeds while keeping all the energy, so copper and steel roughly triple their armor penetration. A 10x powder .50 copper round with the spell defeats the 100 mm front of a Tiger I. The shove at 10x powder throws a man off his feet at about 8 mph. The needle gains only a little because it never deformed in the first place.
