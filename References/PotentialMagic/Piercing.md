# Piercing

Hub: `PotentialMagic.md`. Related: `MassBoost.md`, `../Science/ManaCast.md` (Arrow punch / hardness).

Mostly piercing. A slow dense ball drills a clean channel about its own width. No blast or fragmentation.

Depth ≈ kinetic energy ÷ (resisting stress × frontal area). Higher density shrinks area per kilogram so depth grows.

Example: 1 cm ball, 4.1 kg, 100 m/s → KE = **20.5 kJ** → **2,050 mana** at ημ 1 (or less at high ημ).

| Target | Resisting stress | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Flesh | ~2.5 MPa | Passes through. Deposits ~60 J per 30 cm |
| Wood | ~30 MPa | ~9 m deep |
| Concrete | ~150 MPa | ~1.7 m deep |
| Armor steel | ~2 GPa | ~13 cm deep |

## Special behaviors

- **Overpenetration:** damage equals energy the target resists along the path. Through 30 cm of body ~60 J at 100 m/s, ~0.7 kJ at Mach 0.98, ~6 kJ at Mach 3, regardless of leftover mass. Extra mass does not add damage if the ball exits.
- **Momentum push:** only when the ball stops inside. 4.1 kg at 100 m/s → 410 kg·m/s → ~5 m/s shove on an 80 kg person if all momentum transfers.
- **Back-face break (spall):** concrete, stone, glass blow a wider exit cone. Thin plates lose a punched plug.
- **Intact projectile:** below ~1.5 km/s the ball stays whole and drills. Above that, metal targets flow and the ball erodes.
- **Punch vs shove:** small dense ball punches; large heavy object at the same energy shoves and collapses structures.
