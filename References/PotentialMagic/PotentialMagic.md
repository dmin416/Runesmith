# Potential Magic

Idea space for future spells and applications. **Must follow locked Science.**

```
Useful (J) = mana × 10 × η(L) × μ(INT)
Mana = J / (10 × η(L) × μ(INT))
```

Hub: `../Science/Science.md`. Cast tables: `../Science/ManaCast.md`. Path wear: `../Energy.md`. Apps: Kinetic, Compression, Vacuum, Sound, Flight, CleverApps under `Science/`.

Tables in this folder quote **effect joules** and **mana at ημ = 1** (L2, INT 15). Real cast mana = J / (10 × η × μ). Do not use the old 10%-efficiency (cost = 10× effect) model.
