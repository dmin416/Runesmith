# Pool And Storage

Draft. Piercing still wins on energy. Stored gas pays off for area blasts and defense, not as the default kill method.

Fight capacity = pool + (regen × time) + stored energy. Sustained kill rate = regen ÷ energy per kill.

## Energy per kill (drawn from the pool at 100% coupling)

| Method | Goblin | Dragon |
|---|---|---|
| Boosted needle (0.3 cm) | ~10 J | 0.5 to 2.3 kJ |
| Boosted ball line shot | ~80 J each in line | ~5 kJ |
| Elemental Ball (10 MJ cast) | ~250 kJ each (40 packed) | Not viable |

A 1,000 goblin wave costs 10 to 80 kJ. A 100 kJ pool covers 10,000 needle kills. Aiming and launch rate become the limit before energy does.

## What stored hydrogen adds

- 1 kg holds 120 MJ. A 1 L bottle at 700 bar holds 4.8 MJ. Compressed air at 200 bar holds 0.1 MJ per liter (48× less).
- Filling costs ~150 MJ per kg (142 MJ to split water plus ~8 MJ to compress). That is 6 MJ per 1 L bottle.
- Release costs ~10 J with Ember.
- A 1 L bottle blasts with up to 1.15 kg TNT equivalent for 6 MJ of pool energy. An Elemental Ball gives 0.23 kg for 10 MJ. Storage is ~8× better per pool joule. At 10% synthesis efficiency the edge disappears.
- Round-trip losses make storage a place for overflow regen, not the primary kill budget.

## Allocation

- **Pool:** needles and line shots for hordes and dragons.
- **Regen overflow:** fill bottles between fights.
- **Bottles:** blasts on shieldwalls, packed mobs behind cover and walls.
- **Defense:** a Ward costs the energy of the attack it stops, so the cheapest defense is killing the target first.
