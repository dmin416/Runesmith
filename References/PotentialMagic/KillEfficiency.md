# Kill Efficiency

Draft. Piercing shots win by roughly 300× to 1,000× on energy. Kill energy depends on the depth to the vital organ and the armor on the way. Body mass barely matters.

## Goblins (~80 J to reach the brain)

| Method | Energy per kill | Best use |
|---|---|---|
| Boosted ball or needle (x10 ball at 100 m/s) | ~185 J (1.9 kJ cast) | Single targets |
| Line shot through several | ~80 J per goblin hit | Ranks and corridors (one cast) |
| Shard Spray cone | a few hundred J per kill | Spread groups |
| Frag grenade (Elemental Ball) | 7 to 100 kJ per kill | Packed mobs when casting time matters |

The grenade never wins on energy. Even 150 goblins packed inside 5 m cost ~7 kJ each.

Pressure, compressed air and hydrogen all lose as pure magic-made fuel. Splitting water takes ~142 MJ per kg of hydrogen and burning returns ~120 MJ. Filtering it from air is worse. Compressed air stores only ~0.1 MJ per liter at 200 bar.

Best use of chemistry: magic as the trigger. Carry powder or fuel and spend ~10 J on Ember to ignite MJ of stored energy. That suits walls and huge packed crowds.

## Dragons (~500 J to reach the brain)

A 0.3 cm needle costs ~350 J for 5 cm of 1 GPa scale plus ~140 J for 10 cm of skull plus ~35 J for 1 m of flesh.

- Aim through the eye or mouth to skip most of the scale and bone.
- Budget 2 to 8 kJ for margin. That is a 41 g needle at Mach 0.98 (2.3 kJ, 21 kJ cast) up to 143.5 g (8.1 kJ, 79 kJ cast).
- An Elemental Ball (10 MJ cast) spends most of its energy on air and scale surface.
- Energy beyond the required depth exits the far side. Size the shot to the depth.

A dragon costs about 10× a goblin, not 1,000×, as long as the shot reaches a vital organ.
