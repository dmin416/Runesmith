# Kill Efficiency

Hub: `PotentialMagic.md`. Mass boost: `MassBoost.md`. Ember: `../Science/ManaCast.md`.

Piercing wins by roughly **300× to 1,000×** on energy versus blast for single kills. Kill energy tracks depth to a vital and armor on the way. Body mass barely matters.

Mana at ημ 1 = Effect J / 10. Scale with η(L) and μ(INT).

## Goblins (~80 J to reach the brain → ~8 mana at ημ 1)

| Method | Effect per kill | Mana ημ 1 | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boosted ball or needle (×10 ball at 100 m/s) | ~185 J | ~18.5 | Single targets |
| Line shot through several | ~80 J per goblin | ~8 each | Ranks and corridors (one cast) |
| Shard Spray cone | a few hundred J per kill | tens each | Spread groups |
| Frag / Elemental Ball | 7 to 100 kJ per kill | 700 to 10,000 | Packed mobs when cast time matters |

The grenade never wins on energy. Even 150 goblins packed inside 5 m still cost ~7 kJ each (~700 mana at ημ 1) if that is the share per body.

Pressure, compressed air and hydrogen all lose as **pure magic-made fuel**. Splitting water takes ~142 MJ/kg H₂; burning returns ~120 MJ. Filtering from air is worse. Compressed air stores only ~0.1 MJ/L at 200 bar (`PoolAndStorage.md`, `../Science/Compression.md` air cartridge).

Best chemistry use: magic as the **trigger**. Carry powder or fuel and spend ~**1 mana** at ημ 1 (~10 J) on Ember to ignite MJ of stored energy. Walls and huge packed crowds.

## Dragons (~500 J to brain path → ~50 mana at ημ 1 before margin)

A 0.3 cm needle: ~350 J for 5 cm of 1 GPa scale + ~140 J for 10 cm skull + ~35 J for 1 m flesh ≈ **525 J** (~53 mana at ημ 1).

- Aim through eye or mouth to skip most scale and bone.
- Budget **2 to 8 kJ** margin (~200 to 800 mana at ημ 1): e.g. 41 g needle at Mach 0.98 (~2.3 kJ) up to 143.5 g (~8.1 kJ).
- An Elemental Ball-scale blast spends most energy on air and scale surface.
- Energy past required depth exits the far side. Size the shot to depth.

A dragon costs about **10×** a goblin on a pierce path, not 1,000×, if the shot reaches a vital.
