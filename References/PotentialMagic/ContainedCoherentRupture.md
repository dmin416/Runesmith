# Contained Coherent Rupture (Metal)

> **Design loot.** Hub: `PotentialMagic.md`. Metals: `../World/Materials/Metals.md`. Not locked kit.

Inside a contained field, one wave: every metallic bond in the tuned object releases at once. Form collapses into a still cloud of atoms that settles as loose powder. No light, no sound, no motion beyond the slow fall of dust. Fluffy settle: **~1 L solid → ~10–20 L powder** at the field floor.

Tuned to metal only: non-metallic surface layers survive. Rust, verdigris, tarnish, patina remain as hollow crusts in the original outline until they crumble.

## At a glance

| Metal | Lattice harmonic | Atoms per kg | Visible tell | Powder | Left behind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iron | 9.8 THz | 1.08 × 10²⁵ | Magnetism dies first | Black (fibrous chains) | Rust shell if corroded |
| Steel | 9.8 THz + C overtone | ~1.08 × 10²⁵ | Magnetism dies first | Black; grey glitter if Fe-only | Carbide grit, rust shell |
| Copper | 7.2 THz | 9.48 × 10²⁴ | Red → flat grey | Dark red-brown | Verdigris shell |
| Silver | 4.7 THz | 5.58 × 10²⁴ | Mirror → dull; reflection dies first | Grey-black | Tarnish skin |
| Gold | 3.4 THz | 3.06 × 10²⁴ | Yellow → pale silver | Deep purple-brown | Nothing |
| Bronze | 7.2 + 4.2 THz chord | 8.95 × 10²⁴ | Sheen → grey | Dark brown, faint gold cast | Green/blue patina shell |

## Partial chords

- **Steel, Fe tone only:** iron atomizes; carbide flakes sparkle grey as they fall.
- **Stainless:** Cr/Ni need own tones. Partial chord → iron powder + chromium-rich lace on old grain boundaries.
- **Bronze, Cu tone only:** tin-rich silvery dust in the brown; ancient lead/arsenic may drop as dull beads.
- **Ancient bronze:** mostly patina → full-size hollow mineral shell; metal powder at its feet.
- **Cross-tune:** silver wave passes through gold with no effect (gold needs its own electronic band).

## Converted metals (open)

Live conversion bands (`Metals.md`) may need shifted harmonics or refuse the tune:

- **Mythril / aurium / dark-star Fe-steel:** likely retuned parents, not free passes.
- **Orihalcum:** antimagic; may block or starve the wave inside its volume.
- **Adamantium:** cast-final; rupture vs resonance destroy is a separate beat question.

Mana / ημ cost and field geometry stay unset until a beat needs them.
