# Detonation Tip vs Blast (Heavy Rapier)

> Author note. Watcher's heavy rapier / Lesser Detonation tip: `../Runes/Runes.md`. Kit parts: `../Items/ScrollRuneAbilities.md`. Wave energy law: `../World/Science/Energy/SevenFirecrackers.md`, `KineticImpartation.md`. Impact cousin: `ImpactRune.md`.

Depends almost entirely on **distance** and whether **shrapnel** is involved. Air blast alone is a weak threat to the point.

## Water vs steel

Water has no shear strength, so any pressure difference throws, cavitates and sprays it. Steel holds until stress passes yield (~250 MPa mild, ~1 GPa+ good blade/needle). Below that it flexes and springs back.

## Why a sharp tip shrugs off air blast

A needle or sword point is tiny next to the wave. The shock wraps around it and squeezes nearly equally from all sides. Even pressure does little to steel. Damage wants **lopsided** load. A thin point gives the blast almost nothing to push against.

## Rough pressures

| Source | Distance | Peak overpressure | Effect on the point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large firecracker (M-80 class) | 10 cm | ~1–4 MPa | None. Maybe soot |
| Large firecracker | Touching | Tens of MPa at most | Soot / scorch. Tip intact |
| Hand grenade (~180 g Comp B) | 1 m | ~0.6–0.8 MPa | None from blast |
| Hand grenade | 30 cm | ~7–10 MPa | None from blast |
| Hand grenade | Touching | ~20 GPa (detonation pressure) | Deformed, blunted or snapped |

Flash powder burns fast but does not truly detonate, so contact stays far under steel strength. High explosive at contact is ~20–80× hardened-steel yield. A stress wave into the metal concentrates as the section narrows toward the tip (crack / spall). Same idea as explosive forming and shaped charges.

## What actually hurts the point (grenade-scale)

1. **Fragments** (~1,000–1,500 m/s). Main danger inside the frag radius. Needle bends/breaks; sword tip nicks, chips or bends.
2. **Being thrown.** Loose sword hits ground/wall tip-first. Often worse than the blast.
3. **Heat.** Fireball is milliseconds. Soot or slight discoloration up close. Temper loss needs far longer exposure.

## Bottom line

Big firecracker: soot only. Grenade air blast: tip intact unless the explosive is basically touching it. Real damage is fragments and the weapon getting tossed.

Rapier tip-blast in flesh/wood still wrecks the **target** (soft, no shear). The blade tip survives ordinary tip dumps; contact HE-grade or frag is the worry.
