## Narrative

Strength is personal. Magic, levels and gear let one person outpace many. That is why many kingdoms exist. Close-combat specialists can outpace a crafter in pure melee. D fights how he likes early, then leans into ranged kit and fundamental magic control when bodies outrun him. Golems stay side projects. See `../People/Roland/Kit.md`.

## Detail

### What wins fights

- Attributes (STR / AGI tip speed and power; INT for spell Useful joules)
- Skills and spell levels (η)
- Gear material and runes / enchantments
- Positioning, party roles and prep
- Mana left in the pool

### Mage defense

Mana shields and barriers absorb in joules. Shape matters: a focused disk holds far more threat per mana than a large bubble. Overcharge helps; shape helps more. Insulation and barrier types follow Energy law.

**Mana Hands:** Mind-speed telekinesis. No verbal cast time. Actuation is not limited by body speed. Limits are mental focus, willpower, and mana. Speed is paid as kinetic energy and gets expensive when he actuates faster (cost scales hard with quicker nudges).

**Redirect over block:** Sideways deflection of a thrust needs only a few joules at the weak outer part of the blade. A mage can beat a stronger physicalist by shifting the point rather than meeting force head-on. Full timing and energy tables: `MageDefense.md`.

### Weapons

Mundane iron/steel arms are common. Magical and runic stock sell as separate grades. Plate for knights. Mail and gambeson otherwise. Personal firearms not widespread (high-class archer ≈ cannon); ship magic cannons exist. When plate no longer stops the main foe: `LightArmorLoadout.md`. Rune shield deflection: `ShieldRuneDefense.md`.

### Party roles

Frontline tanks and strikers. Backline archers and mages. Temporary job parties are normal. Same-tier preferred.

### People vs monsters

People-kill XP exists and is high-stakes. Self-defense and rank still make legal aftermath messy. See `../World/World.md` law section.
