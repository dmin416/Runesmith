# Basic Spell Shapes

> **Ideas / feel note.** Named catalog: `Spells.md`. Cast physics: `../World/Science/Energy/ManaCast.md`. Magic families: `../Runes/Magic.md`. Fire Orb stages: `../Runes/RuneCauseEffect.md`. Do not override locked Bolt egg size or Arrow kinetic tables.

## Shape family (early mage kit)

The basic class spells already teach three form words. Ball spells sit on the same ladder.

| Spell | Shape word | Standard form (feel) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mana Bolt** | bolt / bean | **Chicken-egg** size | Locked in prose / `Spells.md` / ManaCast (“blunt bean/egg”) |
| **Mana Arrow** | arrow | Shaft + tip (arrow) | Pure kinetic; size/mass in ManaCast |
| **Spear** family | spear | Long shaft + broad point (spear) | Longer / heavier pierce than Arrow; Ice Spear and kin |
| **Mana Shield** | shield | Bubble → **hard disk** / plate | Coverage, not a projectile |
| **Ball** family | ball / orb | **Softball** diameter (hypothesis) | Fireball, Fire Orb, Aqua Ball, light orbs |

Naming: Bolt / Arrow / Spear / Shield / Ball are the form words. **Fireball** = Ball + Fire. Elemental **Ice Spear** = Spear + Ice. Runic **Fire Orb** / **Aqua Ball** / **Orb of Light** sit on Ball.

## Size ladder (visual)

| Form | Approx. size | Earth anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Bolt (standard) | ~5–6 cm long | Chicken egg |
| Arrow (standard) | Short shaft (~30–40 cm feel) | Light arrow |
| Spear (standard hypothesis) | **~1.2–1.8 m** shaft feel | One-hand / short spear length |
| Ball (standard hypothesis) | **~9.5–10 cm** diameter | **Softball** (~12" circumference) |
| Ball (fat / overcharge idea) | ~21 cm diameter | Volleyball |
| Shield disk | Hand-span to torso plate | Scales with mana poured / skill |

### Why softball, not volleyball, as the default

- Bolt is already egg-scale. A **softball** is about **2×** that linear size: readable upgrade, still a handheld projectile.
- A **volleyball** (~4× egg length) reads as a **head-sized** blob. Fine for a pumped Fireball or a big utility orb, heavy for every “ball” cast.
- Softballs still look like a **thrown object** you can lob; volleyballs start looking like a short-range AOE seed before they even burst.

**Hypothesis lock for notes:** standard Ball / Orb / Fireball **formed size ≈ softball**. Overcharge, high skill, or shop “Fireball” AOE can inflate toward **volleyball** before the boom. Do not invent joule tables here; ManaCast / Energy win for damage.

## How the shapes behave (feel only)

| Shape | Job |
|---|---|
| **Bolt** | Short, thick, blunt pop. Eye / weak-spot finisher. Not a drill. |
| **Arrow** | Slim, fast, pierce. Range opener. |
| **Spear** | Longer / heavier pierce than Arrow. More thrust mass, less “flat” flight. Harder to spam; hits like a thrown spear. |
| **Shield** | Barrier. Starts as bubble, settles to a hard face. |
| **Ball** | Round packet. Holds fill (fire, water, light, pure mana) then flies or sits. Fireball = Ball + Fire + release/burst. Fire Orb rune = Ball chain on a scroll. |

**Arrow vs Spear:** both pierce. Arrow = thin high-speed packet. Spear = longer shaft, broader tip, more stop and shove. Same element can ride either form (Fire Arrow vs a later Fire Spear; Ice Spear is the early named spear in the catalog).

### Elementalization

Converter / phase-change law (`ManaMaterials.md`): pure mana → Fire (or other element) is a **phase change**. **Mana in = mana out** on the conversion step. No separate element tax stacked on top of the cast.

Affinity gates who can cast or scribe the elemental form. Roland at **0%** fire: no body Fireball; word scroll or runic Fire Orb instead.

Scroll quality ≠ rune quality; craft pour = spell ÷ η_craft: `../Runes/ScrollAspects.md`. Shop Intermediate Fireball **≈1 LS**. Body Fire Arrow / Fireball voice still open in `ManaCast.md` (craft Fire Arrow uses Arrow **50**; craft Fireball **100**).
