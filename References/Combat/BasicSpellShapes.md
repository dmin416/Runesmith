## Shape family (early mage kit)

The basic class spells already teach three form words. Ball spells sit on the same ladder.

| Spell | Shape word | Standard form (feel) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mana Bolt** | bolt / bean | **Chicken-egg** size | Locked in prose / `Spells.md` / ManaCast (“blunt bean/egg”) |
| **Mana Arrow** | arrow | Shaft + tip (arrow) | Pure kinetic; size/mass in ManaCast |
| **Spear** family | spear | Long shaft + broad point (spear) | Longer / heavier pierce than Arrow; Ice Spear and kin |
| **Mana Shield** | shield | Bubble → **hard disk** / plate | Coverage, not a projectile |
| **Orb** family | orb | **Bigger than Bolt**, soft sphere | Summons a small, practically useless sphere of mana or material. No kinetic pop, no compression. Fire Orb, Orb of Light |
| **Ball** family | ball | **Softball** diameter (hypothesis) | Damaging / packed round: Fireball, Aqua Ball |

Naming: Bolt / Arrow / Spear / Shield / **Orb** / Ball are the form words. **Fireball** = Ball + Fire (burst packet). **Fire Orb** / **Orb of Light** = Orb + converter (weak sphere; not the Ball rung). Elemental **Ice Spear** = Spear + Ice.

### Orb vs Ball vs Bolt

- **Orb / Bolt shaping:** same initial shape. Orb uses soft restraint (no explode). Bolt adds **small-mana explosive compression** on that shape.
- **Ball:** Orb skeleton + a couple extra lines; bigger packed round. Uses **moderate-mana compression** (more load than Bolt). Fireball / Aqua Ball rung. Softball default; volleyball only when pumped.
- **Bolt pop (no contact rune):** even system-identified Mana Bolt has **no detect-contact** segment. The kinetic pop is the compression constraint coming undone when the packet hits anything denser than air (straw ball undone by a hard hit). Contact triggers are Detonation-family, not Bolt.
- **Runic geometry (Ch 21.5 Notes):** Kit = shared **Orb/Bolt shape**, **Ball**, **Arrow**, **small-mana compression** (Bolt), **moderate-mana compression** (Ball). Arrow keeps a similar shaping **outline** but different **inner** sub-symbols. Component **ratios** are critical; forced ratio tweaks barely change size/effect and waste; whole-diagram shrink lowers in/out together; upsizing compression alone tends to **implode** Bolt rather than a fiercer pop. **Swaps** (converter/fill onto known shape) succeed far better: Aqua Ball → Fireball almost easy; same for water arrow and light ball. Lazy unmodified-ratio grafts still Diagnosis-readable unless the paper burns (occasional). See `../Runes/RuneCauseEffect.md`.

**Hypothesis lock for notes:** Orb size ≈ soft sphere a step up from egg Bolt. Ball / Fireball **formed size ≈ softball**. Overcharge Ball can inflate toward **volleyball**. Do not invent joule tables here; ManaCast / Energy win for damage.

## How the shapes behave (feel only)

| Shape | Job |
|---|---|
| **Bolt** | Short, thick, blunt pop from compression undoing on denser-than-air impact (no contact-detect rune). Eye / weak-spot finisher. Not a drill. |
| **Arrow** | Slim, fast, pierce. Range opener. |
| **Spear** | Longer / heavier pierce than Arrow. More thrust mass, less “flat” flight. Harder to spam; hits like a thrown spear. |
| **Shield** | Barrier. Starts as bubble, settles to a hard face. |
| **Orb** | Soft sphere. Bigger Bolt, no KE / compression. Mana or material fill that mostly just sits or drifts. Fire Orb / Orb of Light = Orb + converter. |
| **Ball** | Packed round packet meant to hit or burst. Fireball = Ball + Fire + release. Aqua Ball stays Ball. |

**Arrow vs Spear:** both pierce. Arrow = thin high-speed packet. Spear = longer shaft, broader tip, more stop and shove. Same element can ride either form (Fire Arrow vs a later Fire Spear; Ice Spear is the early named spear in the catalog).

### Elementalization

Converter / phase-change law (`ManaMaterials.md`): pure mana → Fire (or other element) is a **phase change**. **Mana in = mana out** on the conversion step. No separate element tax stacked on top of the cast.

Affinity gates who can cast or scribe the elemental form. Roland at **0%** fire: no body Fireball; word scroll or runic **Fire Orb** (Orb family, weak fire sphere) instead of a real Ball Fireball.
