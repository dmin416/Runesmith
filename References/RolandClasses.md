## Runic Battlemaster Overlord

Rewrite peak class. Same prestige band as Runesmith Overlord (tree position above Master Runesmith High-Lord; Tier 3 Overlord **×4.5 growth rate** on new basic-stat gains except luck and charisma). Forward-only; see `Levels.md`.

**Keeps from normal Overlord**
- Overlord body traits (organs, muscles, skeleton, tendons, nervous system, skin, circulatory)
- Overlord combat passives and actives already in Source (Might, Indomitable Will, Aura, Intimidation and related)
- Vassal / lend-power tools when useful (e.g. Overlord’s Rune Vassal), without making figurehead rule the fantasy
- Lordly battlefield authority flavor when it serves a fight

**Adds / leans into combat**
- Personal combat power first, especially ranged runic attacks
- Battlemaster skills that reward him shooting, marking and suppressing on the field himself
- Craft and engineer tools support his weapons, ammo, armor and drones as force he still fights beside
- Soft-pedal pure civil lord skills (speeches, Charisma-as-main-reward, throne management) unless a scene needs them

**Trial intent (rewrite)**
- Prove he can win as a runic battlemaster under Overlord pressure, not only as a general raising settlements
- Conquest and vassals can appear as tools. The pass condition centers on his own combat and runic firepower

Offered beside it at T3: Advanced Runesmith, Master Runesmith, Master Runesmith High-Lord and engineer upgrades. He picks Runic Battlemaster Overlord over High-Lord.
