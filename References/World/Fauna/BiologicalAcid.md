# Biological Acid

Earth research: can living things make and spit acid, how strong, and what a **10×** physical monster changes. Story seeds for acid-spitting foes (Myrmekes, etc.) and Ned's gland chemistry. Ned spike→acid craft: `Species/NeedleWorm.md`. Diet toxins (not acid synthesis): `ToxinSequestration.md`. Threat dial: `MonsterThreat.md`. Nav: `Index.md`.

## Caterpillars already spray acid

Some do. Caterpillars in the prominent moth family (Notodontidae), including the puss moth caterpillar and several *Schizura* species, have a gland on the underside of the thorax that sprays **formic acid** at attackers. The caterpillar pressurizes the gland with body muscles and can aim the spray a few centimeters up to roughly a few tens of centimeters.

## Controlled spit (no new exotic anatomy)

Aiming needs fine muscular control, not new organs. Caterpillars have no lungs to blow with. Workable designs:

- **Hydraulic squeeze:** Muscles compress a reservoir and force acid through a narrow duct or nozzle. Puss moth caterpillar and spitting cobras. Narrower nozzle + faster contraction → tighter, longer stream.
- **Mouth delivery:** Reservoir opening into the mouth. Spit from the head instead of the thorax. Real caterpillars already regurgitate gut fluid defensively: a short evolutionary step.
- **Binary mixing:** Two chemicals stored separately, mixed in a reaction chamber at firing, like the bombardier beetle. Produces heat and pressure on its own. Beetle spray reaches about **100°C**.

## Upper limit of acid strength in living things

Two separate limits.

### Weak organic acids (formic, acetic)

Made by synthesis and stored in a lined reservoir. Concentration depends mostly on how much water is removed.

| Creature | Acid | Concentration |
|---|---|---|
| Formicine ants | Formic | ~**60%** |
| Vinegaroons (whip scorpions) | Acetic | ~**84%** |

Weak acids burn, irritate and blind but do not dissolve metal or bone quickly even when concentrated.

### Strong mineral acids (hydrochloric, sulfuric)

Made with proton pumps that use energy to push H⁺ across a membrane.

| Creature | Acid | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Mammal stomach | HCl | ~pH **0.8–1** from secreting cells (~**0.5%** HCl) |
| Tun snails (*Dolium*) and some sea snails | H₂SO₄ in saliva | A few percent, ~pH **1** (dissolve shells / spines of prey) |

**Thermodynamic ceiling:** One ATP provides enough energy to push a proton across a gradient of about **9–10 pH units**. From cell interior near pH **7**, theoretical floor ≈ **pH −2 to −3** (several molar strong acid, roughly **10–25% HCl**). No pump beats that without more than one ATP per proton, which gets expensive. Real organisms stop well short of the limit (energy + tissue protection cost).

### Storage limit

Concentrated sulfuric acid chars protein, chitin and fat by dehydrating them. A creature can only store acid as strong as its reservoir lining survives. Renewed chitin, wax and mucus handle formic acid and dilute mineral acids. Near-concentrated sulfuric (**above ~70%**) destroys any biological lining: it **cannot** be stored. It can only be made on the spot (binary mixing) just before firing.

### Realistic ceilings for a living creature

| Acid type | Plausible maximum | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Formic or acetic | **60–85%** | Severe burns, eye destruction; corrodes some metals over time |
| Hydrochloric | **10–25%** (thermodynamic limit) | Dissolves limestone, bone, shell; etches iron and steel |
| Sulfuric | ~**20–30%** stored; higher only if mixed on firing | Same as HCl, plus charring at high concentration |

## Monster with 10× physical capability (delivery only)

Muscle force, power and metabolism up; **energy per proton-pump step unchanged**. Scales **delivery**, not chemistry. Stronger squeeze does not steepen the ATP gradient. Acid ceiling stays in the table above. What changes:

- **Range:** 10× squeezing pressure → ~**3.2×** exit velocity. Ballistic range scales with v² → ~**10×** range before air drag. Spitting cobra ~**2–3 m**. Cobra-sized monster at 10× power → ~**20–30 m**, with drag pulling a thin stream down more than a thick glob.
- **Volume and rate of fire:** 10× metabolism → 10× production and larger reservoir. A bucket of **20% HCl** does more harm than a teaspoon of **30%**.
- **Reaching the old ceiling:** Extra budget runs pumps near the thermodynamic limit instead of comfortable pH **1**. Mineral acid at the **10–25%** end, which no real animal does.
- **Binary weapon option:** Stored acid near that ceiling + mixing chamber for heat. Hot **25% HCl** works on armor, flesh and wood beyond concentration alone.

**Practical picture:** near-boiling **10–25%** strong acid at **20+ m**, liter-scale volumes, several shots. Steel still takes **minutes**. Flesh, leather, cloth and eyes fail in **seconds**.

## When the biology itself is ten times stronger

Every system scaled tenfold: muscle force, power, metabolism, **energy per biochemical reaction**, tissue toughness. Limits shift from biology to **chemistry**. The ceiling stops being "what a cell can survive" and becomes "what the acid itself can be."

### Acid strength

**Pumping limit:** A normal proton pump gets about **57 kJ** per ATP, enough for roughly **10 pH units**. Ten times that energy allows about **97 pH units**, far beyond anything chemistry permits. The pump stops being the bottleneck. The monster can drive acid to the maximum concentration the substance can physically reach.

| Acid | Maximum possible | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrochloric | About **38%** | HCl is a gas; water cannot hold more at normal T/P |
| Sulfuric | **98%** or higher | Can go past 100% as oleum (SO₃ in sulfuric acid) |
| Formic | Nearly **100%** | Pure formic is far stronger in effect than the ant version |
| Nitric | Up to about **98%** (fuming) | Needs nitrogen metabolism geared toward it; strong oxidizer that sets organics burning |

**Superacids** (fluoroantimonic, magic acid) stay out of reach. They need large fluorine and antimony, rare in any diet, and they attack almost all organics including the creature's lining. Concentrated sulfuric or fuming nitric is the practical top for a living thing even at 10× capability.

### Storage

Concentrated sulfuric strips water from tissue and chars it. Ten times tougher, faster-regrowing tissue does not make the monster immune. It turns damage into slow wear the body keeps ahead of. A thick chitin- and wax-based reservoir lining, shed and replaced continuously, could hold **98%** sulfuric acid. Fuming nitric is harder (oxidizes organics outright); it suits a **binary** design made only at the moment of firing.

### Delivery

- **Pressure and range:** 10× muscle force → 10× nozzle pressure → ~**3.2×** exit velocity → ~**10×** range before drag. Equal size: cobra **2–3 m** → **20–30 m**. Larger body + bigger nozzle: heavy stream drag barely affects over that distance.
- **Rate and volume:** 10× power and metabolism → 10× production and firing rate. Repeated large-volume sprays in one fight.
- **Heat:** Concentrated sulfuric releases enormous heat on contact with water. Spray with a water jet, or onto wet flesh, and it boils on impact. A binary chamber can also preheat the stream.

### Effects on targets

| Target | Effect |
|---|---|
| Flesh, wood, cloth, leather, rope | Concentrated sulfuric blackens and chars within **seconds**. Fuming nitric can ignite them. |
| Bone | Dissolves steadily under HCl; brittle and crumbles under hot sulfuric. |
| Steel | Cold concentrated sulfuric barely affects it (passive layer; industry stores it in steel tanks). Hot concentrated sulfuric and **38% HCl** both pit and thin plate within **minutes**. Anti-armor: hot **38% HCl** beats **98%** sulfuric. Anti-personnel: **98%** sulfuric or fuming nitric is far worse. |
| Stone / concrete | HCl fizzes through limestone, marble and concrete quickly. |
| Glass, gold, platinum, most plastics | Largely resist every acid on this list. |

**Literal 10×-biology monster:** near-pure, near-boiling acid at **20–30+ m**, liter-scale volumes, rapid follow-ups. Steel armor still takes **minutes** to fail, not seconds. Anything organic it hits is destroyed almost on contact.
