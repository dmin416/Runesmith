# Mounts

Riding, draft and companion beasts. Food herds: `../../Food/Food.md`. Ned: `../../People/Ned.md`. Agni: `../../People/Agni.md`. Fat notes: `MountsDesign.md`.

## Narrative

Horses stay common. Tamed monsters work farms, caravans and war. Companion bonds (Ned, Agni) are separate from rented stable mounts.

## Detail

### Trox

Large three-horned draft beast for plowing and farm work. Caravan bulls of the same line exist (three-tailed / three-horned variants). Steady dairy in farm areas from tamed monster herds includes this class of stock.

### Horse

Common riding mount for knights, nobles, escorts and travelers. Guild stables rent by rank. Premium breeds outrun ordinary stock.

### Oversized draft equine

Larger-than-normal equine-type monsters that pull caravan wagons.

### Yellow bird (ostrich-like)

Lanky saddle birds with sharp claws. Street and caravan mounts.

### Ned (Needle Worm line)

D's first tamed companion. Worn as a scarf early. Path steered away from wild Needle Moth toward worm / wyrm forms. Separate from Agni.

### Agni (mustelid line)

Later sacred companion. Starts as a weasel or similar small mustelid. Sacred-beast / church pressure. Not a wolf in the rewrite.

### Other (later)

Four-eyed deer, six-legged lizards, wyverns and exotic stable rentals exist in later arcs. Pull when a scene needs them.
