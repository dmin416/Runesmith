## Narrative

Terra is an Earth-like world with magic, monsters, and a status system everyone has. Power is easy enough to reach that many kingdoms exist. D wakes in Caldris, in the body named Roland Arden, a land with steam, rail, and airships, but no practical teleportation. Materials follow Earth stock plus mana-saturated and special grades. Conflict comes from politics, monsters, dungeons, and villains.

## Detail

### Cosmology and time

- **World:** Terra
- **Moons:** Two. Red and blue.
- **Calendar:** 13 months × 28 days. 364-day year. 24-hour day.
- **Climate:** Weather and seasons. Caldris temperate. Mountains colder.

### Geography and politics

- **Starting kingdom:** Caldris
- **Map shape:** Many kingdoms. Low barrier to high personal power fragments the map.
- **Neighbors (kept):** Alexandria (sun-elf holy kingdom). Bolia (moon-elf lands). Hatfordian Empire (border power).
- **Nobility:** Noble houses matter. House Arden is a Baron's house. See `../People/Family.md`.
- **Politics:** Succession and inheritance fights can drive events. Ceasefire borders can reopen into war. Borders and wars happen. Noble military postings are normal.
- **Early places:** Arden estate. Carwen. Edelgard. Albrook later (boom town). See `Geography/Places.md`.
- **Cover name:** Wayland is a **person** identity, not a town.
- **Lustile:** Academy city in Caldris (arts / smith / mage). Early option Wentworth refused.
- **Institute:** Exists later. Magic school / politics hook.

### Peoples

- Humans dominate Caldris. All types exist. No strong racial-prejudice default.
- See `Society/Races.md`.

### Faith

- **Main (Caldris):** Church of Solaria (sun faith)
- **Also kept:** Lunaria / moon faith. Abyss / cult threat.
- Churches run orphanages. Can sell class crystals and healing. Priests / clerics can remove curses for pay.
- **Solaria path talk:** Acolyte → Cleric → Paladin.
- **Orphanage rule:** Orphans choose church life or leave by about age 14. Class stones are not free to orphans.

### Status system

Everyone has one. Full law in `../Progression/Progression.md`.

Short keep:

- Screens private unless shared or strong peek
- Ascension / class crystals exist; churches involved
- Status-hide gear exists (armbands / items that mask or fake Identify reads)
- Identify, Analyze, Mana Sense exist
- Affinities matter for elemental mage paths
- Tier 1 class cap 25; class tiers exist
- Multiple classes over a lifetime
- Combat and production classes both valid
- Skills can raise attributes; titles exist
- Party XP shared; people-kill XP exists
- Taming exists
- Pre-class ambient mana absorb poisons or kills
- Enchanting ≠ runecrafting
- Diagnosis is D-only
- Death is death (only D transmigrates)

### Magic and materials

- Magic law: `../Runes/Magic.md`
- Mana / energy: `../Runes/Energy.md`. Mana is like air; density rises with altitude
- Metals / hosts: `Materials/Metals.md` (foundations locked; deep fill later)
- Monster cores / mana stones: `Materials/MonsterCores.md` (foundations locked; deep fill later)
- Magic stones already feed fuel, craft, and rail power alongside steam
- Civilian mana-stone lamps are not common; D can make and sell them
- Storage bags: space magic; no nesting; dead in, living usually out. See `../Runes/Magic.md`
- Trade grades: magic items Lowest→Highest; rune ranks Lesser→Common→Greater→Grand→Legendary

### Dungeons and threats

See `Geography/Dungeons.md`.

- Many monsters, villains, and dungeons
- Dungeon cores are real; damage / removal illegal; destruction catastrophic
- New dungeons can appear when mana conditions are right
- Wild monsters behave differently from dungeon-spawned ones
- Stair throats are monster-safe; tamed companions can pass
- Boss / high floors can need guild appointments
- Mana density rises nearer cores / deeper floors
- Magical materials can appear as dungeon / mine products
- Nest breaches and outbreaks can force emergency guild musters
- Monster goods (hides, tusks, stones) are a normal market class
- Monster meat tastes good
- **Never eaten:** goblins (and goblin-kin), ghouls, zombies, liches and other undead (parts / stones only)

### Travel and tech

Era lock: `Tech/Technology.md`. Mounts: `Fauna/Mounts.md`. Vehicle craft: `Science/Vehicle/`.

- Steam, magical rail, airships. No practical teleport. Guns not widespread; ship magic cannons exist.
- Outside settlements, caravans are normal. Crystal-mirror calls for rich / companies.

### Money and trade

See `Society/Economy.md`.

- Copper / silver / gold ladder + royal gold
- Countries tax land, trade, and sometimes dungeon access / income
- Guilds take cuts / fees on turn-ins and some sales
- Auction houses exist (Libra-style)
- Banks / coin storage exist in cities
- Patents and merchant-guild heat can follow inventions
- Inventions can draw patent / guild pressure

### Adventurer institutions

- Adventurer Guilds with rank boards: Bronze → Steel → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Mithril → Orichalcum → Adamantium (`../Progression/AdventurerRanks.md`)
- Ranks gate dungeon entry and job boards

- Cards can hide or gate full credentials; first-name registration possible
- Cover names / false identities usable when credentials allow
- Parties can change; temporary job parties are normal
- Escort / caravan guard work is a normal job type
- Wanted / job boards post by rank
- Thieves Guilds / underworld cells can require Thief, Bandit, or equivalent class to join

### Schools and craft

- Mage and knight schools / academies exist as noble paths
- Mage privileges can include land grants / taxes for nobles who qualify
- Forges, smithies, and workshops can be hired or rented in craft cities
- Armorers and weapon shops sell mundane and magical gear as separate stock
- Spell scrolls and runic goods are sold (Exeor-style shops)
- Contracts can bind with mana signatures / curses

### City life

- Gate entry fees; adventurer cards help
- Libraries and map sales
- Inns and taverns are normal social hubs
- Merchant councils can hold real city power
- Black markets and red-light districts exist
- Thieves’ guilds / underworld networks in some cities
- Public baths exist and are gross
- Gambling dens and casinos exist
- Theater / entertainment mostly for the better-off
- Education outside nobles / churches / guilds is spotty
- Alchemy goods are somewhat common; poison may be gated
- Potions sold graded Lowest→Highest; healing closes flesh for real; Lowest seals but leaves soreness. Prices in `Society/Economy.md`
- Paper, ink, and writing supplies sold
- Farmland and small holdings can be bought near dungeon towns
- City watches / guards exist; not modern police
- Paid Identify services exist; full-status peeks are expensive
- Food and drink by wealth: `../Food/Food.md`. No year-long inn-gruel default. Coffeehouses where coffee is not a far-ship luxury.

### Law and violence

- Slavery exists; slave markets / trade in some places
- Commoners who kill nobles hang even if the noble was guilty
- Self-defense kills still get messy when status and rank are involved
- Nobles can send watchers / agents after estranged kids
