# Radiation Environments

Part 4. Quick orbit table also on `../Radiation.md`. Shielding: `ShieldingAndLimits.md`.

## Earth

| Source | Annual dose |
|---|---|
| Radon and daughters | ~1.2–2 mSv |
| Terrestrial gamma | ~0.5 mSv |
| Internal (K-40, C-14) | ~0.3 mSv |
| Cosmic rays (sea level) | ~0.3–0.4 mSv |
| Total natural average | ~2.4–3 mSv |
| Ramsar, Iran (highest populated) | up to ~260 mSv |
| Commercial flight cruise | ~3–7 µSv/hour |
| One banana (K-40) | ~0.1 µSv |

Surface rate ≈ **0.008 mSv/day**.

## Space

| Environment | Composition | Dose rate |
|---|---|---|
| GCR (deep space) | 87% protons, 12% He, 1% heavy ions, ~1% electrons | ~1.8 mSv/day |
| ISS (LEO) | Trapped protons (SAA), GCR, secondaries (neutrons) | ~0.25–0.5 mSv/day |
| Inner Van Allen | Protons to hundreds of MeV | Very high; transit only |
| Outer Van Allen | Electrons 0.1–10 MeV | High; mostly blocked by hull |
| SPE | Protons, some He/heavier | Hundreds of mSv to several Sv over hours–days |
| Lunar surface | GCR + albedo neutrons | ~1.4 mSv/day |
| Mars surface | GCR + secondaries | ~0.65–0.7 mSv/day |

Apollo Van Allen transit totals ~**2–12 mSv** per mission (fast transit).

## Nuclear detonation energy partition (typical fission)

| Form | Share |
|---|---|
| Blast | ~50% |
| Thermal radiation | ~35% |
| Initial (prompt) ionizing | ~5% |
| Residual (fallout) | ~10% |

Above ~10–20 kt, thermal burn radius exceeds lethal prompt radiation radius. At 1 Mt, third-degree burns extend roughly 3–4× farther than a lethal prompt dose.

## Astrophysical extremes

- **GRB:** within a few thousand ly, strips ozone on an Earth-like world
- **Supernova:** lethal within ~25–50 ly via ozone loss + cosmic ray enhancement
- **Magnetar flare:** SGR 1806-20 (2004) disturbed Earth's ionosphere from ~30,000 ly
- **Accretion disks / quasars:** unsurvivable X/gamma near the source
