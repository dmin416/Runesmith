## Ultraviolet

| Band | Reaches | Atmosphere |
|---|---|---|
| UV-C | Outer stratum corneum; cornea | Fully blocked by O₃ / O₂ |
| UV-B | Epidermis | ~90–95% blocked by ozone |
| UV-A | Dermis | Mostly transmitted |

**Mechanisms:** UV-B/C absorbed by DNA (peak 260 nm) → CPDs (~75%) and 6-4 photoproducts (~25%). UV-A mostly indirect ROS → 8-oxoguanine, strand breaks, photoaging, melanoma contribution. Repair: NER. Xeroderma pigmentosum: ~1,000–10,000× skin cancer risk.

| Effect | Notes |
|---|---|
| Erythema | Inflammatory, not thermal. Fair skin MED ≈ 200–300 J/m² erythemally weighted |
| Photokeratitis | Corneal burn; threshold ~30 J/m² at 270 nm |
| Cataract / pterygium | Chronic UV-B |
| Skin cancers | BCC, SCC, melanoma |
| Immunosuppression | Langerhans cells |
| Vitamin D | Beneficial UV-B path |

**Space:** No ozone. Unfiltered UV-C/B sunburns exposed skin in seconds to under a minute; blinds unprotected eyes fast. Visors: gold + UV filters.

**Far-UVC (222 nm):** absorbed in dead skin / tear film; under study for occupied-room disinfection.

## Extreme / vacuum UV

10–200 nm. Absorbed by air in mm–cm. Solar EUV heats/ionizes upper atmosphere; drives Mars atmospheric loss. Absorbed in outermost nm–µm of skin.

## Visible light

Eye focuses ~100,000× onto retina for a point source.

| Mechanism | Wavelengths | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Photochemical (blue-light) | 400–500 nm, peak ~440 nm | Prolonged bright exposure |
| Thermal | Visible + IR-A | Lasers, eclipse viewing |
| Photomechanical | Any | Ultrashort laser pulses |

Skin: pigmentation; burns at very high irradiance (thermal).

## Infrared

| Band | Penetration | Hazard |
|---|---|---|
| IR-A | Several mm; reaches retina | Retinal burns; glassblower's cataract |
| IR-B | ~1 mm (water) | Corneal burns, skin heating |
| IR-C | Outer ~0.1 mm | Surface skin heating |

Entirely thermal. Moritz–Henriques:

| Skin temp | Time to burn |
|---|---|
| 44°C | ~6 hours |
| 51°C | ~4 minutes |
| 60°C | ~5 seconds |
| 70°C | ~1 second |

| Flux | Effect |
|---|---|
| ~1 kW/m² | Midday sun; indefinite |
| 4 kW/m² | Pain after ~20 s |
| 9.5 kW/m² | 2° burns after ~20 s |
| 12.5 kW/m² | Wood ignites with pilot |
| 37.5 kW/m² | Industrial equipment damage |

Nuclear thermal pulse burn fluences (yield-dependent): 1° ~2–3 cal/cm²; 2° ~4–6; 3° ~8–10. (1 cal/cm² = 41.84 kJ/m²)

Body radiation: Stefan–Boltzmann. Resting human ~800–900 W gross, ~100–150 W net loss to 20°C room.

**Space thermal:** Sunlit 1 AU ~+120°C; shade toward −150°C. ISS exterior roughly −157°C to +121°C.

## Microwaves (300 MHz–300 GHz)

Dielectric heating of water. Purely thermal at known exposures.

| Frequency | Penetration (muscle-type) |
|---|---|
| 915 MHz | ~3 cm |
| 2.45 GHz | ~1.7 cm |
| 10 GHz | ~3–4 mm |
| 95 GHz (Active Denial) | ~0.4 mm |

SAR = σ|E|² / ρ (W/kg). Public whole-body 0.08; occupational 0.4; ~4 W/kg ≈ 1°C core rise threshold.

Effects: deep burns sparing skin surface; cataracts; testicular damage; Frey effect (clicks); ADS ~50°C skin in seconds without designed injury.

## Radio frequency (<300 MHz)

Deep penetration. Whole-body resonance ~70–100 MHz ungrounded. Heating + induced/contact currents. Antenna RF burns. MRI patient SAR ~2–4 W/kg.

## ELF and static fields

ELF (50/60 Hz): no heating at realistic strengths; induced fields; magnetophosphenes (~10 mT at ~20 Hz). Static MRI 1.5–7 T: vertigo, metallic taste, projectile hazard. Magnetars 10⁹–10¹¹ T: lethal at ~1,000 km.
