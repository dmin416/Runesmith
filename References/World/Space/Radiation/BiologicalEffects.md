## Acute radiation syndrome (whole-body, acute)

| Dose (Gy) | Syndrome | Prodrome | Outcome without care |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1–0.25 | Subclinical | None | Chromosome aberrations in lymphocytes |
| 0.5–1 | Subclinical to mild | None to mild | Blood counts dip, recover |
| 1–2 | Mild hematopoietic | 2–6 h, nausea 10–50% | Survives |
| 2–4 | Moderate hematopoietic | 1–2 h | Some deaths weeks 3–8 (infection, bleeding) |
| 4–6 | Severe hematopoietic | <1 h | ~50% lethal (LD50/60 ≈ 4–4.5 Gy untreated) |
| 6–10 | Gastrointestinal | <30 min | Mostly fatal within 2 weeks even with care |
| 10–20 | GI + vascular | Minutes | Fatal in ~1–2 weeks |
| >20–50 | Neurovascular/CNS | Immediate | Death in hours to 2 days |

**Phases:** Prodromal → Latent ("walking ghost") → Manifest illness → Recovery or death.

**Marrow first:** neutrophils ~1 day, platelets ~7–10 days. Stem cell kill → infection/bleeding at nadir.

**Gut at higher dose:** crypt stem cells die; villi (3–5 day renew) slough → fluid loss, bacterial sepsis.

## Organ-specific thresholds (acute)

| Tissue | Effect | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Lens | Cataract | ~0.5 Gy |
| Testes | Temporary sterility | ~0.15 Gy |
| Testes | Permanent sterility | ~3.5–6 Gy |
| Ovaries | Permanent sterility | ~2.5–6 Gy (lower with age) |
| Skin | Erythema | ~2–3 Gy local |
| Skin | Temporary hair loss | ~3 Gy |
| Skin | Moist desquamation | ~15–20 Gy |
| Skin | Necrosis | >25 Gy |
| Heart/vessels | CVD (long-term) | ~0.5 Gy |
| Fetus | Malformation / ID | ~0.1–0.2 Gy at weeks 8–15 |

## Cutaneous radiation syndrome

Unfolds over weeks (unlike thermal burns). Early erythema (hours) → latent → main erythema (weeks 2–3) → desquamation → ulceration → possible necrosis months later. Vessel damage can worsen years.

## Late effects

Leukemia latency 2–10 yr (peak ~5–7). Solid cancers 10+ yr lifelong. Cataracts months–years. Fibrosis (lung, skin, heart). CVD decades. Hereditary effects in animals; not detected in A-bomb survivor children.

## Dose-rate effect

Same dose protracted = less damage (repair keeps pace). DDREF: ICRP 2; BEIR VII 1.5. Deterministic thresholds rise substantially with protraction.

## Radiosensitivity (Bergonié–Tribondeau)

Faster division + less specialization → more radiosensitive.

Lymphocytes > marrow stem > intestinal crypt > gonadal > skin basal > lens epithelium > liver > kidney > muscle > bone > nerve.

## Individual variation

ATM / Nijmegen / Fanconi / LIG4: severely radiosensitive (ATM homozygotes ~3×). Children and elderly more vulnerable. Women higher cancer risk per Sv. Anemia, infection, malnutrition, immunodeficiency lower survival.
