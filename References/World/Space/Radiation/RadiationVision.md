# Radiation Immunity and Vision

Damage immunity vs photoreception. Non-ionizing eye hazards: `NonIonizing.md`. Robust profiles: `RobustHeatHuman.md`. Body vision: `../../Science/Body/Vision360.md`. Hub: `../Radiation.md`.

## Light is radiation; sight needs it

Visible light is itself radiation. Sight works only because light changes the eye. A photon striking the retina flips a retinal molecule from its **11-cis** to its **all-trans** shape: a photochemical reaction caused by radiation.

Total immunity to radiation **effects** would mean no vision at all. A radiation-immune character who can see must be immune to radiation **damage** while still letting light act on the photoreceptors.

That split decides which kinds of blinding still work.

| Blinding type | Mechanism | Radiation-damage immunity protects? |
|---|---|---|
| Retinal burns (lasers, staring at the sun, nuclear flash at close range) | Thermal protein coagulation | Yes |
| Blue-light retinal injury | Photochemical oxidation | Yes |
| UV snow blindness (photokeratitis) | Corneal cell damage | Yes |
| Radiation cataract | Lens protein aggregation | Yes |
| Ionizing damage to the retina or optic nerve | DNA and cell death | Yes |
| Flash blindness / dazzle | Photopigment bleached faster than it regenerates | **No** |
| Glare and saturation | Signal overload; pupil can't close fast enough | **No** |
| Ion-induced light flashes (phosphenes) | Particles trigger photoreceptors directly | **No** (sees flashes without harm) |
| Chemical, physical, smoke, debris, darkness | Not radiation | **No** |

## UV snow blindness (photokeratitis)

**Mechanism:** UV-B and UV-C are absorbed by the cornea's outer layer of cells. The UV fuses DNA bases into dimers and generates reactive oxygen species. The damaged cells die and slough off, exposing the nerve endings beneath.

**Threshold:** ~**30 J/m²** at 270 nm. Snowfields reflect up to ~**80%** of UV, which doubles the dose. In space with no ozone, the threshold is reached within **seconds**.

**Symptoms:** Start **6–12 hours** after exposure: severe pain, gritty sand-in-the-eye feeling, tearing, involuntary eyelid clamping. Functional blindness lasts **24–72 hours** until corneal cells regenerate.

**Radiation-damage-immune person:** Fully protected. Corneal cells survive: no pain, no delay, no blindness. Normal vision is unaffected because the adult lens already filters out UV before it reaches the retina. People who have had their natural lens removed can perceive near-UV (~**300–400 nm**) as a violet-white glow. A character whose UV immunity extends to a UV-transparent lens could gain that perception.

## Blue-light photochemical retinal injury

**Mechanism:** Blue light at **400–500 nm** (most damaging near **440 nm**) excites light-sensitive compounds in the retinal pigment epithelium (RPE): lipofuscin, A2E, and all-trans retinal (spent visual pigment). Excited molecules generate ROS that kill RPE cells and photoreceptors.

**Key property:** Damage is cumulative. Total dose (brightness × time) determines injury. Dim long = bright short. Sources: staring at the sun, welding arcs, long viewing of intense blue LEDs or lasers.

**Radiation-damage-immune person:** Fully protected from **injury**, still **dazzled**.

Immunity must be selective. Vision photochemistry must still happen (11-cis → all-trans flip). Only harmful side reactions (oxygen radicals, oxidized lipids) are blocked. In the heat-resistant model this matches **T2**: RPE packed with antioxidants and oxidation-resistant proteins (`RobustHeatHuman.md`).

## Ionizing damage to retina / optic nerve

| Structure | Effect | Threshold (normal human) |
|---|---|---|
| Lens | Cataract | ~**0.5 Gy** (most sensitive part of the eye) |
| Retina | Radiation retinopathy (vessels, bleeding, swelling) | ~**35–50 Gy** over many treatments; appears 6 months to 3 years later |
| Optic nerve | Radiation optic neuropathy (sudden, usually permanent loss) | ~**50–60 Gy** fractionated; single-dose radiosurgery limit ~**8–10 Gy** |
| Whole eye | Neurovascular collapse | Above ~**50 Gy** acute; death comes before eye damage matters |

**Radiation-damage-immune person:**
- No cataracts, retinopathy or optic nerve damage
- Still sees phosphenes when ions or charged particles pass through the retina (Apollo / ISS). Harmless.
- In an intense field (criticality), the eye registers a flash: useful as a field signal
- Heavy-ion deep space: steady sprinkle of flashes, worst with eyes closed

## Smoke, chemicals, debris, darkness

None of these are radiation. Radiation-damage immunity gives **no** protection. Heat-resistant traits help in some cases.

### Smoke

- Irritant chemicals (acrolein, formaldehyde, HCl) trigger reflex tearing and eyelid clamping
- Particles block vision optically
- Heat from the fire is thermal radiation: **T1** macro heat resistance protects the heat component only
- **Result:** Still functionally blinded. Thermal burns to the cornea are prevented.

### Chemicals

| Agent | Mechanism | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Alkalis (lye, ammonia, lime, cement) | Dissolve tissue; penetrate deep | Worst; can blind permanently within seconds |
| Acids | Coagulate surface proteins; barrier limits penetration | Severe but usually shallower |
| Pepper spray (capsaicin) | Activates TRPV1 pain receptor; forces lids shut | Temporary; no tissue damage |
| Tear gas (CS, CN) | Activates TRPA1 pain receptor | Temporary |

- Radiation immunity: **no** protection
- **T2** heat resistance: partial resistance to acid/alkali burns (proteins that resist heat denaturation often resist chemical denaturation)
- Pepper spray / tear gas: work fully unless pain signaling is also altered

### Debris

Corneal scratches, embedded fragments, penetrating injuries, blunt trauma (orbital fractures, retinal detachment). Fully vulnerable. Radiation and heat resistance offer nothing against mechanical damage.

### Darkness

Without light, photoreceptors have nothing to detect. Radiation immunity adds no night vision. Blind as anyone else. Dark vision needs separate traits: more sensitive photoreceptors, tapetum lucidum, or infrared perception.

## Flash blindness is the main remaining radiation vulnerability

A bright flash bleaches the photopigment (rhodopsin, cone opsins). The eye stays blind until the pigment regenerates. Seconds to minutes for a camera flash; longer for intense sources. Full dark adaptation takes about **30 minutes**. No tissue is harmed, so damage immunity does not apply.

A nuclear detonation at night causes flash blindness far beyond the distance at which it burns the retina.

## What a radiation-damage-immune character experiences

| Source | Experience |
|---|---|
| Laser or the sun | Blinded temporarily (dazzle); recovers fully with no scarring |
| Flashbang or nuclear flash | Blinded temporarily, same as anyone else |
| Welding arcs or unfiltered space UV | Dazzled; no lasting injury (no photokeratitis) |
| Deep space or reactor rooms | Sees phosphenes; no harm |

## Separate traits that reduce dazzle

Not part of damage immunity. Stack if the character also has them:

- Faster photopigment regeneration (recovery in a fraction of a second instead of minutes)
- Wider dynamic range (HDR-like sensing)
- Reflexive protective layer (nictitating membrane, or a lens that darkens in bright light)
- Faster pupil reflex (human pupil takes roughly **0.2–0.5 s** to constrict)
