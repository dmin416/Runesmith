# Moons

Hub: `Space.md`. World: `../World.md`. Ambient soak: `../Science/Energy/ManaConcentration.md`. Metal conversion: `../Materials/Metals.md`. Stone path: `../Materials/MonsterCores.md`. Availability tags: `../Materials/Materials.md`.

## Narrative

Terra has **two moons**, red and blue, that hang near each other in the sky (first noted Chapter 4). Both sit near lunar distance in the open-air mana haze / solar-wind floor band. Mineral deposits on both have soaked for geological time in that field. Prestige ore when a beat can reach them. Not new Earth elements: same metals and stone matrices as Terra, already cooked by ambient mana.

## Detail

### Sky lock

| Moon | Look | Story role |
|---|---|---|
| Red moon | Rust / fire cast | Weapons and structure stock |
| Blue moon | Cold / pale / cyan cast | Rune economy and stone seeds |

Color is mostly surface rock and dust. Craft value is the ores and matrices underneath.

### Shared (both moons)

- Sit near `C_max` / `A_max` (`../Science/Energy/ManaConcentration.md`)
- Ages of soak finish permanent conversion where the parent metal allows
- Almost no street-mundane Ag / Au / Cu / Fe / Ti left near the surface
- Solar-wind implanted volatiles in dust (flavor, not a new element)
- Meteor-mixed **star iron** already cooked before impact
- Low-soak **silica / clay / carbon** matrices remain the best natural space-stone seeds

### Red moon deposits

Rust / fire look: iron-oxide dust and basalt country.

| Deposit | Why it fits | After ages of space soak |
|---|---|---|
| Iron oxides / hematite-rich basalt | Real red airless-body look | **Star iron** seams |
| Copper ores in red host rock | Cuprite / weathered Cu stains | High-grade **aurium** |
| Gold traces in iron country | Common geological pairing | Near-pure **orihalcum** pockets |
| Titanium in dark basalt (ilmenite) | Real lunar Ti carrier under red dust | **Adamantium** feedstock (cast-final) |
| Silica / clay / basaltic glass | Low-soak matrices | Natural space-stone seeds (arcanium → aetherium ladder) |

**One-line:** red = **star iron + ilmenite (adamantium path)**.

### Blue moon deposits

Cold / pale / cyan look: highlands and cool metal stains.

| Deposit | Why it fits | After ages of space soak |
|---|---|---|
| Anorthosite / pale feldspar highlands | Real pale lunar rock; blue-white at distance | Mostly low-soak stone matrix + trapped metals |
| Silver-rich veins | Cool metal; sky-blue mythril sheen | High / true **mythril** |
| Copper carbonate / azurite-class stains | Actual blue mineral color | **Aurium** in blue host rock |
| Nickel–iron + ice-shadow cold traps | Blue-grey metal + polar frost look | **Star iron**; cold-side specialty stock |
| Carbon / graphite lenses | Dark under pale crust; dull to mana | Stone-seed carriers, not magic metal |

**One-line:** blue = **mythril + silica stone seeds**.

### Craft feel

| Need | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Melee / structure metals | Red moon star iron; darksteel forged from it on Terra |
| Rune hosts | Blue moon mythril |
| Mana dampers | Aurium from either (red cuprite country or blue azurite stains) |
| Magic resist stock | Red moon orihalcum pockets |
| Cast-final indestructible | Red moon ilmenite → adamantium (molten cast only) |
| Artificial / refined stones | Pale or basaltic low-soak matrices from either moon |

Exact grade tables and which shaft yields what stay open until a beat needs them. Reaching the moons is the gate, not inventing a third metal law.
