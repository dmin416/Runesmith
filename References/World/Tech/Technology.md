# Technology

Era / tech baseline for Caldris. Fat era notes: `TechnologyDesign.md`. Hub: `../World.md`.

## Narrative

Social structure stays medieval (kingdoms, nobles, knights). Tech reaches toward early industrial magitech. Magic stones fill roles coal, oil and electricity fill on Earth. Result is fantasy steampunk / magitech, not pure High Middle Ages.

## Detail

### Present

- Steam, boilers, condensers
- Magical rail (gnomes invented trains). Passenger and freight. Multi-day. Noisy
- Airships (cost more than rail; prestige / royal band)
- Magic stones as fuel, craft power and rail power
- Iron / steel arms, plate, mail, gambeson, castles, mills, horse transport
- Ship **magic cannons** exist. Personal firearms not widespread: a high-class archer is basically a cannon, so gunpowder sidearms stay niche
- Crystal-mirror calls for rich / companies
- Mage-frozen cold and rail move out-of-season produce
- Mixed Victorian-ish street clothes + full plate; peasants also in simple clothes

### Gunpowder invent (not street baseline)

Caldris does not mass-issue personal firearms. If someone invents or imports the chemistry:

- **Medieval workshop band:** refined black powder can match 19th-century quality with full knowledge (smoke, fouling, iron corrosion stay). Sulfurless black powder is a weaker, less corrosive variant. Smokeless nitrocellulose is chemically possible from small alchemical acid batches but only as a tiny elite supply; stability and batch consistency are the walls.
- **Victorian / magitech industrial band:** single-base smokeless (nitrocellulose), double-base (adds nitroglycerin), and even triple-base (nitroguanidine) are in reach. Acid volume and purity decide scale, not secret knowledge. Victorian is industrial; medieval is workshop quantities.
- **Cleanest practical load:** stabilized single-base or triple-base with a lead-free non-corrosive primer and a jacketed or lead-free bullet. Lead from primers and bare lead bullets is the main health hazard, worse than the powder choice.
- **Indestructible barrels** (adamantium cast-final) remove hot-gas erosion, so double-base becomes the strongest practical propellant. Chamber pressure then hits recoil, the shooter's body and whether the bullet survives, not the barrel.
### Missing or rare (invent / prestige)

- Mass printing (not a thing; press / movable type = invent-only if D builds one)
- Common Terra pencils (D can invent)
- Mass newspapers
- Phones
- Practical teleportation
- Civilian mana-stone lamps as common household stock (he can sell into that gap). Earth lighting ladder (filament → discharge → LED): `../Science/Energy/Lighting.md`
- Matches, ball pens, toilet paper, bicycles, standardized soap, safety pins (Earth-civilian seeds; not baseline)
- Personal **laser** weapons (invent / elite shop path: ruby, copper vapor, CO2 mix). Physics and gases: `../Science/Energy/Optics.md`. Fight doctrine: `../../Combat/Lasers.md`

### Roads and travel

Outside settlements monster density makes solo travel dangerous. Caravans are normal. Trains and airships for long haul. Overland surfaces and day-mile bands: `../Science/Vehicle/Roads.md` (Earth medieval–Victorian ladder; Caldris stays mostly dirt / stone patch with magitech long-haul).
