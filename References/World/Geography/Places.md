# Places

Fat place notes: `PlacesDesign.md`. Population / monster estimates: `PlacesPopulation.md`. Source inventory: `../../SourceLoot/Places.md`.

## Narrative

Early story starts at the Arden estate in Caldris, then moves through towns and roads. Carwen is the first adventuring town. Edelgard is the craft city he aims for next. Later travel hits southern ports and Dragnis Island, where Albrook sits by a new dungeon.

## Detail

**Kingdom:** Caldris. Human-majority. Temperate lowlands. Earth-sized world. 24-hour day. Calendar **12 × 30 + New Year's Day** (**361** days / year). Two moons (red and blue): `../Space/Moons.md`.

**Neighbors (kept):** Alexandria (sun-elf holy kingdom). Bolia (moon-elf lands). Hatfordian Empire (border power; ceasefire fortresses on the Caldris side).

**Arden estate:** Roland's starting home. Baron Wentworth's knight house. Library, mansion dungeon training, then send-off toward Carwen.

**Lustile:** Academy city in Caldris (arts / weaponsmith / mage facilities). Early safe-path option Wentworth refused.

**Carwen:** Mid-kingdom walled adventurer town. Guild, inn, mana-train station, nearby dungeon and goblin forest. First party forms here. Cabin lodging outside town early on.

**Edelgard:** Craft / mountain city. Dwarves and gnomes common. Mines and smith trade. Singing Crow inn. Grimboodle's Magic Emporium and workshops. No local dungeon (hired clears / wild work). Higher inn rates than Carwen.

**Luden:** Southern port city. Caravan / sail hub toward Dragnis Island and Albrook.

**Dragnis Island:** Large southern Caldris island under an aristocrat Duke. Central volcano and S-rank main dungeon (dragon last boss; magus-tower barrier talk). Coast warm/green. Smaller dungeons link toward the main. Export boom in mana stones and minerals.

**Albrook:** Boom town in a Dragnis valley by a months-old volcanic side dungeon. Wall and guild under construction early. Absentee noble; commoner mayor. Farm / workshop peg outside town. Roland registers and works here under the cover name **Wayland** (person identity, not a place). Auction house and shops land as the boom matures.

**Albrook Dungeon:** Labyrinth-type volcanic dungeon believed linked toward the main Dragnis Infernal Dragon dungeon. ~**10** floors. Ambient uses `C = C(h) + k×(D/N)` with high **D** (crazy boss / link) and large **N** so upper floors stay mild (`Dungeons.md`). Heat rises deeper. Crowded upper floors. Deep floor layout stays chapter loot until a beat needs it.

**Travel:** Mana-stone trains look like old steam trains and haul passenger / freight wagons (slow multi-day rides). Airships / flying fortresses exist and cost far more. No practical teleportation. Caravans and sail fill the gaps trains do not.

**Faith sites:** Church of Solaria (sun faith) common in Caldris towns. Churches produce class-change stones and run orphanages.
