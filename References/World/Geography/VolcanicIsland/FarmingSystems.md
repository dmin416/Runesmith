## Part 1: Real World

### Crop Production Basics
- **Soil prep:** moldboard plowing, chisel plowing, disc harrowing, rotary tilling, no-till and strip-till
- **Soil fertility:** NPK fertilizers, lime for pH, gypsum, manure, compost, green manure, biochar
- **Soil management:** crop rotation, cover crops (clover, rye, vetch), fallowing, terracing, contour plowing, windbreaks
- **Planting:** broadcast seeding, seed drills, row planters, transplanting seedlings, grafting fruit trees
- **Crop protection:** herbicides, insecticides, fungicides, integrated pest management, beneficial insects, row covers, netting, scarecrows, fencing
- **Harvest:** hand picking, sickles and scythes, mechanical reapers, combines, forage harvesters, tree shakers, potato diggers, cotton pickers
- **Post-harvest:** threshing, winnowing, drying, grading, cleaning, cold storage, root cellars, silos, grain bins, curing (tobacco, onions, squash)

### Crop Categories
- **Grains:** wheat, corn, rice, barley, oats, rye, sorghum, millet
- **Oilseeds:** soybeans, sunflower, canola, flax, peanuts
- **Roots and tubers:** potatoes, sweet potatoes, cassava, beets, carrots, turnips
- **Vegetables:** tomatoes, peppers, lettuce, cabbage, beans, peas, squash, onions, garlic
- **Fruit:** orchard trees (apple, pear, cherry, peach, citrus), vineyards, berries
- **Fiber:** cotton, flax, hemp, wool from livestock
- **Forage:** alfalfa, clover, timothy, silage corn, pasture grass
- **Specialty:** hops, herbs, mushrooms, tea, coffee, sugarcane, maple syrup

### Livestock and Animal Products
- **Cattle:** dairy and beef
- **Poultry:** chickens, ducks, turkeys, geese, quail
- **Swine:** pigs for pork and lard
- **Small ruminants:** sheep (wool, meat, milk), goats (milk, meat, mohair, cashmere)
- **Bees:** honey, wax, pollination services
- **Aquaculture:** fish ponds, shrimp, oysters, aquaponics
- **Working animals:** horses, oxen, mules, donkeys, herding dogs
- **Byproducts:** leather, bone meal, blood meal, tallow, feathers, manure

### Water and Irrigation
- Flood and furrow irrigation
- Sprinkler systems (fixed, traveling gun)
- Center pivot and linear move systems
- Drip irrigation and subsurface drip
- Wells, windmill pumps, canals, reservoirs, cisterns, qanats
- Drainage tile and ditches for wet ground

### Pre-Oil Mechanization (Steam and Muscle Era)
- Jethro Tull seed drill
- McCormick mechanical reaper
- Horse-drawn cultivators, rakes and hay tedders
- Threshing machines powered by horse sweeps or steam
- Steam traction engines pulling gang plows
- Steam-powered sawmills, gristmills and cotton gins
- Windmills for grinding grain and pumping water
- Water wheels driving mills
- Grain elevators with bucket conveyors
- Incubators heated by stoves for hatching eggs

### Modern Automation
- **Tractors:** GPS autosteer, fully autonomous tractors, implement control systems
- **Precision agriculture:** yield monitors, soil mapping, variable-rate seeding and fertilizer, section control on sprayers
- **Drones:** crop scouting, multispectral imaging, spraying, seeding
- **Sensors:** soil moisture probes, weather stations, leaf wetness sensors, nitrogen sensors
- **Smart irrigation:** controllers that water based on soil moisture and weather forecasts
- **Robotic weeding:** camera-guided mechanical weeders, laser weeders
- **Harvest robots:** strawberry pickers, apple vacuum harvesters, lettuce cutters
- **Dairy:** robotic milking machines, automatic feed pushers, manure scraper robots, activity collars that detect illness and heat cycles
- **Poultry:** automatic feeders and waterers, egg collection belts, climate-controlled houses, automated incubators
- **Swine:** electronic sow feeders, slatted floors with manure pits
- **Grain handling:** automated dryers, aeration fans, bin monitoring cables
- **Indoor farming:** hydroponics, aeroponics, vertical farms with LED lighting and climate control
- **Sorting:** optical sorters for fruit, grain and nuts by color, size and defects

## Part 3: Magic Applications (Runesmith World Draft)

Growth biology limits when magic speeds crops: `Crops.md` (Accelerated Growth). Materials named below must match `References/Runes/` and economy notes when locked.

### Water and Weather
- **Rune irrigation:** a mana stone powers a rune array converting mana into water phase, feeding sprinkler heads or drip lines without wells
- **Mithril nozzles:** sprinkler heads made from low-grade mithril shape and spread the water burst efficiently
- **Aurium-lined pipes:** mana insulation in irrigation lines lets a central mana stone feed an entire field system with no burn on the pipes
- **Frost and heat wards:** rune posts that hold field temperature steady against early frost, extending seasons like a Greenhouse
- **Rain calling:** high-tier weather magic as a royal or church service, priced on spell tier and quality

### Soil and Growth
- **Kinetic tilling:** a rune plow that converts mana into kinetic force to break sod without draft animals
- **Mana-enriched soil:** fields near dungeons or at altitude growing herbs and alchemical crops that only thrive in dense mana
- **Growth acceleration:** a Speed-Gro equivalent where a druid or farmer with an evolved Farming skill pushes mana into crops, costing MP per plot
- **Farming skill ladder (draft; not in Progression yet):** Basic Farming → Farming → Expert Farming → Master Farming → Grandmaster Farming. Each tier would raise yield, quality or plot coverage. Lock names and bonuses only after checking `References/Progression/Skills.md` / SkillsDesign.

### Labor and Livestock
- **Tamed monster plow teams:** monster beasts pulling gang plows faster and longer than oxen
- **Slime composters:** slimes kept in hutches digesting farm waste into fertilizer
- **Needle worm production:** twine, baling cord, netting, row covers, wax and natural pesticides from venoms and formic acid
- **Golem labor:** rune-driven constructs for harvesting, carrying and feeding, the Junimo Hut equivalent
- **Animal bonding skills:** a herder skill that improves product quality like friendship hearts

### Protection
- **Ward fences:** rune posts that repel pests, crows and low-level monsters
- **Monster alarm stones:** detection runes that alert the farmhouse when goblins or beasts cross the boundary
- **Blight dispelling:** cleansing magic from the Church of Solaria for crop disease

### Storage and Processing
- **Spatial storage bags:** harvest stored without spoilage or volume limits, replacing silos and cellars for wealthy farms
- **Cold rune cellars:** frost runes powered by mana stones as refrigeration
- **Stasis enchantments:** preservation magic slowing decay, a magic Preserves Jar
- **Steam-and-mana mills:** steam threshers and gristmills boosted by mana stones instead of coal
- **Dandelion rubber belts:** conveyor belts in grain elevators and processing mills (draft material; confirm before canon use)

## Part 4: Ideas (Story and Worldbuilding Hooks)

### Economy
- Farms near dungeons command higher land prices from dense mana
- Monster breeding speed makes frontier farmland dangerous and cheap
- Runesmiths are rare, so rune irrigation and ward fences stay expensive luxury upgrades for nobles and rich guild farms
- Commoner farms run on steam, tamed monsters and muscle while noble estates like House Arden's run on runes
- Monster bone meal from culled remains gives culling quotas an agricultural market

### Society and Faith
- Church of Solaria harvest festivals tied to blight cleansing and sun blessings
- Guild boards posting farm defense and culling jobs at Bronze and Steel rank
- Farming class tiers creating a respected rural profession path alongside fighters and crafters

### Plot Seeds
- A runesmith designing the first affordable rune sprinkler disrupts the noble estate monopoly
- A dungeon break overruns farmland and forces a mass culling
- High-altitude mana farms becoming contested territory between kingdoms
- Golem harvesters putting laborers and slaves out of work
- Tamed monster plow teams escaping and breeding wild
