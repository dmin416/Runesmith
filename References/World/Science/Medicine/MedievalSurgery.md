# Medieval Surgeon with Modern Knowledge

Design loot / invent scenario. Not Terra street law. Sibling files hold technique depth. Blunt trauma healing: `../Body/BluntTraumaHealing.md`. Toxin recovery: `../Body/ToxinExposureRecovery.md`. Antibiotics: `Penicillin.md`, `Antibiotics.md`. Hub: `Index.md`.

## Setting assumptions

- One surgeon with complete modern medical, surgical, chemical and engineering knowledge in memory.
- Medieval material base: iron and steel smithing, glassblowing, distillation, silk and linen, animal gut, beeswax, honey, plant drugs (opium poppy, willow, henbane), salt, lime, plant ash, sulfur, vitriol.
- **Barrier magic** (modest): small shaped force planes or bubbles that can press, hold, seal or exclude.
- **Alcohol**: distillation available, so concentrated ethanol is possible.
- **Magic knives**: assumed to hold a perfect edge that never dulls and can be cleaned instantly. If they also cauterize or seal vessels, this is noted where it changes the procedure.
- **Suction**: continuous controllable suction with a tip and a collection vessel.
