# Mobile Home And Caravan Amenities

What a traveling or parked living box can carry. Filter modern RV lists into Caldris invent bands. Power density beats for wood vs batteries. Roads: `Roads.md`. Pumps / tanks: `../Energy/Pumps.md`.

## Narrative

A wagon home is a kitchen, bed, waste loop and climate shell on wheels. Baseline Caldris gets stove, bunk, chests and a chamber pot. Everything else is wealth, magitech or Earth-memory invent.

## Detail

### Amenity bands

| Band | Examples |
|---|---|
| Baseline caravan | Solid bunk, stove / brazier, pantry chest, water cask, chamber pot, oil lamp, shutters |
| Craftsman road home | Separate cook corner, ice box / mage-cold box, wash basin, hanging pantry, vent fan, better bedding |
| Rich / invent RV | Full bath loop (fresh / gray / black), tankless or tank heat, slide rooms, solar or mana-stone bank, inverter-like convertors, leveling jacks, outdoor cook |
| Parked mobile house | Skirting, porch, shed, full HVAC, laundry, multi-room plan, anchors |

Categories to raid when designing a build: kitchen, bath, sleep, living, climate, power, water/plumbing, exterior storage, safety (smoke / CO / exits).

### Climate efficiency (useful heat)

| Method | Efficiency to space |
|---|---|
| Open campfire | 5–10% |
| Open fireplace | 10–20% |
| Propane / oil furnace | 60–80% |
| Tight wood stove | 70–80% |
| Electric resistance | ~100% at point of use |
| Heat pump | 200–400% (COP 2–4) |

### Energy density

| Source | Energy per kg |
|---|---|
| Dry wood | ~4.5 kWh |
| LiFePO4-class battery | 0.1–0.15 kWh |
| Lead-acid | 0.03–0.04 kWh |

Rough checks: one 2×4×8 (~4 kg) ≈ **18 kWh** chemical. One cord firewood ≈ **6000–7000 kWh**. 12 V 100 Ah lead-acid ≈ **1.2 kWh** total (~0.6 usable).

**Heat delivered from that 2×4:** campfire ~0.9–1.8 kWh; good stove ~12–14 kWh; battery resistance ~0.6–1.2 kWh; battery + heat pump ~1.2–4.8 kWh.

Burn untreated wood only. Treated lumber fumes are poison.
