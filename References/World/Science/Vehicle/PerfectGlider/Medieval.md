# Perfect Glider: Medieval Setting

Hub: [PerfectGlider.md](PerfectGlider.md).

## Launch methods

| Method | Design A | Design B | Design C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot launch into wind | No | No | **Main method.** Jog into a 10 to 20 km/h breeze |
| Bungee crew off a hill | Works at 200 kg | Needs a cliff drop after release | Works |
| Windlass winch (horse or crew) | Best method, 300 to 500 m height | Works | Works |
| Horse tow | Works at 200 kg | Works | Works |
| Cliff or tower dive | Not needed | Main method, ~25 to 35 m drop | Not needed |
| Slope soaring | Easy in any steady wind | Shelled version only, strong wind only | Light wind only. Not safe in 30+ km/h wind |

## Care and upkeep

The castings are magic. The joints are not forgiving. Keeping these wings airworthy takes the routine of a modern sailplane club, not the casual care given to a cart or a saddle.

| Task | When | Medieval-available means |
|---|---|---|
| Clean every joint and sleeve face | Before each packing | Soft brush, clean water, linen cloth |
| Check every taper seats fully | Each deployment | Feel and sight check, written checklist |
| Replace seal strips | Every few flights or when torn | Waxed silk or oiled linen tape, beeswax |
| Lubricate hex shaft joints | Regularly | Tallow or graphite |
| Inspect and patch ballast bladders | Before each ballast fill | Oiled silk or waxed leather, gut bladders |
| Strain ballast water | Every fill | Cloth strainer |
| Store packed wing | Always | Sealed, dry case kept off sand and dirt |

- **Discipline is the real requirement.** A medieval workshop can supply every consumable. What it must add is a written rigging checklist followed on every flight. One skipped joint check or one grain of sand can ground the wing or cost lift in the air.
- **Setting compatibility:**
  - **Unpowered A, B and C** and **human-powered C** fit a medieval world with magic castings.
  - **Engines, batteries, solar cells and jet turbines** do not exist there. Those versions need a time traveler's supplies or an equivalent magic power source.
