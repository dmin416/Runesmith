# Perfect Glider: Hawaiian Islands

Hub: [PerfectGlider.md](PerfectGlider.md).

Trade winds blow from the east-northeast at roughly 25 to 36 km/h below a ~2,000 m inversion. Above it, winds are light or westerly. All values use the **Real** case.

**Design A results:**
- Every channel westbound from ridge-lift height.
- Every channel eastbound except Kaʻieʻie Waho, using ballast.
- Kauaʻi to Oʻahu eastbound needs full ballast plus a climb above the ~2,000 m inversion. Only possible in lighter trades.
- **Mauna Kea to Kauaʻi in one glide (471 km): not possible.** Reach is 310 to 340 km depending on load. Two ridge-lift climbs on the way (Maui and Molokaʻi or Oʻahu) make the trip work in about 6 to 7 hours.

- Only the short Maui County channels (11 to 15 km) are reachable unpowered. Each needs a high launch point.
- **Shelled:** Puʻu Kukui on West Maui (1,764 m) covers Pailolo and ʻAuʻau. Kamakou on Molokaʻi (1,512 m) covers Kalohi to Lānaʻi. Haleakalā (3,055 m) covers ʻAlalākeiki.

**Design C results:**
- **Westbound:** Every channel works from ridge-lift height. The trade tailwind roughly doubles its glide.
- **Eastbound:** Only the short Maui County channels work unpowered. Its slow speed makes headwinds brutal.
- **Strong trades are a hard limit.** Its best-glide speed (32 to 35 km/h) is close to strong trade wind speed. In 30+ km/h trades it cannot ridge soar safely: it gets pushed back over the ridge into the turbulent rotor behind it, where its weak roll authority is at its worst. Design C is a light-trade (under ~25 km/h) craft in Hawaii.
- **Pedaling does not fix eastbound crossings.** At ~29 km/h pedaling airspeed into 25 km/h trades, ground speed is ~4 km/h.
- **Mauna Kea to Kauaʻi in one glide: not possible.** Reach is 250 to 280 km shelled and 210 to 230 km exposed. Three or four ridge-lift climbs on the way make it work, in light trades only.
