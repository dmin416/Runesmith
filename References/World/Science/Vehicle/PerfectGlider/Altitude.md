# Perfect Glider: Maximum Altitude

Hub: [PerfectGlider.md](PerfectGlider.md).

Applies to all designs. Design B only reaches these heights in wave or very strong thermals.

| Lift source | Typical top | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary thermals | 1,500 to 3,000 m | Stop at cloud base |
| Desert thermals | 5,000 to 6,000 m | Nevada, Namibia, Australian outback |
| Hawaiian thermals and ridge lift | 1,500 to 2,500 m | Capped by the trade wind inversion |
| Thunderstorm updrafts | 16 to 17 km in the tropics | 20 to 50 m/s updrafts |
| Mountain lee waves | 23,203 m (record) | Theoretical reach ~27 km |

- **Record:** Perlan 2 reached 76,124 ft (23,203 m) over the Andes in Argentina on September 2, 2018. It was designed to exceed 90,000 ft (27 km).
- **Thunderstorms:** The airframe survives. Hail, ice buildup, lightning and −60 °C air remain lethal. Design C's poor roll authority makes storm turbulence especially dangerous for it.
- **Design C in wave:** Strong wave winds aloft (often 70 to 150 km/h) blow it backward. It can climb in wave but not travel in it.

| Altitude | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Above ~3,800 m | Supplemental oxygen |
| Above ~12,000 m | Pressure-breathing oxygen |
| Above ~19,000 m (Armstrong limit) | Full pressure suit or sealed cabin |
