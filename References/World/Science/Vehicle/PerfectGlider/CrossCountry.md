# Perfect Glider: Cross-Country Speed Over Land

Hub: [PerfectGlider.md](PerfectGlider.md).

Average speed including thermal climbs, Real case (MacCready optimal cruise speed in parentheses).

| Thermal strength | A 200 kg | A 500 kg | A 900 kg | C shelled | C exposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 m/s (weak) | 54 km/h (89) | 75 km/h (128) | 92 km/h (163) | 39 km/h (65) | 33 km/h (54) |
| 2 m/s (typical) | 70 km/h (112) | 99 km/h (159) | 122 km/h (199) | 51 km/h (82) | 43 km/h (68) |
| 4 m/s (strong) | 91 km/h (143) | 128 km/h (202) | 158 km/h (246) | 66 km/h (105) | 55 km/h (86) |

- **Design A:** A good summer day gives **500 to 1,200 km** depending on load.
- **Design B:** Ordinary thermals give no climb. In strong desert conditions it manages short hops of 20 to 60 km between climbs.
- **Design C:** A good summer day gives **250 to 500 km** in light wind. Its cruise speeds of 65 to 105 km/h are where its glide ratio and roll authority are both reasonable. It climbs in thermals too weak for any other design and lands almost anywhere.
