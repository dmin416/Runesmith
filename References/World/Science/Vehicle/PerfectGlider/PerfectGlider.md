# The Perfect Glider

Gliders cast from a material with infinite strength and infinite stiffness. Once cast, no part bends, flexes or flutters. Cast parts can be made as thin and as light as desired.

All three wings collapse as nested telescoping sleeves. Pods and pilot shells collapse as nested rings.

| Design | Summary |
|---|---|
| **A "Tandem"** | Two people, 31 m span, glide ratio 77 Ideal / 72 Real |
| **B "Falcon-scale"** | One person, 100 kg total, 3.5 m span backpack wings |
| **C "Pocket Wing"** | One person, 100 kg total, 25 m telescoping wing that lands on foot |

**Use the Real numbers.** They are the working model for every plan, route and story beat. Ideal is a ceiling only.
