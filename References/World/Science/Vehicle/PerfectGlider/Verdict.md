# Perfect Glider: Verdict

Hub: [PerfectGlider.md](PerfectGlider.md).

| Role | Best design | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday utility | **C** | Foot launch, foot landing, human-powered, solar-assisted, weak-thermal climber |
| Cross-country and windy crossings | **A** | Ballast for wind penetration, high speed, best glide, stable in rough air with wing ballast |
| Stunt, dynamic soaring, powered dart | **B** | Smallest span, agile, fast. Glide and landing are poor |

## Confidence

| Claim | Confidence |
|---|---|
| Glide ratios, sink rates, stall speeds, power needs (Real case) | High. Standard aerodynamics. This is the final model |
| Ideal case | Ceiling only. Not for planning |
| Gust loads and roll rates | Medium. Standard approximations. Assumes full outboard flaperon travel |
| Clearance budget | Medium. Nearly full on A and C. Any growth costs drag, pack size or grit tolerance |
| Joint penalty (Real case) | Medium. Depends on clearance, sealing and maintenance |
| Packed sizes | Lowest. Assumes sleeves nest, seat and seal as described. If extra clearance is needed, sleeve count rises or taper must increase |
| Belly-skid landing loads | Low. Rough estimate only |
