# Falling

Free-fall thresholds, posture and Shepherd's Leap pole brake. Soft-fall spells stay separate. Hub: `../Science.md`.

## Narrative

Low falls are about landing skill. High falls are about speed and what breaks first. When mana is empty, a planted pole can stretch the stop over meters.

## Detail

### Free fall (no drag yardstick)

```
v = √(2 g h)     // g = 9.81
KE = ½ m v²      // tables use m = 100 kg
h = v² / (2 g)
```

Under ~**10 m**, drag barely matters. Terminal ≈ **50 m/s** spread-out, ≈ **80 m/s** feet-first.

| Outcome | Height | Speed | KE (100 kg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 0.5 m | 3.1 m/s | 0.49 kJ |
| Deep knee bend | 2 m | 6.3 m/s | 2.0 kJ |
| Crawl roll, trained | 3.5 m | 8.3 m/s | 3.4 kJ |
| Roll, some damage | 3.5–7 m | 8.3–11.7 m/s | 3.4–6.9 kJ |
| Feet-first bone risk | 3–6 m | 7.7–10.8 m/s | 2.9–5.9 kJ |
| Catastrophic | 10–15 m | 14–17 m/s | 9.8–14.7 kJ |
| ~50% death yardstick | ~12–15 m+ | ~15+ m/s | ~12 kJ+ |
| Death near certain | 25 m+ | 22+ m/s | 24.5 kJ+ |

Roll helps by raising stop distance: `F ≈ m v² / (2 d)`. Same 8.3 m/s over 0.1 m ≈ 34 kN; over 1 m ≈ 3.4 kN.

Feet-first loads ankles/spine early. Flat on hard ground is not “safer” at catastrophic speed (organ shock).

### Shepherd's Leap (pole brake)

Empty-mana emergency. Plant a **2.5–4 m** pole, slide the shaft, brake over meters. Earth shepherd practice: dead drops up to ~**2× pole length** (~8 m on a 4 m pole), often **6–9 m** band in popular reports. Goat fat on the shaft lowers μ.

Story use: bag pole → plant → slide. Not a soft-fall spell. Soft ground and tip plant quality decide whether the brake holds.
