# Focus Capacity

Hub: `../Science.md`. Multitasking skill and Parallel Thinking: `../../../Progression/SkillsDesign.md`. Mental attributes: `../../../Progression/Training.md`. Full-sphere vision load at N-men capacity: `Vision360.md`. Crystal / LLM / mind-copy holding file: `CrystalMinds.md`.

**Capacity** is the absolute mental budget in **men-worth** (1× = one average adult's full deliberate mind). Multitasking and Parallel Thinking only carve and tax this number. Without capacity, their % and parallel counts are empty.

Earth anchor: one intense stream at a time; working memory ~4 items inside that stream. Same-intent copies under one focus (eight Mana Hands all turning the same skewers) stay **one** task, like clenching fingers and toes while dancing.

## Capacity (men-worth)

Raised by Intelligence and high Parallel Thinking. Soft feel bands below; lock hard INT breakpoints later if needed.

| Cap | Feel | Solo focus (men) |
|---|---|---|
| 1x | Street average. One full deliberate mind. | 1.0 |
| 2x | Sharp specialist / gifted student. | 2.0 |
| 3x | Top human intellect under load. | 3.0 |
| 5x | Early superhuman crunch (heavy mage / engineer). | 5.0 |
| 10x | Mental work of ten men when fully spent. | 10.0 |
| 15x | Research-team mind (high Parallel Thinking). | 15.0 |
| 20x | Small bureau of thinkers in one skull. | 20.0 |
| 50x | Institutional brain. | 50.0 |
| 100x | City-scale intellect (apex invent). | 100.0 |

**Spend rules (always multiply by capacity):**
- Solo: effective = `1.0 × capacity`
- Multitasking main (anchored): `(100% − split%) × capacity` at full use
- Multitasking side: `(split% ÷ K) × skill efficacy × capacity`
- Multitasking even N-way: `(1 ÷ N) × skill efficacy × capacity` each
- Parallel Thinking: `(capacity ÷ skill level)` each, at **100%** of that fraction

## Multitasking (skill): % factors only

One mind. Extra exertion. Peels a **split %** off main across **K** side tasks. Side slices run at **skill efficacy**; main remainder stays full.

**Skill efficacy = 10% × level** (L1 = 10% … L9 = 90%).

| Skill L | Efficacy |
|---|---|
| 1 | 10% |
| 2 | 20% |
| 3 | 30% |
| 4 | 40% |
| 5 | 50% |
| 6 | 60% |
| 7 | 70% |
| 8 | 80% |
| 9 | 90% |

**Side effective men** = `(split% ÷ K) × efficacy × capacity`  
**Main men** = `(100% − split%) × capacity`  
**Even N-way each** = `(1 ÷ N) × efficacy × capacity`

| 2 | 20% | 50% | 10% | 5% each | 5% | 2% each | 2% each |

## Parallel Thinking (skill)

Literal separate thought trains. **Skill level = N parallels** (L1–L9, then evolves). Each gets `capacity ÷ N` at **100%** (no Multitasking tax). Higher level raises N. Not separate personalities. Columns past 9 are post-evolve ranks.
