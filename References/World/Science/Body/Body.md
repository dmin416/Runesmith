# Body

Living-body anchors for heat, limbs, stealth and teeth. Cast law: `../../../Runes/Energy.md`. Grill / resistance grind ties Progression. Hub: `../Science.md`.

## Narrative

Extra limbs cost attention more than cortex area. Skin heat moves with `Q = mcΔT`. Hiding a heat signature is continuous watts. Kids' teeth follow Earth eruption ages unless the story says otherwise.

## Detail

### Extra limbs (nerves / brain)

Arm: ~5 named nerves from the brachial plexus, on the order of **10⁵** fibers (about ⅓ motor). Cortex footprint for one extra arm is low single-digit percent. Bottleneck is **attention and coordination** (premotor / parietal / cerebellum already near full on two hands). Practice can drop cost from near-full focus toward automatic (extra-thumb remapping studies). No fixed % tax. Starts high, falls with training. Applies to Mana Hands multitask, grafts and golem-limb control feel.

### Skin heat / cool

```
Q = m × c × ΔT
c_skin ≈ 3.5 J/(g·°C)
ρ ≈ 1.1 g/cm³
thickness (epidermis+dermis) ≈ 1.5 mm
m ≈ 1.65 kg per m² ≈ 3 kg for 1.8 m² adult
```

| Case | ΔT | Whole body Q |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen skin 0 °C → 20 °C | 20 | ~210 kJ |
| Heat-stressed 45 °C → 33 °C | 12 | ~126 kJ |

Per m²: ~115 kJ and ~69 kJ. Friction/vibration paths need more input (~60–80% efficient → cold case ~260–350 kJ real). Scale with mass and ΔT. Mana from `Mana = J / (10 × η × μ)`.

### Thermal grill drill

Safe Earth illusion: mild warm (~40 °C) and cool (~20 °C) bars feel like burn without damage. Story grind can push past that into **shallow** real burn/frost so Heat/Cold/Pain/Recovery rank, plus fine Shaping/Regulation. Keep damage shallow. Potion top-up when Recovery lags. Ned tip-pokes optional. Deep tissue is waste.

### Heat-signature stealth

Sustained power, not one-shot ΔT:

```
Q_loss ≈ h × A × ΔT
Resting metabolism ≈ 100 W
Mana/s = P / (10 × η × μ)
```

Example: at ~100 W, INT40 L2 (~22 J/mana) ≈ **4.6 mana/s**; INT500 L9 (~500 J/mana) ≈ **0.2 mana/s**. Treat as a sustain line. Same style for small continuous vacuum-seal leaks later.

### Tooth ages (approx.)

**Primary lost:** 5–7 centrals; 7–8 laterals; 9–11 canines + first molars; 10–12 second molars.

**Permanent erupt:** 6–7 first molars + lower centrals; 7–8 upper centrals / lower laterals; 8–9 upper laterals; 9–12 canines/premolars; 11–13 second molars; wisdom ~17–20s or never.

By ~12–13: adult **28** (no wisdom). Up to **32** with all wisdom.

### Recoil a street adult can take

About **80 kg**, street baseline STR/AGI **15** (`../../../Progression/Training.md`). Almost any total impulse is fine if spread out. Limits are peak force and how fast it lands. Launch math: `../Energy/Kinetic.md`, `../Energy/Projectiles.md`.

**Sharp kick into the shoulder (recoil energy):**

| Recoil energy | Feels like | Typical impulse |
|---|---|---|
| Under 20 J | Hunting rifle, fine all day | 10 to 15 N·s |
| 40 to 60 J | Heavy magnum, painful after a few shots | 15 to 25 N·s |
| 100 to 140 J | Elephant rifle, bruising, a few shots max | 25 to 35 N·s |
| 200+ J | Injury risk | |

Same impulse hurts less from a heavier weapon: recoil energy = impulse² ÷ (2 × weapon mass).

**Steady push (force):**
- Braced standing man holds about **300 to 500 N** before being pushed off balance.
- Around **500 to 600 N**, boots start sliding on normal ground.
- 400 N for 0.1 / 0.5 / 1 s → 40 / 200 / 400 N·s total impulse.

**Launcher examples (60 g arrow):**
- 1 km/s → 60 N·s; over 0.2 s ≈ 300 N. Easy.
- 3 km/s → 180 N·s; over 0.5 s ≈ 360 N. Manageable braced.
- Much past ~400 N·s needs prone, wall brace or a mount.
