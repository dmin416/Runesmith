# ART (Gut Check)

Autonomic Response Testing, lean/sway and dowsing as one readout family. Hub: `../Science.md`. Distinct from Mana Sense and from Basic Perception. Hub: `../Science.md`.

## Narrative

The body ranks weak field and resonance cues before conscious thought. The answer shows as motor tone: indicator muscle, rods/pendulum or a lean. Same channel under different tools. Empty symbolic puzzles (card faces, passwords) have nothing for the channel to rank.

## Detail

### Premise (locked)

ART, muscle testing, dowsing and body lean/sway are the **same family**. They report autonomic and tissue state. Agreement across forms is corroboration, not separate magics.

| Form | What moves | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ART / muscle test | Indicator muscle or finger ring | Substance, organ, field, yes/no with examiner |
| Dowsing | Rods, stick, pendulum | Locate / rank at a distance |
| Lean / sway | Whole-body balance | Solo ask and feel the drift |

### What it can read

- Toxins, pathogens, allergens, fields and stressors below awareness  
- Proximity / resonance of real substances and goods (biocompatible fit + potency feel for that body)  
- Organ-level localization when the map is clear  
- Magnetic polarity bias in many regions (north tends to strengthen indicator, south weaken)  
- Spatial threat feel (open depth / void under a surface) when organized  

Ultra-low threshold vs ordinary senses. Each ANS is person-specific.

### What it cannot read

- Biologically / materially identical options (face-down identical cards, pure labels, codes)  
- Exception: if the subject **already knows** and the body storms, you read **their stress**, not the ink  

### What breaks it

- Dysbiosis (noisy retests)  
- Switching (mixed laterality → opposite answers until cleared)  
- Stacked toxin / trauma / chronic EMF load  
- Examiner leading or bad challenges  
- Empty challenges (no field difference)  

EMF / pulsed RF biases toward sympathetic noise. Proximity matters. Do not hang locks on red-cell membrane damage claims. Use ANS readout and symptom load.

### Practical use

Screen rooms, substances, exposure and goods. Mitigation should retest stronger. Blinded checks only when the hidden difference is real in field/tissue terms.
