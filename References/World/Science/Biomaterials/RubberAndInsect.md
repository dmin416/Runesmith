# Rubber And Insect Materials

Latex rubber, vulcanization and insect-derived craft stocks. Animal-material process: `Biomaterials.md`. Ned harvest: `../../../People/Ned.md`.

## Narrative

Rubber is plant latex turned into elastic or hard goods. Insects and spiders supply silk, glues, waxes, dyes, chitin and spring protein. Ned can push toward the record holders on both strength and stick when his silk / gel / hemolymph paths mature.

## Detail

### Rubber

Natural rubber = cis-polyisoprene in latex (~30–40% rubber in Hevea). Gutta-percha / balata = trans form → hard, leathery thermoplastic.

**Collect:** tap bark (trees ~6–7 years). Coagulate by smoke paddle, acid, then sheet or crepe.

**Mesoamerican mix** (*Castilla* latex + morning-glory juice): ~50/50 max bounce (balls); ~75/25 max wear (soles); pure latex for glue.

**Vulcanize (S + heat):** crosslinks stop melt/cold crack.

| Product | Sulfur band | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Soft rubber | ~1–3% | Elastic cloth, hose, tires |
| Hard rubber (vulcanite) | ~30–40% | Machinable black solid (combs, insulators) |

Carbon black multiplies abrasion life. Products: rubberized cloth, molded goods, dipped latex, solid/pneumatic tires, buffers, insulation.

**Caldris:** natural latex regions or monster analogues. Vulcanization is an invent unlock (controlled S + heat). Until then: raw/Meso-style mixes, gutta-hard goods, or Ned/resilin springs.

### Insect / spider toolkit

| Need | Material | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Cord / armor cloth | Silk, spider dragline | Dragline ~steel by thickness, ~5× by weight; bark-spider toughness champion |
| Spring | Resilin | ~97% energy return (beats rubber) |
| Hard plate / tip | Sclerotized cuticle; Zn/Mn cuticle | Horn analog; wear edges |
| Fast glue | Hemolymph phenoloxidase | Crosslinks / melanizes in minutes |
| Underwater glue | Caddisfly silk | Phosphate–calcium wet bond |
| Instant grab | Aggregate glue droplets | Capture spiral |
| Dry grip | Cribellate nano-fuzz | No liquid |
| Varnish / hot melt | Shellac | Lac resin |
| Film / dressing | Chitosan (from chitin) | Acid-soluble |
| Dye | Cochineal, kermes, lac | Reds |
| Tan / ink | Gall tannins | Leather + iron-gall ink |
| Wax | Beeswax, insect wax | Candles, lost-wax, waterproof |

**Silk process:** fibroin core + sericin gum (~20–30%). Degum in hot soapy water. One cocoon ~300–900 m reelable. ~2500–3000 cocoons / lb raw. Kill pupa before emerge or the thread breaks. Spun silk from pierced cocoons.
