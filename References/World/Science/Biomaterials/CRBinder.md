## 1. Core Concept

The bug secretes a concentrated protein blend. The collagen fraction supplies stiffness, tensile strength and a template for mineral growth. The resilin fraction supplies elasticity, fatigue resistance and energy return. Diluted in water and worked into cheap local bulk (soil, sand, gravel, crushed stone, limestone dust, plant fiber, dissolved sea minerals), a very small mass of binder controls the structure and behavior of a very large mass of finished material.

This is closer to real biology than it sounds. Nature already builds hard materials where a small organic fraction dictates the behavior of a large mineral fraction.

| Natural material | Organic fraction by mass (real, approx.) | Bulk phase | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone | 20-30% (mostly collagen type I) | Hydroxyapatite | Stiff, tough, self-repairing |
| Nacre (mother of pearl) | 3-5% | Aragonite tablets | Work of fracture roughly 1,000 to 3,000 times higher than plain aragonite |
| Tooth enamel | about 1% | Hydroxyapatite | Hardest tissue in the body |
| Sea urchin spine | under 0.2% | Calcite | Trace protein changes how a single crystal fractures |
| Glass sponge spicule | thin protein core | Silica | Flexible glass rods |
| Insect cuticle | nearly 100% organic | Chitin plus protein | Armor assembled from water-processed protein |
| Termite mound | Small (saliva and secretions) | Local clay and sand | Mounds several meters tall that stand for decades with built-in ventilation |
| Paper wasp nest | Small (saliva protein) | Chewed wood fiber | Light, water-shedding paper |
| Sandcastle worm reef | Small dabs of protein cement | Sand grains and shell fragments | Reefs assembled underwater from loose sand |
| Caddisfly larval case | Thin silk | Pebbles, sand, twigs | Stone tubes bound together underwater |
| Hagfish slime | About 0.004% solids | Seawater | A few grams of secretion gels liters of water in a fraction of a second |

Termites, paper wasps, sandcastle worms and caddisfly larvae are the closest real analogs to the bug. Each secretes a small amount of protein or silk and turns local soil, wood, sand or pebbles into large durable structures.

The magic required is narrower than "turn water into stone." It covers three things:

1. **Efficiency.** Each unit of binder organizes far more bulk than any real biopolymer.
2. **Energy.** Mana pays the energy cost that kilns, bacteria or electric current pay in the real world.
3. **Water locking (high-magic tier only).** Concentrated binder holds water itself as a structural solid.

## 3. The Central Problem: Mass Balance

Water plus 1% protein makes a hydrogel. Dry that gel and only the 1% protein remains as a thin film. A ton of finished solid needs a ton of something. Three tiers solve this.

| Tier | Source of mass | Magic level | Permanence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Binder plus aggregate | Soil, sand, gravel, stone, fiber | Low | Permanent once cured |
| 2. Mineral harvesting | Dissolved minerals in seawater or brine plus carbon dioxide | Medium | Permanent (real minerals) |
| 3. Water locking | Water itself becomes structure | High | Depends on sustained magic |

**Design rule (suggested canon):** the more water in the finished product, the more the product depends on ongoing magic. Tier 1 and Tier 2 materials survive a dispelling because the chemistry is finished. Tier 3 materials can revert to slush or puddles if the magic is stripped. This gives a natural limit and a story hook.

### 3.1 Tier 1: Binder Plus Aggregate

The binder replaces cement, lime, bitumen and glue. Water acts only as a carrier and partly evaporates during curing. This tier is the most defensible and covers most bulk construction.

### 3.2 Tier 2: Harvesting Dissolved Minerals

Real seawater figures (approximate):

| Component | Grams per liter |
|---|---|
| Total dissolved salts | about 35 |
| Magnesium | about 1.29 |
| Calcium | about 0.41 |
| Dissolved inorganic carbon (as bicarbonate and carbonate) | small; limits calcium carbonate to roughly 0.2 g/L unless carbon dioxide is pulled from air |

Converting the magnesium to brucite (magnesium hydroxide) yields roughly 3 g per liter with no carbon required. Calcium carbonate yield is limited by carbon supply. Realistic total: around 3-4 g of mineral per liter, or roughly 250-350 cubic meters of seawater flowing past the work for every tonne of mineral. That is slow but continuous. Tier 2 suits structures grown in place over weeks or months: seawalls, breakwaters, piers, harbor foundations, reef barriers.

Better feedstocks than open seawater: salt lakes, evaporite basins, mineral hot springs and mine brines. These can carry ten to a hundred times the dissolved load.

### 3.3 Tier 3: Water as Structure

Real materials that are mostly water yet act solid:

| Material | Water content (real) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ettringite (a cement mineral) | about 46% by mass | Contributes to early concrete strength |
| Epsom salt | about 51% | Water built into the crystal lattice |
| Gypsum | about 21% | Plaster sets by binding water |
| Cartilage | 70-80% | Carries body loads for decades |
| Double-network hydrogel (Gong et al., 2003) | about 90% | Compressive fracture stress in the tens of MPa |
| Pykrete (ice plus about 14% wood pulp) | about 86% | Several times stronger than plain ice and melts slower |

Real proteins hold roughly 0.3-0.4 g of "bound water" per gram of protein. This shell of water does not freeze and does not behave like liquid. The magical extension: CR binder extends that ordered shell from under half a gram to 20-100 g of water per gram of binder. The result is a structured hydrate that cannot evaporate or flow.

Superabsorbent polymers in diapers absorb 300-1,000 times their weight in water, which shows the volume side is plausible. The magic is in making the water rigid and unable to escape.

## 4. Magical Mechanisms with Real Anchors

### 4.1 Templated Nucleation (Catalytic Seeding)

**Real basis.** Biomineralization proteins rich in acidic groups (aspartate, glutamate, phosphoserine) grab calcium ions and lower the energy barrier for crystals to form. They choose which mineral forms (calcite versus aragonite) and which way crystals grow. A supersaturated sodium acetate hand warmer crystallizes completely the instant a tiny seed appears. The seed's mass is trivial compared to the mass that crystallizes.

**Magical extension.** Each binder molecule is a perfect seed that never gets buried or used up. Nucleation continues as a chain reaction, like a hand warmer that keeps crystallizing as long as feedstock remains.

### 4.2 Prion-Style Self-Propagation

**Real basis.** Prions and amyloids template their own shape. One misfolded protein converts normal copies into the same shape. Lab amplification methods turn trace seeds into large quantities. Amyloid fibrils are among the stiffest protein structures known, with stiffness in the GPa range.

**Magical extension.** CR binder converts mana-loaded precursor in the mixing water into more of itself. One gram of seed propagates into kilograms of scaffold. Propagation stops when precursor runs out or when a stop signal arrives (drying, a specific mineral, a mana pulse).

**Story hooks.** Contamination becomes a danger. Spilled active binder in a reservoir could gel an entire cistern. Mixing tools must be cleaned or burned. Weaponized propagation could seize up a city's water supply.

**Suggested canon:** this mechanism sets the **amplification factor**, the single most important dial in the system. Plain binder works at Tier 1 percentages. Amplified binder cuts the required amount by 10 to 1,000 times. Scaling math: `CRBinderInfrastructure.md`.

### 4.3 Mana as Reaction Energy

**Real basis 1: mineral accretion (Biorock).** Wolf Hilbertz showed in the 1970s that low-voltage direct current through a steel frame in seawater raises the pH at the frame. Calcium carbonate and magnesium hydroxide deposit on it, growing a limestone-like layer at a few centimeters per year. Reported strengths fall in the range of ordinary concrete.

**Real basis 2: microbial cement (MICP).** Bacteria such as *Sporosarcina pasteurii* use the enzyme urease to break down urea. The reaction raises pH and releases carbonate, which precipitates calcium carbonate between sand grains. Loose sand becomes sandstone in days. Commercial bricks have been grown this way at room temperature (bioMASON). Self-healing concrete (Jonkers, Delft) embeds bacterial spores that seal cracks when water enters.

**Real basis 3: the kiln problem.** Portland cement requires kilns near 1,450°C. Lime requires around 900°C. Glass requires well over 1,000°C. Fired brick requires 900-1,100°C. Cement production alone accounts for roughly 8% of global human carbon dioxide emissions.

**Magical extension.** Mana stored in the secretion replaces electric current, bacterial metabolism and kiln heat. The binder acts as enzyme, electrode and furnace at once. Cold synthesis of stone, glass and ceramic is the core economic disruption.

### 4.4 Water Locking

Covered in Section 3.3. Summary: an extended non-freezing hydration shell turns water into a rigid structural phase. Only Tier 3 relies on it.

### 4.5 Crosslink Cascades

Real chemistries that set proteins into solids:

| Chemistry | Real occurrence | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Dityrosine bonds | Resilin; photo-oxidation | Elastic network |
| Lysyl oxidase aldehyde links | Collagen in tendon and bone | Stiffness, insolubility |
| Quinone tanning (sclerotization) | Insect cuticle after molting | Soft pale cuticle turns hard and brown within hours |
| DOPA-iron coordination | Mussel byssal threads | Reversible bonds that break under load and reform; sticks underwater |
| Transglutaminase | "Meat glue" in food processing | Glues proteins together |
| Genipin | Gardenia fruit extract | Crosslinks collagen, turns it blue |
| Plant tannins | Vegetable-tanned leather | Rot resistance, toughness |
| Chromium salts | Chrome-tanned leather | Heat and rot resistance |
| Riboflavin plus UV light | Corneal crosslinking eye surgery | Stiffens collagen in minutes |
| Glycation (Maillard reaction) | Browning in food; aging tissue | Stiffening with sugars |

**Magical extension.** The secretion carries dormant crosslinkers that activate on a trigger: sunlight, drying, calcium contact, heat or a mana pulse. Each trigger implies a different worksite technique (`CRBinderMaterials.md`).

### 4.6 Recommended Canonical Explanation

> The bug's secretion is a seeding matrix. Its collagen domains template mineral and crystal growth in whatever bulk material is present. Its resilin domains form an elastic net that locks together under sunlight and drying. Mana stored in the secretion pays the energy cost that kilns, bacteria and electric current pay in the mundane world. In concentrated form it can also order water itself into a structured hydrate.

### 4.7 Cascade Amplification (Clotting Model)

**Real basis.** Blood clotting is an amplification cascade. A small trigger activates a few enzyme molecules. Each activates many molecules of the next enzyme, which each activate many more. A handful of trigger molecules ends in vast numbers of fibrin strands within minutes.

**Magical extension.** CR binder is the final substrate in a cascade. A trace trigger secretion (or a mana pulse) starts a chain that converts dissolved binder into solid in seconds throughout the whole volume. This explains instant setting across large pours, which drying alone never could.

### 4.8 Extreme Dilution (Hagfish Model)

**Real basis.** Hagfish slime is about 99.996% water. A few grams of secretion organizes liters of seawater as coiled threads unspool and mucus vesicles burst and swell. Ratio: roughly 1 part solids to 25,000 parts water.

**Magical extension.** A dedicated thread secretion lets tiny amounts command enormous water volumes for gels, barriers and Tier 3 hydrate stone. One kilogram at hagfish dilution organizes about 25 tonnes of water.

### 4.9 Spinning from Water (Silk Model)

**Real basis.** Spiders turn a water-based protein solution into fiber stronger than steel by weight at room temperature using only ion exchange, a pH drop, water removal and stretching (`CRBinderScience.md`).

**Magical extension.** Every secretion in the family can be drawn into fiber, cast into film or set into bulk depending on handling. Drawing aligns molecules into fiber. Pouring lets them set randomly into solids with the same properties in every direction.

### 4.10 Updated Canonical Explanation

> The bug produces a family of secretions from specialized glands, like an orb-weaving spider. The collagen-resilin binder seeds mineral growth and forms an elastic net. Silk dopes draw into fibers stronger than steel by weight. Cements set underwater and anchor to stone. A trigger secretion starts a clotting-style cascade so whole pours set at once. Thread secretions organize thousands of times their weight in water. Mana stored in each secretion pays the energy cost that kilns, mills and furnaces pay in the mundane world.
