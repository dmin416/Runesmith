# Wood Processing

Natural seasoning and vacuum / heat magic shortcuts. Structural steel contrast: `../Metallurgy/StructuralForge.md`. Vacuum craft: Old `../Metallurgy/Vacuum.md` until pulled.

## Narrative

Green wood is usable when you plan for shrink. Furniture-dry stock normally costs years. Even heat plus vacuum collapses that to days and unlocks crack-prone species, at the cost of a mastery curve when pressure or heat is botched.

## Detail

### Natural chain

1. Fell (late fall/winter preferred for pests/sugar, not faster dry)  
2. Buck and debark soon (bark traps moisture and beetles)  
3. Rive with the grain or saw boards  
4. Seal ends (ends dry **10–15×** faster than faces → checking)  
5. Air dry: sticker stack, cover top ≈ **1 year per inch** → ~12–20% MC  
6. Indoor / heated shed → **6–10%** for furniture  

Defects: check, warp, twist, cup, case-hardening (dry shell over wet core).

### Magical / vacuum kiln path

| Trick | What it does |
|---|---|
| Vacuum dry | Water boils ~40–60 °C. Inch hardwood green→furniture in **days** |
| Volume heat | Even core heat kills moisture gradients that crack boards |
| Condition | Brief humid heat if a dry shell forms, then resume |
| Sterilize | Core **56 °C / 30 min** kills insects/fungi (pallet standard) |
| Freeze-dry | Ice sublimes under vacuum → near-zero shrink (shipwreck method) |
| Thermal mod | 180–230 °C in vacuum (no O₂ burn): darker, rot-proof, weaker/brittle |
| Vacuum impregnate | Pull air → flood oil/resin/reagent → release → deep soak |
| Steam bend | Soften lignin ~100 °C moist, bend, lock dry in hours |
| Vacuum charcoal | 400–600 °C no air → ~75% C at 400, ~90% at 600. Clean smith charcoal |

### Setting effects

- Seasoned lumber without year stockpiles  
- Crack-prone rare woods become workable  
- Shipwrights, coopers, bowyers, carpenters and smiths pay for the mage  
- Overheat, uneven vacuum or fast repressurization still ruins batches
