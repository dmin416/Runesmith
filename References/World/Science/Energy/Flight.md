# Flight

Hover and lift power. No locked “flight is cheap/expensive” fiat. Pay the equation for the method in use. Cast: `../../../Runes/Energy.md`. Hands hold ≠ flight. Felt speed / optic flow at altitude: `../Body/SpeedPerception.md`. Hub: `../Science.md`.

## Narrative

Holding a mass still with Hands is grip against gravity. Staying up in air dumps momentum into the air (or uses another model). Pick one equation. Do not mix silently.

## Detail

### Static hold ≠ flight

Mana Hands hold rows (e.g. 80 kg → tens of J/s grip) are restraint. They are **not** hover cost in free air.

### Actuator-disk hover

```
P_ideal = (m g)^1.5 / √(2 ρ A)
P_real ≈ P_ideal / FM          // FM ≈ 0.6–0.8
Mana/s = P / (10 × η × μ)
```

ρ ≈ 1.2 kg/m³. Larger disk area A cuts power (`1/√A`).

Example **80 kg**, A = 1 m²: P_ideal ≈ **14 kW**; realistic ≈ **18–24 kW**.

| Tier | J/mana | Mana/s @ 14 kW | @ ~21 kW |
|---|---|---|---|
| INT 40 L2 (~22) | ~22 | ~650 | ~960 |
| INT 500 L9 (~500) | ~500 | ~29 | ~42 |

### Vacuum-pocket assist

```
F = ΔP × A
Establish ≈ ΔP × V
```

80 kg on 1 m² needs ΔP ≈ **785 Pa**. V = 0.1 m³ → ~**79 J** to establish. Ongoing = leak maintenance, **if** sealed. Living-target vacuum rules still apply when the pocket is on a resisting person. Self-pocket on cooperating caster is a different contest.

### Method checklist

| Method | Pay |
|---|---|
| Actuator-disk | Continuous P_ideal / P_real |
| Vacuum pocket | ΔP×V + leak |
| Jet / thrust | KE / F·v (Kinetic when absorbed) |
| Reactionless / ground-push | Separate equation; never mix with disk |
