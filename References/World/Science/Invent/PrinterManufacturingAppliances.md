### 6.7 Refrigerator

| Part | Material | Method | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compressor shell | Low-carbon steel | Deep draw two halves | Welded shut after assembly: a hermetic compressor is never opened |
| Compressor internals | Cast iron crankcase; steel crank; aluminum or iron piston; spring steel reed valves | Cast, machine, grind | Reed valves lapped flat |
| Compressor motor | Induction motor: silicon steel laminations, copper windings | Stack and wind | Windings sit in refrigerant and oil, so insulation must resist both |
| Hermetic terminal | Glass-to-metal seal with steel pins | Fuse glass around pins in steel cup | |
| Condenser | Steel or copper tube with welded steel wire fins | Bend tube; resistance weld wires | |
| Evaporator | Aluminum roll-bond (two sheets with printed pattern of anti-bond ink, rolled together, then inflated with pressure to form channels) or copper tube with aluminum fins | | |
| Capillary tube | Small bore copper | Drawn | Length and bore set the pressure drop |
| Filter drier | Steel or copper shell with molecular sieve desiccant | | |
| Line joints | Copper | Braze with silver or phosphorus-copper alloy while flowing nitrogen through the tube to prevent internal scale | |
| Evacuation and charge | | Vacuum pump down to below about 500 microns, confirm it holds, charge with exact weight of refrigerant | Moisture or air left inside ruins the system |
| Cabinet | Steel sheet outer; ABS or HIPS liner | Stamp outer; thermoform liner | Inject polyurethane foam between them (chemistry) |
| Door gasket | Flexible PVC extrusion with magnetic strip inside | | |
| Thermostat | Capillary bulb or bimetal | | |

**Alternatives with fewer hard parts:**
- **Absorption refrigerator:** Ammonia, water and hydrogen in a sealed welded steel circuit, powered by a flame or heater. No compressor and no moving parts. Easier mechanically; the chemistry is the hard part.
- **Icebox:** Insulated cabinet plus ice. The printer prints the cabinet.

### 6.10 Cookware and knives

| Item | Material | Method | Heat treatment | Finish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cast iron skillet | Gray iron | Sand cast or print | None | Machine or grind cooking surface smooth; season with polymerized oil baked at 230-260°C, repeat several coats |
| Carbon steel pan | Low-carbon steel sheet | Spin form or press | | Season as cast iron |
