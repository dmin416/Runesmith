# Paper and Hide Writing Surfaces

Mundane and magical sheets for notes, contracts, schematics and scrolls. Companion: `WritingTools.md`. Physical sizes, stack weights, pipe scaling and channeling heat: `PaperFormats.md`.

**Lock scope:** firm against early rewrite pegs and **Ch 19** (written). Shop numbers from Source Ch 20+ (old **9 LC** blanks, Mana Arrow **1 SS**, etc.) are **not** locked. Retune those when those chapters are rewritten.

Coin pegs still firm: `../../Society/Economy.md` (**5 LC = 1d**; goblin ear **5 LC**; meal **5 LC**; unskilled day **5–10 LC**).

## Four stocks

| | **Paper** | **Hide** |
|---|---|---|
| **Mundane** | Rag / plant pulp. Ordinary ink. | Sheep, goat, calf, kid, lamb parchment and vellum. |
| **Magical** | Mana-bearing plant fiber (magic wood and lesser mana pulps). | Monster / magical-beast skins finished to keep mana. |

**Price weight:** feedstock supply first. Paper quality is a lighter consideration than infinite beast byproduct vs constrained plant / flock supply.

**Economy type (locked):** **craftsman** market, not industrial (`../../Tech/Technology.md`: mills exist; no printing press; no mass manufacturing). Wonky next to modern bulk paper is fine. Workshops and skills, not factories.

|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Lowest magical hide** (rough monster-skin blank) | **2–6** | **~4–5** | Infinite dungeon / wild beasts. Hide is byproduct. Finish labor only. About **one goblin ear** or less. |
| **Crappy mundane paper** | **5–12** | **~8** | Small pulp shops. **Above** the cheapest magical blanks. |

### Labor reading (why these numbers are safe to proceed)

Shop price ≠ one craftsman’s full day per sheet.

- **Rough magical blank (~4–5 LC):** Retail sits near **1 unskilled day** or **1 ear**, but a tannery turns **one pelt (20–40 LC)** into **several** A5 blanks. Embedded labor is a fraction of a day per blank; the rest is shop cut, city rent, and finish. That matches infinite byproduct + craftsman markup, not “one day to make one scrap.”
- **Crappy paper (~8 LC):** Above the cheapest blanks on purpose. Small pulp shops are slow and capital-poor. About **1 meal to 1 unskilled day** per sheet is harsh vs Earth mill paper and correct for non-industrial Caldris.
- **Fine paper (~20 LC) / magical paper (~35 LC):** About **1 skilled day** to a bit over **1 master day** at retail. Specialist fiber and sizing; fair for mage/scribe goods.
- **Mundane rough parchment (~45 LC):** About **1–2 master days** at retail, which fits **days of real skin work** and finite flocks (historically labor-heavy).
- **Finest bands (120–250+):** Multi-day / multi-week master work and reject waste. Prestige, not bulk.

**Proceed with these bands.** The only soft feel is rough blank ≈ ear/meal; that is intentional city retail, not a claim that finishing one blank takes a full day of labor. If street stalls ever need to feel dirt-cheap, use the **2–3 LC** bottom of the band without changing the rest of the ladder.

## Why the four markets diverge

### Magical hide (supply king)

- Beasts refill. Infinite byproduct into craftsman tanneries.
- Mana already in the tissue.
- Lowest blanks undercut paper.

### Mundane hide (finite flocks)

- Farm animals, not dungeon trash.
- Labor and yield dominate. Stays expensive beside cheap magical blanks.

### Mundane paper (craftsman pulp)

- Small vat/press shops. No factory curve.
- No free carcass stream → usually costs more than lowest magical hide.
- Wins on habit, lightness, non-magical bureaucracy.

### Magical paper (scarce mana fiber)

- Limited mana timber; specialist processing.
- Premium plant blank for spellbooks and buyers who want fiber over skin.
