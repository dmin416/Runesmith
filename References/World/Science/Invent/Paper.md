## Four stocks

| | **Paper** | **Hide** |
|---|---|---|
| **Mundane** | Rag / plant pulp. Ordinary ink. | Sheep, goat, calf, kid, lamb parchment and vellum. |
| **Magical** | Mana-bearing plant fiber (magic wood and lesser mana pulps). | Monster / magical-beast skins finished to keep mana. |

**Price weight:** feedstock supply first. Paper quality is a lighter consideration than infinite beast byproduct vs constrained plant / flock supply.

**Economy type (locked):** **craftsman** market, not industrial (`../../Tech/Technology.md`: mills exist; no printing press; no mass manufacturing). Wonky next to modern bulk paper is fine. Workshops and skills, not factories.

## Why the four markets diverge

### Magical hide (supply king)

- Beasts refill. Infinite byproduct into craftsman tanneries.
- Mana already in the tissue.
- Lowest blanks undercut paper.

### Mundane hide (finite flocks)

- Farm animals, not dungeon trash.
- Labor and yield dominate. Stays expensive beside cheap magical blanks.

### Mundane paper (craftsman pulp)

- Small vat/press shops. No factory curve.
- No free carcass stream → usually costs more than lowest magical hide.
- Wins on habit, lightness, non-magical bureaucracy.

### Magical paper (scarce mana fiber)

- Limited mana timber; specialist processing.
- Premium plant blank for spellbooks and buyers who want fiber over skin.

## Earth historical anchors

Convert with **5 LC = 1d**. Order-of-magnitude only.

| When / where | Item | Historical price | Rough LC read |
|---|---|---|---|
| European mill era from **13th c.** | Paper spreads as cheap challenger to skin | - | - |
| England **late 1300s** | Paper ~**6–10d**/quire | ~**0.25–0.4d**/sheet if ~25/quire → **~1–2 LC** raw | Caldris crappy paper sits higher (craftsman scarcity) |
| England **1395** | Parchment ~**3.8d**/skin | **~19 LC**/skin before cutting into pages | |
| Late medieval summary | Paper often ~**6×** cheaper than parchment | Mundane paper << mundane hide | |
| Manuscripts | Labor often **~70–80%+** of book cost | Materials are not the whole price | |

**Caldris twist:** magical beast hide plays Earth’s “cheap bulk surface” role for magic work; mana paper is specialist plant stock; sheep parchment stays finite-livestock prestige.

## Grade table (finish quality)

Within a stock. Global shop order follows the LC table.

| | Crappy paper | Fine paper | Rough hide | Finest hide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finish relative (mundane flock logic) | **1x** | **3–5x** | **10–20x** | **50–150x** |
| Magical rough hide | - | - | **Below** crappy paper | - |
| Raw input | Rag / coarse pulp | Clean fiber, sizing | Sheep/goat; or common monster skin | Select calf/kid/lamb; or rare beast / master finish |
| Labor | Minutes in shop batches | + sizing / calendering | Days per skin | Weeks per skin |
| Best use | Notes, receipts | Books, contracts; magical spellbooks | Working / field; magical runic blanks | Decrees; heirloom scrolls |

## What goes on which stock

| Job | Usual stock |
|---|---|
| Notes, schematic practice (Ch 19) | Mundane paper |
| Ordinary contracts | Mundane paper; lasting copies on mundane parchment |
| Early runic blanks | Lowest **magical hide** (shop monster skin, or later Ned gel-nodule skins when strong enough: `../../../People/Ned.md`) |
| Magical ink | Shop reagents, or later Ned gel-nodule fluid |
| Spellbooks / many regular scrolls | Magical paper and/or magical hide |
| Heirloom runic / royal magical docs | Finest magical hide |
