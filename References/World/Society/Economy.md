## Narrative

Caldris uses a copper, silver, and gold coin ladder. Each step is ten times the one below. Royal gold is a larger rectangular piece at the top. Early prices and wages peg to that ladder. Full price books: `EconomyDesign.md`.

## Detail

**Calendar (locked):** **1 month = 30 days**; **1 year = 12 months + 1 New Year's Day = 361 days**. Monthly lodging quotes = **30** nights. Cosmology: `../World.md`.

**Peg:** 5 large copper (LC) = 1 historical penny (d)

**Pricing method (locked feel):** Compare goods in **days of unskilled labor**. Unskilled day band **5–10 LC** (use **~7.5 LC** midpoint when converting). Caldris mixes eras by production method (farm ≈ labor-stable; steam iron/steel cheaper than medieval; handwritten books/scrolls stay dear; Needle Worm silk is cheap; rail is Victorian-cheap on lines; personal power / spatial gear stays destrier-tier). Full rule + tables: `EconomyDesign.md`.

### Currency ladder

Three metals. Small and large of each. ×10 per step. Royal gold is larger and rectangular.

| Coin | Abbr | In LC |
|---|---|---|
| Small copper | SC | 0.1 |
| Large copper | LC | 1 |
| Small silver | SS | 10 |
| Large silver | LS | 100 |
| Small gold | SG | 1,000 |
| Large gold | LG | 10,000 |
| Royal gold | RG | 100,000 |

- 1 LS = 100 LC

### Purchasing power (unskilled 5–10 LC/day)

| Coin | In LC | Labor equivalent | Street feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 LC | 1 | a fraction of a day | a snack |
| 1 SS | 10 | 1–2 days | inn night, two meals |
| 1 LS | 100 | 10–20 days | leader stone; registration is **2 LS** |
| 1 SG | 1,000 | 100–200 days (~half a year) | starting pouch scale; class crystal is **2 SG** |

Each tier jumps roughly “a day → two weeks → half a year.” Test any price against that.

### Potions

Behavior: `../../Items/Items.md`. Graded Lowest → Highest. Healing and recovery potions **close flesh** when drunk or poured. Heat runs through torn muscle and staunches bleed. The brew can hurt worse than the wound. This is body mending in the story, not a game meter.

| Grade / item | Effect / price feel |
|---|---|
| Lowest / low-grade healing | Seals holes; leaves residual soreness. Not equal to a strong priest mend. About **2–5 SS** |
| Hangover potion | Separate item from healing. About **8–15 LC** |
| Higher grades | Close more; leave less ache. Prices later |

Dungeon-gate stalls mark potions up about **25% or more** over normal market.

**Draft potion ladder (open; not locked):** Low **2–5 SS** (above) → Mid **1–2 LS** → High **5–10 LS** → Greater **1–3 SG**. Follows the ×10 coin feel when the ladder is opened.
