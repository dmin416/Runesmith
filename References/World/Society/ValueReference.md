# Value Reference

> **Design note.** History vs research vs Recommended. **Recommended** is a promotion queue into Design, not a lock. Promote chosen rows into `PriceCatalog.md` / `EconomyDesign.md` one at a time. Existing locks win on conflict. Bare "gold" without small/large reads as **SG** (Lock).
>
> Lean lock: `Economy.md`. Item catalog: `PriceCatalog.md`. Story chronology: `../../../Story/Notes/StoryPrices.md`.

**Conversion peg:** 5 LC = 1d. So 1s (12d) = 60 LC and £1 (240d) = 1,200 LC.

Coins: 1 SS = 10 LC. 1 LS = 100 LC. 1 SG = 1,000 LC. 1 LG = 10,000 LC. 1 RG = 100,000 LC.

History is English unless noted, mostly 14th century.
