# Races

Fat race notes: `RacesDesign.md`.

## Narrative

Humans dominate Caldris. Many other peoples live and work across Terra: dwarves, elves, goliaths, gnomes, beast races, dragon-kin, halflings, orc-blooded people, and mixed bloodlines. Wild monsters are separate from peoples.

## Detail

**Caldris majority:** Human. Peoples mix in cities without a strong racial-prejudice default.

**Also present (from Old loot, kept):**

| People | Notes |
|---|---|
| Dwarf | Craft focus. Underground forge tradition. Strong runesmith / golem reputation. Half-dwarves exist. |
| Elf | Long-lived. Sun elves and Moon elves are political / faith rivals. Half-elves and other mixes exist. |
| Goliath | Large mountain peoples. Warrior reputation. Sahildr. |
| Dragon-kin (Dracs) | Lizard-like, dragon-blood talk. Not mindless lizard monsters. |
| Beast race | Animal-tribe umbrella. Pure vs lesser (ears/tail). Feline, ursine, wolven, and more. Reyna is wolven. |
| Gnome | Small, dexterous. Trains, fine machinery, alchemy. Half-gnomes (Helci). |
| Halfling | About half human height. |
| Orc bloodlines | Civilized half-orcs and thinner mixes. Wild orcs are monsters. |
