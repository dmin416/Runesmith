# Family

House Arden and close household. Ages below are from early chapters unless noted.

## House Arden

Knight house of the Kingdom of Caldris. Baron Wentworth Arden is head. The house wants a warrior reputation and does not want to be linked to crafting professions in public.

Roland is the 4th son and an illegitimate child. His birth mother was a commoner who does not live at the estate. He starts in a side-wing room and is mostly ignored until Mana Sense raises his standing.

### Wentworth Arden
Baron. Military official. Large build, mustache, scar from left eyebrow to lip. Looks about early forties but is near mid-sixties. Wants sons trained for combat and army service. Approves Roland's Runesmith plan only if Roland earns his keep and keeps the Arden name off crafting work. Sends Roland to a cabin near Carwen with no further money and army enlistment as the fallback. Chapter 7 aside: rates Roland’s monster performance satisfactory and clearer-headed than the other sons (who needed rescue on first fights); expects the cabin hardship to push him back into the army. Chapter 67: called Silver Wolf; silver armor; border ceasefire with Hatfordian Empire ends his posting; intel on Roland points to Edelgard (wrong; Roland already left). Carries white-silver locket: dark-haired woman holding a baby whose face is scratched out.

### Tabitha Arden
First wife / main wife. Mother of Reyner, Edwin and Sophia.

### Francine Arden
Second wife / mistress living in the house. Mother of Robert, Dianna and later Lucienne. Dislikes Lucienne spending time with Roland.

### Roland's birth mother
Unnamed commoner. Not present at the mansion. Roland has no clear memory of her face.

## Household

### Martha
Roland's personal maid. Older woman. Only person consistently kind to the original Roland. Tends sickness, food and packing (sandwiches for the hedge hideout). Wants to go with him when he leaves. Not allowed. Asks him to write if he needs help. He hopes to hire her later if he has money.

### Adam
House butler. Gray hair, black tux. Handles tests, tutors, ascension crystal delivery and Roland's departure arrangements. Confirms to Wentworth that Roland accepted Carwen and knows the destination.

### Drill instructor
Unnamed. Large bald scarred soldier under the Baron. Runs physical training and oversees the goblin bravery test when Wentworth is busy. Whacks trainees with a stick when they slack.

## Early plot notes

- Chapter 3: dinner reveal of Mana Sense leads to magic testing and books.
- Chapter 4: aptitude orb (not full status read). No magic tutor. Basic combat skills, Climbing thresholds, forge ban. Age-9 bravery kill (50 + 200 First Kill XP). Ascension book lore.
- Chapters 4 to 5: training, ascension, Mage class, 0% elemental affinities. Ritual crystal delivered by Adam while Wentworth is away and the older sons are at the knight academy.
- Chapter 6: Mage L3; Blessed by Mana; Runesmith plan. Wentworth denies Lustile, orders earn-keep mage work via Adam, and forbids linking the Arden knight name to crafting in public.
- Chapter 7: Last estate goblin clears; Lucienne secret goodbye; calligraphy books packed; mana train to Carwen; cabin letter (no money; army fallback). Wentworth expects failure into enlistment.
- Chapter 8: registers as bronze adventurer under first name only to hide the Arden name. Guild keeps full credentials. Stole and buried a class-up crystal before leaving the cabin.
- Chapter 10: clumsy Arden watcher tails him in Carwen town only (not into the forest; once found drunk). Roland plans to leave for Edelgard.
- Chapter 13: after ~half a year Roland digs up the buried class-change crystal; the watcher ambushes him in the west woods (throwing knives, heavy rapier, ~183 cm). Calls him bastard son and implies the Baron.
- Chapter 14: fight continues. Watcher (chestnut hair, bushy beard, bloodshot) admits **surveillance / reporting only**, not protection; expected Roland home in a week; wants knighthood. Plans kill + blame beasts, then wipe the party. Party kills him (Gale Step, detonation rapier tip; Sahildr impact hammer finish; +479 XP). Roland starts confessing to the girls. Note: commoners who kill nobles hang even if the noble was guilty.
- Chapter 15: partial confession (bastard earning keep; watcher snapped; alternate-hire theory private). Girls found him via Hilde + Reyna's watcher flag + Sahildr. Body dumped floor 3; Roland keeps rapier; first [Highest] Detonation schematic + Runic Scholar.
- Chapter 17: suspects Robert or the wives more than Wentworth; goes dark (no letters). Explains zero affinities and craft path to the party; quits alone.
- Chapter 19: Wentworth learns Roland and the watcher are gone (party left Carwen too). Weekly reports had stopped. Fumes over escape / foul play / death. Adam sent for more searchers. Guild won't reveal adventurer locations to a Baron (Ducal/King pull only).

## House Dreux (Edelgard)

Count's house ruling Edelgard. Chapter 54: Louis Dreux (second son) hired Abyssal Cult to kill merchant council while heir Armand Dreux is at the Hatfordian border. Aimed at buying/taking businesses for succession leverage. No hard proof. Chapter 67: Armand returns from border with ceasefire; hosts Silver Wolf (Wentworth); not yet named heir; compliments on battlefield performance.
